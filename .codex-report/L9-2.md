## NEEDS_RULING

### 1. Step 9b calibration refit under the 120 s wall deadline (ED-RULING item 6)

Evidence (executed, synthetic only):
- The deadline (`_ProjectionWorkBudget`, `powermetrics_fiducial.py:527-552`) starts when `detect_pulses`
  starts and is checked at each counted cell, but the time it bounds is 99% coordinate-descent fitting, which
  is uncounted, fixed-size work (4 coarse + 4 fine grid sweeps per pulse, `powermetrics_fiducial.py:808-829`):
  9.735 s of 9.857 s per synthetic detection on this VM.
- Total work per detection is already deterministically finite without the deadline: fixed grids per pulse x
  59 pulses, plus at most `DETECTION_PROJECTION_CELL_BUDGET` cells. The deadline adds no reproducible bound.
  It only converts a slow host into `detection_nonconvergent`.
- The refused synthetic run fired at ~5.6x the isolated fit cost on its VM. A failed refit returns
  `instrument_calibration_invalid` and is not cached (`reduce.py:1526-1594`, only successes enter
  `physics_cache`), so each replay re-runs the refit and its outcome depends on the host at that moment.
- The code itself quarantines the deadline's trigger and count as non-reproducible
  (`detection_projection.diagnostics.reproducible`).

Options, with my reading:
- **(a) Replay keeps the refit and the cell budget, without the wall deadline.** Outcome becomes a function of
  bytes only; work stays bounded by the fixed grids plus the cell budget. Requires a production change outside
  WRITE_SCOPE (`rederive_detection_from_artifacts` has no budget arguments; `reduce.py` calls it at 1547), so
  it is not mine to make. **Recommended**: it is the smallest change that makes 9b reproducible and keeps the
  physics check.
- **(b) Hash-bind the harvest-recorded fit; arithmetic left to the independent recompute.** Removes the
  deadline exposure and the repeated per-member refits (the largest measured runtime cost), but the replay
  loses its own physics re-check. Also production scope.
- **(c) Keep as is.** It fails closed: a deadline firing in the release but not in the dry run would surface as
  `release_digest_differs_from_dry_run`, not as a wrong number. It cannot be shown reproducible: whether
  it fires depends on host load, which is what refused 9b in the L9-1 runs. Margin on the Mac with real data is
  unmeasured (real data may not be opened here).
