### Isolated per-cell cost of the projection (executed)

`.codex-report/l9-2/projection_bench.py` builds the synthetic 59-pulse v3 calibration from
`tests/b5_analysis/support_s10/calibration.py` and times the unmodified
`rederive_detection_from_artifacts` on it, on this idle VM:

| mode | cells | wall s | CPU s | ms/cell | disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plain | 5,839 | 9.822 | 9.708 | 1.682 | converged |
| `sys.monitoring` PY_UNWIND global only | 5,839 | 9.904 | 9.733 | 1.696 | converged |
| PY_RETURN local events only | 5,839 | 9.845 | 9.761 | 1.686 | converged |
| joined-harness observer layout (both) | 5,839 | 9.703 | 9.542 | 1.662 | converged |

A converging synthetic projection needs 5,839 cells. The wall deadline is measured from the start of
`detect_pulses`, so "ms/cell" above is total detection time divided by cells, not the cost of a cell.
`.codex-report/l9-2/projection_split.py` times `_accepted_region_projection` separately (diagnostic process
only):

| total s | pulses | coordinate-descent fits s | projection s | cells | projection ms/cell | fit s/pulse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9.857 | 59 | 9.735 | 0.122 | 5,839 | 0.021 | 0.165 |

The fits (uncounted work) take 99% of the wall time. Cells are ~100 per pulse, so the refused run's 5,204 cells
at 120 s means ~52 of 59 pulses fitted, i.e. ~2.3 s per pulse against ~0.41 s per pulse for the same reference in
isolation on that VM (24.36 s): a ~5.6x slowdown of CPU-bound pure-Python code. The joined harness's read-only
`sys.monitoring` observer has no measurable effect on that code (excluded as a cause under (d)). Real-corpus
cell counts (112,205-137,189, frozen comment at `powermetrics_fiducial.py:78-82`) would add only ~2.5 s at this
cell cost; real-data exposure to the deadline is driven by fit time per pulse, i.e. host speed and load.
