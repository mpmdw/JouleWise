## Findings

### HIGH

**HIGH-1 (c, a): the runtime admits an incomplete planning-turn member, so a deadman- or budget-cut energy enters β_T and decides U5.**
- `joulewise/align/apple/r1_runtime.py:1130-1151` (`k_planning`) returns `energy_j = read_bundle_record(...)["turn_owned_j"]` with no check of `member.status`, `ep.flags` or `token_budget.incomplete`. Compare `k_step` (`:1032`), which at least refuses `status != "succeeded"`.
- The contract (`r1_runtime_contract.py:42`; `docs/align/r1_runtime_contract.md:69`) asks the planning kinds for `reference_c`, `g0_release_c`, `delta_slow_c`, `energy_j` only, and the driver's `planning_temperature_summary` (`scripts/align_r1_rehearsal.py:292-345`) reads exactly those; `u5_verdict` (`:370-393`) then judges ruling 6 / L2 on them. Probe A shows the path end to end.
- This is likely, not theoretical: the designed start states preheat **inside** the G0 wait (`r1_runtime.py:920-946`, `PREHEAT_CAP_S = 900` at `:68`) after the gate deadman is already armed (`workload.py:429-434`, armed at G0 entry per CHAIR ruling 1). Preheat (≤ 900 s) + G0 (≤ 600 s) can consume up to 1,500 s of the step's 1,580 s `deadman_s` (`configs/align/v4/r1_runtime.json:15`), leaving less than a 4,096-token decode (~370 s, FD:145). The member then ends with `episode.deadman:0`, `status` is still reported, and the energy is that of a partial turn.
- Consequence: a biased β_T, a wrong U5 verdict (R1-blocking item, ruling 6), and the kit admits it (`check_r1_evidence` only checks `u5.verdict ∈ {pass, fail, inconclusive}` and `c == 1.0`, `scripts/align_v4_kit.py:195-197`).
- The same gap, less likely, covers `step-d`, `step-30b` and the A-B-A legs: `k_step` (`:1030-1047`) refuses only a non-`succeeded` status; a deadman-cut step keeps `succeeded` with `episode.deadman:0`, and `derive_step_d_slots` (`:474-531`) would then freeze S1-S4/S7 from a truncated step.
- Recommendation: refuse (exit 3) any model member whose episode carries `episode.deadman:`, `episode.decode_budget:`, `episode.g0_unreleased:`, `thermal.start_gate_timeout`, `thermal.gate_timeout:`/`hold_timeout:` or `token_budget.incomplete`, for the planning, step and A-B-A kinds; add `member_complete` (bool) to those kinds in `KIND_FIELDS` and have the driver require it true. Test: a synthetic planning member cut by the deadman must refuse.

**HIGH-2 (d): the gated D runs and X-G capture their own warm reference, so the ruling 1(d) duty bar measures a G0 that W1 never runs.**
- `k_gated_d` (`r1_runtime.py:1116`) and `k_x_g` (`:1161`) call `run_member(config)` with no reference hook, and `gate_policy` (`:181-187`) installs `window_reference: {"role": "capture"}` for every R1 member. `ThermalSession.capture_reference` (`thermal_gates.py:390-407`) then runs at warmup entry (`workload.py:396`) and takes the median of the preceding 60 s as the member's R.
- In the phase plan (`align_r1_rehearsal.py:207-216`) the gated runs follow `X_G_contrast` and each other with no idle between them, so each one's R is the state right after the previous heavy member. G0 (`thermal_gates.py:512-515`, one-sided `≤ R + δ`) then only has to shed the warmup's own heat, and the episode reference for G1/H is that warm release.
- W1 is different by design: ordinal 5 captures R and ordinals 6-18 consume it (`configs/campaigns/align_v4/collection_plan.json` `runtime_requirements.window_reference`: "member 5 captures; later members consume"; FD, CHAIR ruling 18). A D member after a D predecessor must cool to that R + δ, which is the 300-600 s G0 that ruling 1 (CRITICAL) is about.
- So R1's `deadman.phases_s.G0` (`align_r1_rehearsal.py:424`, `r1_runtime.py:385-406`) and the 40 % share cannot fail on G0, and G1/H hold to a warmer target than W1's. The pre-declared duty bar (ruling 1(d)) is judged on the wrong physical case; the step tests, by contrast, do get the soak R via `reference_hook` (`:903-906`, `:965`), and ruling 1(c)'s recovery curve gives the lead a separate G0 prediction. Neither the contract doc (`r1_runtime_contract.md:111`, "Every R1 member captures its own reference"), the integration report nor protocol v4 says the duty bar is measured against a self-captured warm reference.
- Lead decision needed before R1, not a code call: either (i) hand the gated runs the soak R (`reference_hook`) and precede each with an `idle-until-soak` or a `g0-release-soak`, which makes their G0 the W1 case; or (ii) keep self-capture and write into protocol v4 and `r1_slots.duty_bar` that the G0 term is the warmup-only case and the W1 G0 is predicted from the ruling 1(c) curve. (i) costs about 3 × (≤ 1,500 s) of R1.
