## Counting and confidence

Each core track has **n=4 original seats: Fable (F), Opus (O), Sol-1 (S1), Sol-2 (S2)**. C-R/C-D/C-V embed F/O reports. Their syntheses, appendices, and the earlier agreement are not additional votes. The three reviewer personas inside a Sol reviewer2 document are one seat. Prereg drafts have n=2, both Sol; auxiliary reviews are one assignment each.

In the tables, **original k/4 → surviving k/4** counts seats that raised the *stated proposition* and whose opposite-family reviewer agrees with it. **XR** is Sol reviewing Claude; **XO** is Opus reviewing Sol. `A` means AGREE; `Aq` means agreement only with the stated qualification; `D` means DISAGREE; `U` means UNVERIFIABLE. A qualified broad issue can survive while its specific remedy is rejected in the same row. Silence is not agreement or disagreement. No reviewer is added to the denominator.

**High-convergent (HC):** original support from both families, surviving in both cross-review directions. **High-reviewed (HR):** original support in one family, explicitly endorsed by the other family's reviewer, usually with source/arithmetic corroboration. **Medium (M):** useful proposal or new reviewer finding without that complete agreement; may still be urgent. **Unresolved (U):** an empirical outcome, disputed constant, or unsupported sufficiency claim. Owner rulings are binding rather than confidence votes. These labels assess the action/objection, never predicted detector efficacy.

Evidence keys resolve to immutable refs/paths in the source register. Section/claim numbers refer to those source documents. Historical tests are attributed to their authors unless listed under this report's verification.
