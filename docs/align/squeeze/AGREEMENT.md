## How agreement is counted

For each of the three workflow tracks, **n = 4 available independent seats**: Fable (**F**), Opus (**O**), Sol seat 1 (**S1**) and Sol seat 2 (**S2**). C-R/C-D/C-V contain the F/O seat reports in appendices. Their synthesis is evidence about adjudication, **not a fifth vote**. Sol seats refer only to their matching track. A listed seat raised the issue or action; silence is not disagreement. **k/4 counts agreement on the stated issue, not necessarily on the remedy, sample size, or priority.** Conflicting remedies are stated explicitly.

Cross-family support means at least one Claude and one Sol seat independently raised the issue. It gives high confidence that the issue deserves attention, not that the proposed experiment will work. Direct source evidence can make a singleton urgent. V0 is one additional Sol review session containing three simulated reviewer perspectives, **not three seats**; AR and KR are separate tooling reviews. None is added to the core four-seat denominators. The preregistration comparison has **n = 2 Sol drafts**, not cross-family replication.

Evidence keys below resolve to pinned commits and paths in the source register. Section names identify the supporting passages; a source's reported tests remain **historical reported evidence**, unless explicitly reproduced here.

- **Replay is not an implemented universal third detector.** F proposes replay identity against fabricated logs; O/C-D correctly notes that only designated pairs are replayed and trusted logging already excludes some fabrication. Recommend an uncontaminated operator-owned reference when such subtraction is used. Freeze the allowed attack interval, raw gross integral, subtraction and uncertainty together. A fixed reference subtraction is not permission to learn a duration regressor that absorbs hidden work.
- **Composition is a hypothesis, not exact metered evidence.** C-D proposes transporting off-Mac attacks with a per-site cost factor κ and checking fully logged residuals by rank p≥.10, κ CI and prediction intervals. Non-rejection is not equivalence; additivity, contexts, new-call cost, variance and thermal changes can break transport (ER Limits; AR H2; KR M1). Recommend composed results for planning/exploration unless a prospective equivalence/transport assay and independent metered validation justify the exact claim. Actual metered final attacks remain necessary.
- **The compact power argument sizes only part of its verdict.** C-D's test against a .025 null on six monitor-missed attacks does not power its separate ≥.5 lower-bound requirement or SQ2 best-response noninferiority. Even 5/6 catches has `Pr[Binomial(6,.5)≥5]=.109375`, so its one-sided 90% lower bound is below .5. Size the full decision, not the easiest conjunct.

Do not call this an eligibility-count-only extension.
