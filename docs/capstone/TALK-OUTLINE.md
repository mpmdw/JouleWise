**Working title:** Sabotage Leaves an Energy Signature: A Pilot Study of Physical Auditing for AI Agents

**Format:** 16 slides, 23 minutes of speaking; allow two further minutes for questions if the slot permits. The title names the research question. The demonstrated result is detection of known log omissions in a non-adaptive pilot, not detection of malicious intent.

## Speaker guardrails and cuts

- On slide 3, replace “the agent cannot edit joules” with “the agent cannot rewrite an independently retained meter reading under our privilege assumptions.” It can still change what work it performs and how much energy it draws.
- On slide 6, explain that “rest” includes memory, fabric, conversion and fans; this test does not identify a DRAM component or validate the absolute gain of the inference rails. The contrast is latency-bound, so transfer to bandwidth-bound decode is unproved.
- On slide 10, “held out” refers to fresh benign seeds after design selection. The sabotage checks are reused seed-1 anchors. The fitting rule/code is frozen, with coefficients fitted per session and task pair. Do not describe this as a wholly independent attack test.
- On slide 11, explain the sample-maximum threshold without a nominal 5% or 2.5% guarantee. Shared leave-one-out fits, heavy tails and window effects need their registered treatment. A count target alone cannot establish a deployment false-alarm rate.
- A 20-minute cut removes most of slide 7's narration (45 s), shortens slide 9's statistical explanation (30 s), slide 11's exclusions detail (45 s), slide 14's gate detail (30 s) and slide 15's baseline discussion (30 s). Keep the caveat labels visible.
- Backup material: evidence ledger E1–E9, the legacy-versus-sequence comparison, and the related-work map from the paper outline. Do not add a sealed-results backup slide.
