Why it is still a pilot

On screen

8

Three layers and the squeeze

1:00 · at 7:20

Monitors see content, the meter sees quantity, probes see internals. The squeeze is a hypothesis: hiding from one should expose you to another.

On screen
Monitorreads content
Metermeasures quantity
Probesread internals

Escape routes that might beat more than one layer:

  • logged, plausible cover work;
  • filler or encoded reasoning;
  • padding to match the expected energy;
  • latent reasoning at fixed compute. The meter is blind here: the weight-only physics effect is estimated at ≤ 3×10⁻⁵ of energy, a design estimate, not a measurement.
Say

"Hypothesis, untested." Energy's niche is computation the runtime does not mediate: side generation, tool subprocesses, runtimes the auditor cannot trust or re-run.

v4 and v5

1:15 · at 8:35

v4 turns the pilot into a registered test with rules fixed before data. v5 asks whether an adaptive agent can sabotage successfully and still escape a joint energy-plus-monitor audit.

On screen
R0→R1→blind registration gate→W1→3 D windows→calibration, m ≥ 39 per pair + held-out test→independent recompute→release

Each box carries its [FREEZE] status (done, pending or refused), never an outcome. Qwen3.8-27B at 8-bit; the KM003C captured in every window; full transcripts sealed and unscored.

v5 endpoint: side-task success and no joint alarm, per attempt, reported with utility. The joint rule is calibrated at 0.05 and compared against energy alone, monitor alone, and time plus monitor. The monitor is a frontier model called by API, off the measured machine.

talk, 30 Nov→paper, mid-Dec→v5 windows, Dec–Feb→NeurIPS 2027

Likely questions

Can't the agent pad the log to match the energy?

Yes; it is one of the escape routes on slide 8. Padding has to be logged work, which the monitor reads. That trade is what v5 tests.

Why not just re-run the episode?

On a seeded local stack a rerun already catches stripped reasoning. Energy matters where you cannot re-run: untrusted runtimes, tool subprocesses, hardware you don't control.

Is 76 % of the energy outside the rails?

Only in this memory-traffic contrast. It says the rails are incomplete, not what share of inference they miss.

Isn't powermetrics controlled by the same host?

Yes. That is the pilots' main limit, and the reason every v4 window captures the external meter and v5 qualifies a DC-input endpoint.

What did v4 find?

Sealed until the registered release.
