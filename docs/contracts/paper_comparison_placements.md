## Reading and adoption rules

`CP-Xnn-*` are exact **proposed site identities**, not installed fill tokens.
Grouped historical token families identify one semantic table/census site;
repeated paragraph sites have separate identities. Existing token spellings and
dated retirement records remain historical references, never reactivation.
The four model/phase X4 floor sites explicitly include S6's existing registry
token families `TERMINAL_REFUSAL_REASON_*`, `NO_EXACT_FLOOR_REASON_*`,
`AVAILABLE_DIAGNOSTIC_CLAUSE_*`, and `POINT_DIAGNOSTIC_CLAUSE_*`. Naming them
here supplies S1's proposed token census to S6 without transferring ownership
or adopting an issuing binding; exact successor spellings and per-cell suffixes
remain subject to lead adoption.
Supplier names below are specification labels, not claims that code exists.
Artifact-field descriptions explicitly mark unsettled projection schemas;
no proposed field or grant is an adopted wire extension.

Every empirical supplier must obtain evidence through `open_paper_input(ref)`.
A named family is only a candidate route. `UNREGISTERED` is a role disposition,
not a supply-map key: lead adoption must bind exact roles and per-subject grants,
complete fixed/transitive censuses, inventory, receipt, fresh replay and reopen.
The pending small-model reported-energy role is not an issuing registration.
`UNRESOLVED` is no family or grant; no sixth family is invented. An authenticated
parent never licenses another placement. Every row, including proposed synthetic
and schematic copies, stays `PROPOSED_STOP_FILL`; existing illustrations retain
their present independent authorization. No paper text or selector changes here.

For refusal precedence, an authenticated governing before-comparison stop wins
before a close-out refusal is considered. Missing, invalid or unissued evidence
selects the fallback, not an empirical refusal. Table 3 preserves independently
issued outcomes. Registered not-evaluated wording in DS-32/PG-08 requires its
own authenticated stop/nonissuance route; file absence is insufficient.
The claim-side-bound semantics, joins and any numeric grant extension require
lead ruling. F+B remains planning-only and non-gating. Ratio A/B never implies
a model outcome. Adoption must install guarded prefill tokens and the two
D-166 split-refusal forms without creating PG-03.

The tables in this contract, the registry proposal appendix, and the custody
proposal appendix are checked together by `tests.test_paper_comparison_placements`.
They are specification agreement checks, not hardware or issuance validation.
