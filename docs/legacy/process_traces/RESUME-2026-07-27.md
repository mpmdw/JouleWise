drift **11.581 ms** against a 10.00 ms limit.

`b = max pulse-edge residual + effective trace-anchor bound`) settles the cause:

| term | Δ pre→post | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| pulse-edge onset residual | +10.803 ms | **93.28%** |
| effective anchor bound | +0.778 ms | 6.72% |
| wall-minus-monotonic | **−0.201 ms** | opposite sign |

Large wall-clock adjustments DID occur inside window B (up to 244.98 ppm,
40.09 ms max step) but **neither overlapped a calibration** — both calibrations
were smooth (+4.26 and +5.28 ppm, no step >0.035 ms). **Clock pinning does not
fix window B.** Its dominant term is fiducial onset-residual repeatability;
thermal-versus-dispatch fraction is **UNKNOWN** and needs its own investigation.

Also established: B's pre-calibration (35.436 ms) is the **highest fiducial in
the entire 22-calibration corpus**. The 7 prior passing brackets drifted
0.167–2.901 ms, and `instrument_calibration_mismatch` had **never fired before**.
B is a ~4× outlier, not a marginal-metric artifact.
