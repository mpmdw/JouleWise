05 / 07Anticipated questions · prepared replies
  • “Why gross headline?”Idle subtraction can penalize energy-proportional devices and reward high-idle ones. D-067 keeps both bases but makes gross the only ranking basis.
  • “Is idle stationary?”Not assumed. C-023-IDLE-STATIONARITY remains live; NEG-8 start/end cells and pre/post idle envelopes expose drift; the sentinel refuses contaminated idle windows.
  • “Sampler perturbation?”DF-TELEM-ON/OFF is designed, but the extra-sampler current-hardware layer was honestly unavailable tonight. External-meter validation awaits wall/PD hardware; no null is claimed without equivalence margins.
  • “Clock alignment?”The harness records spawn-ready wall/monotonic envelopes and bounds the anchor against the window. The 34.6 ms trace-boundary catch led to the anchor-lag drain fixes.
  • “Batch=1 is pathological.”Agreed. Static batching is live-verified supported with per-request evidence; probes passed, and AP-BATCH models E(B)=fixed+B·marginal under floor gates.
  • “n and uncertainty?”Core floor cells use , cross-repetition rules, and HAC variance in reducer 0.4.1+. Fixed-n/demotion prevents outcome-driven growth.
  • “Reproducibility?”Strict validation, hash-pinned artifacts, immutable raw evidence, a publication privacy pack, and deterministic re-reduction are in place. The independent external re-reduction is still pending.
