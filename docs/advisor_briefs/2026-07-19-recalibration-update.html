The 07-17 suite-cell inflation was root-caused to a macOS video screensaver engaging on an awake idle display — not the power source. An environment-guard admission system was built, adversarially reviewed through eight fix/audit rounds, merged, and then used to re-measure the suite cells in two bracketed quiet windows: , every member satisfying all D-077 admission predicates (three baselines show CPU-side excursions the GPU-idle classifier does not gate — recorded, not hidden).

What changed since the 17 Jul brief

Primary-data diagnosis attributed the contaminated suite cells (43 of 50 bundles, ~+30% energy, −11% throughput) to the macOS Ventura video screensaver engaging at HID-idle +20 minutes on an awake display — corroborated to the second in the power-management assertion log. The power-source hypothesis was checked and refuted: AC, 140 W, 100% charge throughout. The response (decision D-077) is an environment-guard admission system: campaign preflight over display/screensaver/power/thermal state with fail-closed criticals, a per-run idle admission gate with evidenced retry, a sustained-window cooldown v2, and an unwaivable environment_admission_failed claim barrier. It went through an eight-round adversarial fix/audit arc (every fix round independently re-audited) before merging.
