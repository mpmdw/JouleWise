# P2-038 anchor-lag-aware powermetrics drain

## Scope and authority

This follow-up implements the xhigh triage #2 ruling from the retained r4
production-shaped bundle diagnosis. The prior post-marker drain waited in wall
time, while reconstructed `timestamp_s` endpoints remained behind availability
time by the startup midpoint-anchor lag. No quiet-machine campaign, true-MLX
run, or live `/usr/bin/powermetrics` capture was run in this delegated agent
session.

## Decisions

### DRAIN-9 — Include the measured midpoint-anchor lag in the hard deadline

The drain deadline is now:

```text
measured_anchor_lag + 2 / power_hz + 0.25 s
```

The lag uses the same provisional five-stamp wall-minus-monotonic envelope as
the existing current-era clock derivation, with `sampling_stopped` standing in
for the unavailable post-termination `post_parse` stamp. Deadline time begins
before this calculation and the existing absolute deadline continues to guard
capture reads, parsing, evidence derivation, scans, and final-prefix extraction.
The existing `try`/`finally` termination path is unchanged.

### DRAIN-10 — Compare support endpoints in the reconstructed trace domain

The wall-stamped `sampling_stopped` endpoint is projected to the trace-relative
domain by subtracting the provisional point anchor. Parsed record support
endpoints advance only by the current-era rule (record zero at the point anchor,
records after zero by elapsed durations `1..i`). Drain success requires a parsed
support endpoint at or beyond that projected stop endpoint. In-window marker
stamping, reducer window clipping, raw parsing, and final authoritative-anchor
prefix selection are unchanged.

### DRAIN-11 — Pin the r4 failure and the live acceptance observables

The adapter regression uses r4-domain values: a 0.5188790559768677 s anchor
lag, a 5.95499587059021 s request window, an initial final support endpoint
0.034621477127075195 s short of `sampling_stopped`, and a 0.113498166 s next
interval. The next plist frame becomes available at 0.60 s after stop: beyond
the former 0.45 s deadline, but inside the new 0.9688790559768677 s budget. The
test requires that final support bracket the marker.

A separate test pins the exact anchor-lag-aware budget, and the silent-sampler
test requires bounded termination while retaining an honestly short final
support endpoint. The production-path assertion now also requires final support
at or beyond `sampling_stopped`, non-null bracketing and cadence, cadence at
least 4, and an empty idle-subtracted request reason list.

## Accepted lens-fix addendum

### DRAIN-12 — Fail closed on a nonsensical projected stop endpoint

The provisional drain bracket now rejects a projected trace-domain stop
endpoint that is non-finite or not strictly positive. This closes the accepted
lens reproduction where `point_anchor_s=90`, `sampling_stopped.epoch_s=87`,
and the projected endpoint is `-3`: record zero is not accepted as a bracket,
the drain exhausts its deadline, and the existing post-termination path reports
the captured support without a false bracket-success signal.

### DRAIN-13 — Cap the derived anchor lag before deadline construction

`MAX_POST_MARKER_ANCHOR_LAG_S = 5.0` is the named hard cap for the provisional
anchor-lag deadline contribution. The lag is validated after envelope
arithmetic, so finite inputs whose subtraction overflows to infinity and finite
inputs deriving a one-million-second lag both fall back to the five-second
capped contribution. The sampler-stop budget therefore remains bounded by the
cap plus two sampling intervals and the existing 0.25-second margin.
