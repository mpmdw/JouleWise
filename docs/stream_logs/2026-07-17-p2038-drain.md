# P2-038 bounded powermetrics stop drain

## Scope and authority

Deadline-critical implementation of the xhigh triage ruling: retain the
marker-bounded request window, but allow the live powermetrics process a bounded
post-marker interval to emit a sample that brackets the right edge before it is
terminated. No production or quiet-machine capture was run in this agent
session.

## Decisions

### DRAIN-1 — Drain only on the marker-bearing evidence stop path

The seam is `PowermetricsTelemetryAdapter.stop_sampling_with_evidence`, directly
before `_take_measured_capture` terminates the process. This method receives the
controller's already-created `sampling_stopped` stamp. The compatibility
`stop_sampling` path has no marker and remains unchanged.

### DRAIN-2 — Bound the drain by sample arrival or elapsed wall time

While the sampler remains alive, poll its native capture every 50 ms. Stop
polling when the current-era reconstructed final sample endpoint is at or after
`sampling_stopped`, or after `2 / power_hz + 0.25 s`, whichever comes first.
Termination continues through the existing process-stop helper after either
condition.

For the polling-only reconstruction, reuse `sampling_stopped` as a provisional
post-parse stamp. The authoritative evidence derivation still records the same
five stamps as before and obtains its real `post_parse` stamp after termination;
no new stamp or event is emitted.

### DRAIN-3 — Preserve marker-bounded measurement semantics

The additional native record is retained as raw evidence and reconstructed with
the existing point-anchor formula. Reducer windows remain bounded by the
`sampling_stopped` event and overlap-clip interval support, so support after the
marker contributes no measured-window energy. No parser, clock, controller, or
stamping formula changed.

## Fix-round addendum (2026-07-17)

### DRAIN-4 — Termination and the absolute deadline cover every drain outcome

The evidence stop path now takes the measured capture in a `finally` block, so
an exception from process polling, capture inspection, or provisional parsing
cannot bypass sampler termination or leave `_process` owned by the adapter. The
absolute drain deadline is checked before and after capture reads, plist
parsing, provisional evidence inputs, and evidence derivation. If an
indivisible operation consumes the remaining budget, no later expensive drain
step runs; control proceeds directly to termination. Losing a right-edge sample
remains visible to the production gate, while a zombie sampler is forbidden.

The fix-round tests inject a polling failure and an oversized capture that
grows while its read and plist parse jointly consume the complete budget. They
also compare the exact pre-drain plist frame bytes and closed-form endpoint
sequence against the final appended capture, ruling out a shifted or duplicated
in-window prefix.
