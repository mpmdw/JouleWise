## AXISC-1 — Generation and claim instrumentation are separate outcomes

Decision: every final semantic result records
`runtime_generation_supported` separately from `claim_instrumentable`.
Overall `supported` is conjunctive. External-draft generation that lacks
actual proposal counts or request-scoped decode-emission boundaries is
`unsupported_for_joulewise(event_observability)`, even if text and direct
accepted-draft flags are available.

Why: this directly applies the binding AXI xhigh consult, the landed AXI-SA
counter/event contract, and D-070's no-fixture/no-unobserved-mechanism claim
posture. Configured draft depth is not proposal evidence, and flattened token
callbacks are not decode-step emissions.

Evidence: `scripts/axi_sc_spec_decode_spike.py:derive_evidence_verdict`,
`:_claim_observability_evidence`; verdict document “Binding classification”.

## AXISC-2 — External draft and native MTP remain different families

Decision: the harness requires explicit mode `draft_model` or `native_mtp`.
External draft requires a non-null, distinct draft artifact and complete draft
identity. Native MTP forbids a draft path and keeps
`draft_model_identity:null`. The two modes have separate generation-failure
codes and are never pooled.

Why: an absent draft model can mean ordinary generation or native MTP, so null
identity alone is not a mechanism discriminator. The binding consult requires
separate contrast families, and any future MTP candidate selection belongs in
D-016 rather than this spike.

Evidence: parser/argument validation and requested-parameter fields in the
harness; verdict document source-level answers A and B.

## AXISC-3 — Pinned mlx-lm supports external drafts but hides AXI-SA proposal/step evidence

Decision: classify the installed external-draft generation path as a
source-level `feasible_candidate`, not as JouleWise support. Record public
`GenerationResponse.from_draft` flags as direct accepted-token observations.
Pinned mlx-lm 0.31.3 leaves `tokens_proposed` and `acceptance_rate` null and
emits no fabricated `decode_emission` events. The general harness dynamically
detects and exercises an explicit `speculative_decode_callback`; a runtime

decode-step emitted slices can reach `supported` after controller

Why: `speculative_generate_step` keeps actual proposal count, acceptance-loop
index, and verification-iteration boundary inside the generator. Public
`stream_generate` flattens the committed burst into singleton responses. The
configured `num_draft_tokens`, call shapes, or adjacent accepted flags could
reconstruct plausible values but would be inference forbidden by AXI-SA.

Evidence: installed `mlx_lm/generate.py:473-487`, `:593-634`, `:657-753`;
verdict document “External draft model”; focused configured-cap anti-gaming
