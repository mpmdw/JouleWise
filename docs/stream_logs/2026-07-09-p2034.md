# Stream ledger - P2-034 broad campaign packs (2026-07-09)

Branch: `p2034-broad-packs`. Scope: `docs/campaign_packs/` plus this ledger.
Entry IDs use `P2034-n` to avoid collision with the rqvar `P34-n` prefix.

### P2034-1 [contract] Six broad pack surfaces

Decision: P2-034 authors six campaign-pack files:
`c5_2_7_device_perf_w_rankings.md`,
`c5_2_8_placement_optimality.md`,
`c5_3_1_3_5_replication.md`,
`c5_i_1_i_2_i_5_import_family.md`,
`c5_i_3_flores_fertility.md`, and
`c5_i_4_harness_overhead_floor.md`.

Why: the ratified design groups work by plug-in-day campaign surface rather
than one file per RQ ID. The hard separations are import versus export, and
FLORES versus the generic import family.

Consequence: multi-question packs carry one DRAFT AP row per consumer while
sharing manifest, expected-artifact, figure, and runbook sections.

### P2034-2 [contract] Lead pins applied

Decision: the import-family pack reserves a second-family placeholder but names
no second source; HumanEval is the first executable L0-L1 milestone. C5-2.7
uses "runtime revision/build family held constant; backend kernels named" rather
than same-binary wording. C5-3.5 starts with the smallest method-transfer suite;
the high-value promoted bundle is iteration two.

Why: these are the lead-ratified resolutions to the design memo's open
questions.

Consequence: MMLU/tinyBenchmarks remain rejected as first imports, FLORES stays
separate, and cross-ISA runtime wording avoids an impossible same-binary
constraint.

### P2034-3 [guardrail] Claim ceilings and refusals copied from registry

Decision: every new AP row includes D-053 registry fields, countable
selection_scope, multiplicity rule, floor gate, denominator provenance, and the
registry ceiling plus exact forbidden upgrade.

Why: P2-034 is gated by the D-059 claims linter and the registry wording wins
over pack prose.

Consequence: pack text refuses generic heterogeneous-boundary device ranking,
optimal-placement claims before measured validation, population claims from one
unit, public benchmark credibility without replication, benchmark
capability/accuracy claims, causal difficulty-energy claims, tokenizer
efficiency ranking without both FLORES legs, and hidden harness-overhead item
energy claims.
