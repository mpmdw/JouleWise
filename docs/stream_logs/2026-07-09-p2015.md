### P15-2 UCB residual-percentile floor rule

[SUPERSEDED by P15-7 at the R2 fix round: estimator replaced by the false-effect guard floor; do not promote this entry]

Decision: floor cells use a one-sided 95% UCB on the 95th percentile of
absolute residuals or same-condition ABBA contrast residuals. Bootstrap is the
default at `n >= 10`; small cells use a conservative largest-residual fallback.
Why: the floor is a claim guardrail against false effects, not a mean-noise
estimate.
Consequence: campaign artifacts can be conservative without assuming normal
noise.
Confidence: medium-high pending counterreview.

### P15-3 [PROMOTE-TO-DECISION-LOG] Unknown error-budget terms cap claims

Decision: unknown sensor/systematic/drift/telemetry terms cap claim level; they
do not block L0/L1 operation, raw reduction, or strict validation.
Why: C-023 distinguishes instrument operation from L2/L3 claim readiness.
Consequence: the harness can keep producing auditable bundles while claim
tooling refuses over-strong wording.
Confidence: high.

### P15-4 [design] Same-condition ABBA supplies `floor_cmp_j`

Decision: comparative floors come from duplicate-label ABBA blocks where A and
B are identical configs/payloads. The artifact gate remains
`max(floor_abs_j, floor_cmp_j)` per `analysis_plans.md`.
Why: same-cell repeats alone do not expose ordering, cooldown hysteresis, or
false contrast effects.
Consequence: comparative claims inherit the larger of absolute noise and false
contrast noise.
Confidence: high.

### P15-5 [design] Telemetry perturbation is split into extra-sampler and true on/off layers

Decision: current hardware measures normal telemetry versus normal telemetry
plus extra task sampler. True telemetry-on/off energy perturbation is deferred
to wall/PD hardware because disabling the only telemetry removes the energy
trace.
Why: this satisfies the C-015/R2 sampler-smoke need without pretending to have
an independent meter.
Consequence: extra-sampler overhead can be bounded now; full telemetry
perturbation remains an `unknown` term and caps affected claims.
Confidence: high.

### P15-6 [design] Window-B revalidation tolerance

[SUPERSEDED by P15-7: tolerance rule now uses the false-effect guard floor and largest-residual check per detection_floor.md]

Decision: Window-B-start floor revalidation passes when the new UCB is within
1.25x the Window-A floor gate, or overlaps the prior UCB with no new quality
state. Otherwise the old floor is stale for Window-B L2/L3 claims.
Why: this catches OS/runtime/thermal drift without rerunning the full floor
campaign unless the instrument changed.
Consequence: Window B can begin with a cheap staleness check and top up only
affected metric/window classes.
Confidence: medium pending lead review.

### P15-7 [PROMOTE-TO-DECISION-LOG] [review-fix] R2 findings accepted and applied

Decision: accepted the fresh reviewer's 7/7 findings and all three design-call
verdicts for `docs/phase_2/detection_floor.md`.
Promotable current state: the estimator is the false-effect guard floor in
`detection_floor.md`, with the largest observed false effect and Student-t
prediction bound as the primary rule; bootstrap is sensitivity only. Unknown
sensor/systematic/drift/telemetry terms cap claim level but do not block L0/L1
operation, raw reduction, or strict validation. Window-B revalidation uses the
false-effect guard floor, largest-residual/contrast check, and no-new-state
requirements in `detection_floor.md`.
Fixes applied:

- FIX-1: replaced the 95th-percentile floor with the false-effect guard floor
  prediction-bound estimator; bootstrap is sensitivity only.
- FIX-2: added campaign economics with shared bundle counts, ABBA x4 sizing,
  Window-A/Window-B totals, runtime estimates, and cooldown cap-hit behavior.
- FIX-3: added the conservative request-floor condition-family mapping rule and
  optional AP-2 long-prompt/long-decode request floor cells.
- FIX-4: defined `n` as strict-valid bundles or ABBA blocks only; item windows
  cannot be counted as independent replicates.
- FIX-5: split stochastic variance propagation from deterministic drift bounds.
- FIX-6: changed cadence gates to window-local p95/bracketing max sample gaps
  and made request-window `>= 4.0` mandatory for L2/L3 absent a stronger AP
  bound.
- FIX-7: replaced wall/PD absolute-delta acceptance with a matched-block bridge
  model and demoted PD direction/ordering agreement to smoke evidence.
Why: the lead accepted the review findings and endorsed the concrete fix
shapes; the design doc needed to stop implying unsupported small-sample
percentile coverage.
Consequence: P2-015 now defines a defensible operational floor artifact and a
derivable campaign plan without amending contract files.
Confidence: high.
