## Family Taxonomy Chosen

The taxonomy uses one family per seeded estimand/campaign role, not one
family per paper section. This keeps the multiplicity accounting close to
the actual opportunity to cherry-pick.

| AP | family_id | role | rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| AP-1 | FAM-Q4-GRID-MODEL | primary | Holm across fixed/prompt/decode and holdout claims; sensitivities secondary unless frozen primary. |
| AP-2 | FAM-2M-SHAPE-CONTRASTS | primary | Holm across the frozen four-profile request/phase contrasts. |
| AP-3 | FAM-Q5-WITHIN-BOUNDARY-RANKS | primary | Holm within each frozen same-boundary workload-shape x metric rank family; expanded sweeps use BH q=0.10 and secondary/exploratory labeling. |
| AP-4 | FAM-C5W1-TOKEN-SHAPE-EQUIV | primary | Holm across category residual equivalence tests. |
| AP-5 | FAM-C5-LADDER-SCORED-ENERGY | secondary | Holm across predeclared ladder energy/energy-per-correct contrasts; item/difficulty sweeps use BH q=0.10 or exploratory. |
| AP-6 | FAM-CONTENT-SENTINEL-EQUAL-SHAPE | secondary | Holm across four condition-vs-repeated-seed content contrasts. |

Rationale:

- AP-1 through AP-4 are primary because they support the strongest
  claim-bearing mechanics: Q4 modeling, 2M shape asymmetry, within-boundary
  ranks, and token-shape sufficiency.
- AP-5 is secondary because correctness-bearing energy-per-correct remains
  deliberately quarantined and denominator-guarded.
- AP-6 is secondary because it is a sentinel/control for shape-generalization
  language rather than the main headline result.
- Holm is used for small confirmatory families. BH q=0.10 is named only for
  explicit sweeps where the registry permits a broader search; otherwise
  post-hoc findings are exploratory.
