## 2. THE CENTRAL MEASUREMENT FACT (read before any measurement decision)

**The instrument is ATTRIBUTION-limited, not NOISE-limited.** Ratified as **D-078 clause 11**
(Ed, 2026-07-25) — see `docs/decision_log.md`.

- Repeatability is excellent: **0.29–0.49 J** on ~50 J points; a settled reference pair three
  hours apart agreed to **0.007 J**.
- But energy *attribution across a phase boundary* is limited to **~0.7–1.0 J**, because the
  composed clock-anchor bound is ~31 ms and power swings ~33 W at the prefill/decode boundary.
- Composition is **additive** and measured: fiducial calibration 24.9 ms (80–87%) + bundle-local
  3.3–6.1 ms + edge span.

**Consequence:** `admissible_set_uncertainty_dominates_point_floor` is now a **labelled claim
path**, not a refusal. Extraction publishes the widened floor with `floor_source` naming the
dominant term, and keeps the point floor as a labelled repeatability diagnostic.

**BINDING CONDITION (Ed's, verbatim intent):** the anchor term appears in **both** the floor and
each claim's decision interval. Both are legitimate and different, so the effective clearable
effect is **floor + claim-side bound ≈ 5 J for phase contrasts** — not the floor alone. Every
publishing artifact must say so. Nobody may later delete one term as an apparent double count.

**Do not** launch an instrument-tightening program. It was measured and eliminated: extraction
would need a 10–32× bound reduction, and each cell's *bundle-local* term alone already exceeds
the entire required budget — **even a perfect calibration still refuses.** Revisit only if a
pilot shows target effects below ~3× the widened floor.

**Never re-collect hoping a window passes this gate.** It is structurally permanent.

  `CAL-REBRACKET-01` handles it now.
- The floor gate's refusal is not a data-quality signal — see §2.
- `git push --force` is blocked by the harness classifier; push to a new branch name instead.
- Site capsule pages have a 30 KB gzip+base64 shard budget; the decision log auto-paginates. Adding
