## 5. Plain reading against the judge's two outcomes

**Reading.** The data support neither outcome cleanly, and lean to a qualified version of the first. After the first six members the session is flat: from member 7 on the GPU decode-mean frequency has slope +0.003 MHz per step (n=44; kept only +0.05, n=34; null blocks only +0.03), Spearman rho -0.12 to -0.03, nowhere near a trend, and block medians stay between 1287 and 1319 MHz. The only downward movement is a warm-in over abs-r01 to r06 (1380, 1329, 1309, 1308, 1304, 1291 MHz; r01 is the single run that holds the 1380 MHz top state for the whole decode), which affects kept members only (r07 is the first lost member), and it ends at the plateau the rest of the session sits on. So there is no session-long heat-soak decline, but there is a six-run settling at the start. Within every slow-mode decode, lost or kept, the GPU falls about 125 MHz from the first third of decode to the last (lost median -127.5, kept slow-mode median -125.2), so limiting inside a decode is visible at 10 Hz in all of them equally, and is not specific to the lost. The lost members are not indistinguishable from their mates, though: all 10 lost members have a lower GPU mean than the mean of their clean mates (median -31 MHz, range -52 to -18; top-state fraction lower in 10 of 10, median -0.17), against a kept-versus-kept-mates spread whose 10th to 90th percentile is -41 to +75 MHz. The reason is that 8 of the 40 kept members (abs-r01, b02-b1, b03-b2, b04-a2, b05-b1, b07-a1, b08-a1, b09-b1) run in a distinct 'fast mode' (GPU mean 1336 to 1380 MHz, top-state residency 0.56 to 0.99, shortest decodes at 55.6 to 55.8 s, little in-decode fall, median -16 MHz), and none of the 10 lost does; against kept members in the slow mode (n=32, median 1292.5 MHz) the lost (median 1279.5, range 1267 to 1286) sit at the low edge (all 10 below the slow-kept median, but 5 of 32 slow kept members are as low) rather than outside it. Chance alone puts no lost member in the fast mode with probability 0.14, so this is weak evidence. In plain terms: the lost members were running at the slow end of the GPU frequency range, 18 to 52 MHz (1 to 4 percent) below mates on average, as slow-mode kept members do, but they did not run in some separate or deeper limited state, and the session does not drift downward after warm-in. Strength: moderate for 'no session drift after the first six runs', weak (p about 0.14, 10 members) for 'lost members are the slow-mode ones', and the data cannot say what puts a member in fast mode or whether that is a thermal effect. What would change this reading: knowing what distinguishes the fast-mode runs (their sparse, unpatterned placement across blocks and their shorter decodes suggest a separate cause, not order).

Confirmation: no power, energy, current, voltage, battery or joule/watt field was read, printed, computed with or written; power-like keys in the rich records were discarded at load before any use, and power_trace.csv, summary_metrics.json, outputs/ and raw/powermetrics.plist.zst were not opened.
