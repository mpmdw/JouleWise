### Q2 — conditional support

**Unreadable historical capture bytes need not stop finalization if every number comes from authenticated, frozen acceptance/ledger records and the window’s own verified captures. “Hash chain and pin for the rest” must not conceal a known mismatch or omit an unavailable observation from a calculation.**

Current finalization requests whole-ledger custody verification (`joulewise/analysis_manifest_v3.py:3641–3663`), which stops on the first custody failure (`calibration_ledger.py:2396–2414`). The registration instead distinguishes changed, acceptance-relied captures from unreadable captures and unused changed captures (`registration_block5.md:4419–4452`). That supports removing the unreadability stop.

The number-changing case is a historical observation contributing to an acceptance threshold or **B_max** being silently dropped because its raw capture is unreadable. B_max includes all eligible ledger observations through T, including systematic-invalid ones; readability is not a selection criterion (`trigger-erratum/ERRATUM.md:331`; `L9_DESIGN.md:1590–1613`).

Keep the acceptance’s sealed byte binding, cutoff sequence/digest, complete authenticated ledger prefix and terminal pin, observation classifications and recorded bounds, and fresh authentication of both own captures. Never substitute or recompute an unavailable historical capture. Preserve any recorded acceptance-relied custody mismatch as blocking; an unreadable sibling must not downgrade it (`calibration_ledger.py:6648–6654`). A chain proves recorded bytes and history, not that unavailable raw evidence remains unchanged.

This conclusion is from source inspection; no capture or ledger data was opened.

### Q3 — F3 should_fix

**The descriptive contrast proposal is defensible, but inferred rather than explicitly specified. The proposed omission of all floor-cell lines is not clearly supported by the text.**

The rule says “any unit of a cell or contrast” and “the same estimator” (`analysis_plan_block5.md:529–540`). The battery-assist contrast rule explicitly limits computation to steps 1–4 and excludes Holm, floor decisions and outcome words (`558–565`). That is a sensible analogy for a line licensing no claim, but it is still an analogy.

Reported mean, half-width and counts follow the worked example. For readers, label the contrast interval **metrology interval**, state that remaining exclusions still apply, and retain the sentence that this sensitivity licenses no claim. Restored counts and which units were restored should be inspectable beside the line.

The counts syntax does not itself exempt floors: the plan calls them cells with registered estimators and units (`282–287`), and the design’s own terms include floor cells (`L9_DESIGN.md:73–74`). Record the intended scope before implementing “no floor line”; do not present that omission as textually settled.
