## §1. Q1 — Converging gauntlet, not sunk-cost continuation

**Verdict: converging gauntlet.** The evidence is the finding classes across
the four rounds, and they do not repeat — they move outward and shrink:

1. **Rounds 1–2 (T26, pre-lexicon):** unbuilt or later-built vocabulary inside
   replacement prose — twice, same signature. That WAS a same-signature pair,
   and it was treated as the doctrine requires: not with a third writer round
   but with a structural cure (built-terms lexicon + first-use lint, R-1). The
   lint mechanically reproduced the seats' failure signature (74 findings on
   the held plan) and now holds at 0/94.
2. **Round 3 (adjudication 1):** the mechanical layer at zero; the failures
   moved to a class the lint is structurally blind to — properties of the
   UNREPLACED draft (R1: unbuilt ≥2 threshold contradicting surviving lines
   21/185/356; R2: decision-bearing \(R_{cm}\) with no construction anywhere).
   17 blockers, 11 of 15 blocking blocks inheriting from the two roots.
3. **Round 4 (adjudication 2):** the seat's own synthesis states the two prior
   roots ARE cured at their definition sites. The failures moved again, to the
   cure's perimeter: three surviving draft sites (96/103/298), the cure's own
   inputs one level down (\(q_j\)), consistency among the cure's restatements
   (guarded vs unguarded), a missing reporting slot, one un-anchored span.
   6 blockers, every one "cheap to cure (a quoted span, a clause, one added
   column, one built definition)" per the seat, with exactly one requiring a
   ruling already made (S04's ≥2 criterion for the label word).

The standing trigger is two consecutive rounds failing with the SAME
signature. Rounds 3 and 4 do not share one: round 3's roots are cured and
round 4's blockers are residue of the cure itself — new material whose inputs
and survivors were not fully swept. Blocker count 17→6, severity narrowing
from "mechanism the paper never contains" to "clause and anchor defects," and
the mechanical floor holding at zero across both rounds is the shape of a
gauntlet converging, not a loop treating motion as progress.

One honest caveat, recorded so the magistrate does not over-read this verdict:
the *meta*-class "the sheets assert what the unreplaced draft contradicts or
never builds" has now appeared twice (R1/R2, then BL-1/2/3), each time at
strictly narrower scope. The failing instrument is the string-keyed census
(BL-9's `will `-keyed sweep missed two survivors in round 3; the criterion
vocabulary at lines 96/103/298 was never swept in round 4). That is a defect
of the sweep method inside the formulation, not of the formulation — it is
handled as a specification on the next round (§2), not a formulation change.

## §2. Q2 — Ruling: (a) ONE bounded perimeter round

**Chosen: (a).** The six local cures plus the eight should-fixes exactly as
adjudication 2 specifies, then ONE final fresh adjudication; a further failure
PARKS the apparatus until the campaign's fill session, with no discretion to
run a further pre-data round. Two specifications binding on the round:

- The writer's sweep for surviving retired vocabulary must be concept-keyed,
  not string-keyed: an explicit pass over the draft for every occurrence of
  the retired criterion family (exceed/exceeds/exceedance applied to the
  dominance test, "dominance predicate", "contributed more than scatter",
  `_v4`, `256`/`p256`) with each hit dispositioned in the sheet — the two
  census escapes to date (BL-9, then BL-1/BL-2) were both string-key misses.
- Every paper-facing restatement of \(R\) (Outcome A, H28, Item 10, H01)
  must carry or reference the unguarded qualifier per S02's definition —
  one binding definition, restatements subordinated to it.

**Deciding consideration, including spend.** The campaign data does not exist,
so round 7 cannot run now regardless of this ruling, and option (c)'s premise —
that further pre-data polishing is negative-value — would be right if the six
blockers needed data or were mere polish. They are neither. The seat is
explicit that none requires new evidence or a new ruling; all are locally
curable from what already exists. That makes their cost now one bounded writer
round + one adjudication (small against the four rounds already spent, and the
apparatus is demonstrably one perimeter away from usable), while their cost
under (c) is paid at the worst time and partly in silence: a parked sheet with
six KNOWN blockers, resumed weeks later inside a busy fill session, is exactly
the ruled-not-installed environment in which BL-6's un-anchored span leaves
the retired 256-token arithmetic on the page and BL-4's ambiguity yields a
wrong \(R\) against a threshold of exactly 2 — defects the fill session's live
adjudication may catch only after they have shaped the filled page, or not at
all (BL-4 produces a plausible wrong number, not a visible contradiction).
Cheap now, expensive-and-silent later decides (a) over (c).

Against (b): a formulation change is what round 2's same-signature pair
correctly bought (the lexicon+lint). No such pair exists now — the current
formulation (frozen draft + two sheets + mechanical lint + fresh-seat
adjudication) is the thing that caught every one of these defects, each round
at narrower scope. Replacing it after its best round yet would spend a new
formulation's failure surface to fix a sweep-method defect that a one-line
specification covers. The park-on-failure clause inside (a) is the self-limiting
guard: this ruling authorizes one round, not a resumed loop; a fifth failed
adjudication parks the apparatus automatically, with no further cold consult
needed to enforce it.
