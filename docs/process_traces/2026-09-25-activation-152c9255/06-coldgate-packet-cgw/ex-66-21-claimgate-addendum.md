## 5. Claim-gate final texts v2 (CG-1..CG-4) — self-contained, supersedes ex-20 §5

**CG-1 (directional claim).** Estimand θ = the equally weighted mean over the k registered quiet-window envelopes of the envelope-mean contrast E[B−A] in joules (or J/correct), on one frozen instrument epoch, registered sign. Each envelope is one replicate. The registration states a fixed block count n_reg per envelope; an envelope retaining fewer than n_reg blocks is excluded before estimation and listed in the artifact; k counts retained envelopes; k < 5 → `not_estimable`. (When every envelope retains n_reg, θ equals the block-population mean; a registration that needs the block-population mean under unequal n_e must register a weighted estimator and its uncertainty rule in advance, none is ruled here.) Envelope observation: value_a = mean of block A values, value_b = mean of block B values. Stochastic terms per envelope: for a term whose `correlation_scope` is `independent_run`, variance_a(e) = Σ_i variance_a(i) / n_e², variance_b(e) = Σ_i variance_b(i) / n_e², covariance_ab(e) = Σ_i covariance_ab(i) / n_e² (division by n_e², not √n_e); for a term registered as shared across every block of an envelope, the block-level variance and covariance are carried once, undivided; any other or missing scope → `not_estimable`, reason `envelope_term_scope_unknown`. Deterministic terms per envelope: mean of the per-block bounds. Feed the k envelope observations to `estimate_paired_blocks` (df = k−1). F_est = g(k_cal)·(|mean_cal| + t_{0.975,k_cal−1}·s_cal/√k_cal) with mean_cal, s_cal the mean and SD of the k_cal envelope-mean null (same-model ABBA) contrasts of the same-epoch accepted calibration, g = `small_sample_guard_factor`; k_cal < 5 → `not_resolvable`. F_est carries the estimand's unit and contains no timing term; B enters once, on the claim side. Admit `direction_supported` iff (1) Holm-adjusted two-sided p < 0.05 at frozen m (AP-5M: 5, missing kept); (2) metrology interval and decision interval θ̂ ± (h+B) both exclude 0 on the registered side; (3) |θ̂| > F_est strictly. Optional registered magnitude claim "by more than τ": register τ ≥ F_est before data; additionally require decision-interval near endpoint > τ and Holm on the shifted null p = P(T > (|θ̂|−τ)/SE). Claim wording is scoped to the measured nights unless blocks span ≥ 3 distinct calendar-date sessions. Sign-flip (`exact_sign_flip_p_value`) is a printed diagnostic with its minimum attainable p; it bears on no decision when k < 8. F_block remains the published single-block instrument characteristic and never gates a mean.

**CG-2 (equivalence claim and claim-shape rule).** Register, before data, Δ in the estimand's unit with Δ > F_est + B strictly, recording the F_est value and the calibration artifact id used.
