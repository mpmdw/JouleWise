## 3. A2 — the equivalence path: NOT taken; W1 is derivation window one

**Ruling: derive the successor directly. Revision 5 supersedes revision 2's equivalence look for this epoch.** This reverses ex-20 J3, and the charge presents that question expressly (charter §9).

Reasons, physical first:
1. **The July envelope was set by frames of one length; the new regime has frames of another.** B is the worst fitted edge residual plus the anchor bound (P8), and edge residuals grow with the sample interval: the same instrument at ×2.07 frames gave ×1.57 the median B. At ×1.10 frames the expected shift is about +6 %. r7's level screen is exactly r7's corpus maximum, so a continued acceptance would bind every later bracket capture to a ceiling that sits inside the new regime's upper tail.
2. **The equivalence look cannot tell a +6 % regime from an identical one.** With 12 draws, P(PASS) is roughly 0.5–0.6 at +5 % against ≈0.5–0.8 at +0 % (P8). A PASS therefore does not establish equivalence; it establishes "not grossly different", and the paper would have to say so. That is the bad-science risk the gate exists to stop.
3. **A PASS is expensive to make mechanically true.** It needs B1's sites (ii) and (iii), a continuation registry entry (the registry is empty on main, `bracketing:196`), a continuation file, and its dated addendum PR, all claim-bearing and all exercised only on the branch that has at most even odds. The direct path needs none of them.
4. **What the path saves.** On FAIL it costs no extra window (prereg `:477`); on PASS it saves W2 and the successor issuance, about 0.4 expected windows. Against that, a continued r7 trips its screen at 4–7 % per claim window under a +3–5 % shift (P8) and then needs the same two derivation windows anyway, later, with a lost claim window on top.
5. **Authority.** Ed's issue 316 ruled the equivalence look to avoid three blind nights. The direct path is two windows with rules fixed before data, which is the blindness Ed defined. The D-184 addendum gives the council registration-text design; Ed is told plainly in R14 and the arm-notice NO stands.

Consequences: ex-20 R5(m) is deleted; the m < 6 INCONCLUSIVE rule and the < 6/12 futility stop no longer describe the same night with two different outcomes (an inconsistency in ex-20 the charge did not list, finding X1); B1's cure narrows to R4.
