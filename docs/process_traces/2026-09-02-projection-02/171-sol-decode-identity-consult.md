## Findings

**F1 — blocker — Prefer a distinct unit-set identity (option d).** Reviewer sentence: keep `scientific_config_identity` exact per config and give the four projection units a separately named closed-set hash; do not redefine exact identity as set membership.

Q1: `scientific_config_identity()` type-normalizes the complete config and removes only `run_id`, replacement/calibration tags, and `repN`; it therefore retains the entire `workload_profile` and the `decode-prompt=…` tag (`joulewise/identity_pins.py:217-236`, `configs/campaigns/d117_contrast_v5/generate_configs.py:1858-1879`). Suite reference and effective-manifest SHA are typed workload fields and are emitted together (`joulewise/schemas.py:898-955`, `joulewise/schemas.py:1120-1138`). D-044 deliberately makes manifest bytes part of run identity and says sameness is hash equality, not membership (`docs/decision_log.md:2465-2513`).

The real decode identity is consequently eight exact per-prompt identities per arm, not one. Rotation selects eight manifests (`configs/campaigns/d117_contrast_v5/generate_configs.py:1394-1402`, `configs/campaigns/d117_contrast_v5/generate_configs.py:1660-1709`); freeze compares every config’s full workload with one declaration and then demands one scientific hash (`joulewise/identity_pins.py:1236-1253`, `joulewise/identity_pins.py:1445-1466`).

No referenced floor plan exists in the supplied generated root (V2). The generator creates producer names/references but its output inventory contains only the contrast pack (`configs/campaigns/d117_contrast_v5/generate_configs.py:1042-1044`, `configs/campaigns/d117_contrast_v5/generate_configs.py:1267-1295`, `configs/campaigns/d117_contrast_v5/generate_configs.py:2551-2565`). The floor mint likewise rejects members with multiple scientific identities (`scripts/mint_floor_artifact.py:755-805`). Thus current end-to-end binding is exact prompt-manifest/config identity, not merely model/runtime/profile, and the rotating arm cannot bind to one per-arm floor.
