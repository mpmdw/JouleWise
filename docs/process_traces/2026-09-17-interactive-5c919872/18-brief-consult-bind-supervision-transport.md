FORCING PROBLEM (executed evidence; the escalation rule fired). The night driver's bind loop (`scripts/run_night.py`, `_BindTask` around lines 2000–2040 and its caller around 2100–2120; read them) supervises a sampler worker process that produces one quiet-admission observation per interval, while the parent must keep running the agent census every 30 s and must fire the bind deadline on time no matter what the worker does. Three consecutive review rounds found the supervision blockable, each on a different edge of the same seam:
- Round 1 (execution refuter, `docs/process_traces/2026-09-17-interactive-5c919872/13-refuter-execution-astra.md` F6): `ready()` called `process.join()`; the prescribed regression used a fake task and proved nothing about the real one.
- Round 2 fix: `ready()` polls with zero timeout; regression uses a real indefinitely sleeping worker (pre-send hang) — killed the join mutant.
- Delta re-audit (`16-delta-reaudit-astra.md` F1, BLOCKER): `ready()` establishes pipe READABILITY, not the availability of a complete message; `result()` then performs an unbounded `recv()` on the supervisor thread. Same signature as round 1: a production blocking path outside what the test exercised.

Also recurring: real-tool interface assumptions (round 1 `top -s 30.0` rejected by `top`; bench fix for `top`'s independently rounded user/sys/idle percentages not summing to 100), found only by the lead running the sampler natively.

CONSTRAINTS THAT BIND THE DESIGN: the census and the bind deadline are evaluated on every tick of the parent regardless of worker state (brief D3/D6); on expiry the parent terminates and reaps the worker (no zombie); a malformed, partial or absent observation is an ERROR sample (terminal `night_probe_error`), never "quiet"; the worker runs `ps`, `top -l 2 -s N` (N integer seconds) and `sysctl`, each already under a bounded subprocess timeout; observation size is small (a few KB; top consumers capped at ten) but diagnostics may be larger; no new dependency beyond the standard library; macOS only; the sampler cost is counted (observer included) and must stay ≈ 0.2–0.3 cpu-s per 30 s round; existing tests in `tests/test_run_night.py` (regressions 1, 2, 5, 8, 10 of the seat brief `01-brief-seat-quiet-admission.md`) must keep passing or be revised with a stated reason.

THE LEAD'S CANDIDATE (disagree if you can): replace the pipe with an atomic-file handoff. The worker writes its complete observation JSON to `<night_dir>/quiet_sample.<n>.tmp` and `os.replace`s it to `<night_dir>/quiet_sample.<n>.json` as its LAST act; the parent never reads a pipe: readiness = the final path exists (one `os.stat`), result = read the file (bounded by its size; parse errors → ERROR sample), and the worker's exit status is collected with `join(0)`/`waitpid(WNOHANG)` only. A worker that dies mid-write leaves only a `.tmp` (ignored) and is detected by its exit or by the per-sample deadline (interval + grace) at which the parent kills and reaps it and records an ERROR sample. The supervisor thread disappears; the bind loop is a single `while` on the parent's clock with a census tick, a deadline check, and a non-blocking sample poll.
