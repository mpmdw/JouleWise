### SG-1 with RF-4. The cell rule (written about 21:41 PDT; the section times below were first typed as estimates and corrected against `date` at 21:47)

**AMENDED. The minimum is 5.** `rules.cell_unit_minimum` changes from 8 to 5 (a C change); the rule still applies
per stratum per target cell, a removed quad member still removes its whole quad, and `cell.below_minimum` still
removes the window.

*Why.* The doctrine allows an exclusion for two reasons only: a physical hazard measured directly, or a number that
would be wrong or could not be attributed. The allowlist's own text for `cell.below_minimum` names neither: "fewer
valid members in a cell than the registered minimum (statistical power of the reported number)". With 7 kept units a
cell's stratified mean is right, its interval is right (Student's t with 6 degrees of freedom, 1.29 times the
full-n half-width), and every unit is attributable. That is a precision preference, and precision is not a ground
the doctrine admits for discarding a day of clean measurement. The number-integrity boundary in the registered
estimators is 5: the floor guard `joulewise.detection_floor.small_sample_guard_factor` is defined for 5 ≤ n < 10 and
raises "guard factor undefined below n=5" at n = 4 (EXECUTED). Below 5 units in a stratum the cell's floor cannot be
computed, so the floor cell has no number and every GAMMA contrast judged against that floor is not resolvable; a
reported cell with n = 1 has no variance. "No number" is the same integrity ground the catalog already uses for
`roster.no_science_bundles`. So the exclusion is sound at 5 and not above.

*What it costs and buys.* Recomputed from the registration's own loss model (one member lost independently with
probability 1/37): a floor window is claim-usable with probability 0.8486 at a minimum of 8, 0.9709 at 7, 0.9961 at
6, 0.9996 at 5. Of the 15.1% of floor windows the present rule removes, 14.5 points have exactly one target cell
short (at 7 of 10 quads) and the other whole. If the true member-loss rate is 5% (forty codes remove a member; only
idle-admission aborts have a measured rate), the present rule removes half of all floor windows (0.508 usable) and
the amended rule keeps 99% (0.991); at 8% the figures are 0.169 against 0.929. The price of the amendment is a wider
printed interval when a stratum is short: the half-width factor t(0.975, n − 1)/t(0.975, 9) × √(10/n) is 1.169 at
8, 1.293 at 7, 1.467 at 6, 1.736 at 5; the floor guard g(n) is 1.134, 1.225, 1.342, 1.5. Against the addendum's
counter-argument (a pack is never re-armed after a claim-usable attempt, so a short cell is never tightened by a
second window): under the registered loss rate that case is the 7-of-10 case almost every time, where the re-arm
would narrow one cell's interval by about 10% at the cost of a day; and the kept-unit counts are printed beside every
cell with the attempt history, so the reader sees the n.

*(i) Minimum, per stratum per target cell:* AMENDED to 5; per stratum per target cell CONFIRMED (the code applies
`minimum[stratum]` to every stratum of every cell with `target` true; a cell with `target: false` can never be the
reason: synthetic non-target cell at 5 of 10 quads with the target at 10 of 10 gives `claim_usable` true, EXECUTED).
*(ii) Whole-quad removal:* CONFIRMED (two lost members in one quad cost one quad: kept {quad 9, repeat 10}, EXECUTED).
*(iii) Window-level effect of `cell.below_minimum`:* CONFIRMED at the amended minimum; category NUMBER_INTEGRITY; the
number it protects: below 5 kept units in a stratum the registered floor estimator is undefined, so the cell's floor
and every contrast judged against it have no number.
*(iv) Fixed weights 0.2 / 0.8 and the smaller stratum's degrees of freedom:* CONFIRMED; the plan must print the
quantiles for 6, 5 and 4 degrees of freedom and the guard at 7, 6 and 5 (changes below).
*(v) Window scope against cell scope (position E):* CONFIRMED window scope. Attempts are never mixed (§7.2), so a

and the other whole costs a window with probability 0.0004 under the registered loss model, so the question is nearly
moot.
