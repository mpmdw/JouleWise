## Recommendation

Adopt cure 1 (capture-only collector, batch finalisation after envelope 12) **but not alone**: the record's decomposition hides two serial costs cure 1 does not touch, and with the A267 attestation added the plain cure 1 lands at roughly 3.5–4.5 s per slot, failing R6's 2 s bar. I verified all twelve rows of record 02's table from the archive stamps, then timed the pieces on this bench: `parse_frames` 5.30 s, `align_frames` 2.37 s (bounded case), sha256 of the 130 MB plist 0.05 s, one `pgrep -g` census 13 ms, and the 113 journaled groups per envelope make `cleanup_groups` about 1.5–2.3 s of the "2.8 s exit + cleanup + spawn" bucket; each of the 19 round rows also re-hashes the plist. The design that reaches ≤ 2 s with margin is three moves: (1) capture-only collector that stamps `recorder_exited` and exits ~0.4 s after `sampling_stopped`; (2) the chain spawns collector i+1 at its scheduled instant after a *bounded* wait (≤ 1.5 s) for collector i's exit, and only then runs envelope i's attestation and cleanup, inside the next slot's 60 s lead-in and before the next sleep; (3) per-slot cleanup restricted to journal rows written before that spawn (a line-count cut), so the reaper never touches the new collector's groups. Expected drift: 0.4–0.7 s chain-level, ~0.6–0.9 s in `session.json`, margin > 1 s. A strictly serial cure 1 plus a single-`ps` census would sit at ~1.8 s session-level — technically under the bar with no margin — so if move (2) is refused on R4-fidelity grounds, say so and take R6's re-registration branch rather than ship a design that meets the bar only on a warm log store.

## Q3 — Output contract

Collector at exit (stage 1): `raw/powermetrics-idle-N.plist` complete and the recorder reaped (`power.cleanup.returncode`), `raw/round-*/`, the provisional `rounds.jsonl` already journaled per round (`:801–805`, unchanged), and `session.json` stage 1 = today's session minus reduction: `power = {argv, identity, processes, cleanup, term_sent, kill_sent, stamps: {pre_spawn, first_parse, sampling_started, sampling_stopped, recorder_exited}, anchor: null}`, `interior: null`, `end_stamp`, `whole_envelope_observer_cpu_s`, `start_drift_s`, `finalisation: {"state": "pending"}`. `recorder_exited` is stamped right after `process.wait()` returns and is fed to the deriver as `post_parse`: the deriver uses that stamp only as the controller-coverage causal bound (`uncertainty_evidence.py:984–992`, `post_parse.after − pre_spawn.before ≥ Σ elapsed`), and "the recorder has exited" bounds every frame exactly as "the parse finished" did; a deferred post-parse stamp would pass that check trivially and lose its diagnostic force.
Chain, per slot, after collector i exits: reads stage-1 `power.stamps.sampling_started/sampling_stopped.epoch_s`, runs the R4 query, writes `timed-log.txt` and rewrites `session.json` atomically with `network_time_provenance.attestation` (A267 brief Part 3 item 12, unchanged).
Finaliser (`sample_quiet_predicate_evidence.py finalize --out envelope-NN`, run by the chain for each envelope after the last capture, each bounded to ~60 s): parse → `align_frames(frames, stamps)` → `reduce_interior` → per-row `integrate` and raw hashes → rewrites `rounds.jsonl` and `session.json` read-modify-write, carrying `network_time_provenance` (with attestation) forward untouched, adding `power.anchor`, `interior`, `finalisation: {"state": "complete", "finalised_epoch_s", "stage1_sha256"}`; keeps `session.stage1.json` for audit. Failure leaves stage 1 in place; `pilot_summary` then excludes by the existing names (`clock_anchor_unresolved`, `incomplete_interior_support`, `:349–352`) — no new exclusion vocabulary, so no protocol re-registration. `pilot_summary` consumes the final `session.json` exactly as today (`anchor.status`, `interior.complete_support`, `start_drift_s`, `interior.power.energy_j`).
