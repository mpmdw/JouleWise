## Q1 — ruled option (a), in **digest-keyed-table** form, not "a second digest"

**No v2 schema key is needed, and the registration document must NOT be the selector.** D-166's thirteen keys are criterion content (`kind`, `ratio_id`, `threshold`, `numerator`, `denominator`, …; verified sha256 `dfe55f8d…c265` = `night_gate.py:37`); `kind` means "comparative", not a plan class. Making the document declare its class means editing frozen pre-registered bytes and changing the pinned digest — the re-freeze the guard exists to make visible. The selector must live outside the bytes: **the digest is the key.**

Shape (`joulewise/night_gate.py`): a module constant `REGISTERED_REGISTRATIONS` mapping each accepted digest to `(registration_class, registration_basename, ruling_ref)` — one entry for D-166, one for the evidence pre-registration. In `_check_registration` (`:1331-1362`) replace the equality at `:1349` with a table lookup, refusing `night_refused_registration` "registration sha256 … is not a registered pre-registration". **Add the binding a bare digest set destroys:** require `entry.basename == Path(plan.registration_path).name` and that the chain match the entry — `rows["C5"].measured["chain_sha256"]`/`chain_path` are already populated, since `evaluate_static` runs `_check_chain_identity` (`:1074`, digest `:1102`) before `_check_registration` (`:1377`). Bind the evidence entry to its chain **digest** (frozen per campaign), D-166's to its chain **basename** (`calibration_derivation_only.zsh`), so ordinary calibration-chain edits need no new gate ruling.

Receipt row (`:1361-1362`): `measured["detail"]` becomes `f"registered pre-registration {ruling_ref} ({registration_class}) hash passed"`, and `measured` gains `registration_class` and `registration_ruling`; the refusal at `:1356` loses "does not match D-166". Docs: `NIGHT_HANDBACK.md:134` is the authority clause; the C1 description and refusal table (`:88-110`) take the registered-set wording. The runbook is untouched by Q1.

**Strongest reason 44a is wrong:** it rules only that "the gate learns an evidence-registration digest" — literally a *widening*: with a bare second digest a calibration night can name the evidence registration (or the reverse) and C1 still PASSes, so the false attestation is reachable from both directions. 44a also never rules the attestation itself: `night_gate.py:1362` writes the literal `"D-166 registration hash passed"` into every DIAGNOSTIC_NO_PACK receipt, so a seat that adds a digest and stops ships a receipt that still lies. ## Q2 — ruled (a) idle-only first, with a ruled δ and upper-bound sizing

Protocol: settle 900 s, then **N = 12** consecutive 600 s envelopes with a predeclared interior 480 s analysis interval (≈2 h 15 min), no load generator, `quiet_admission.sample_interval` as recorder only. Exclusions frozen before the arm: census not clean; AC/thermal/HID probe failure or missing round; cadence or anchor unresolved; 480 s interior not fully covered; recorded busy-cores above the pre-registered PROVISIONAL threshold. The summary reports per-envelope J per 480 s; `s1` (envelope sd) **and** `sΔ` (sd of consecutive-envelope differences — the only one that sizes a paired campaign); the one-sided 95 % **upper bound** on `sΔ`; observer cost including census; cadence; boot id and OS build; filtered and unfiltered.

Arithmetic both seats get wrong. Paired sizing is `n ≈ 8 sΔ²/δ²` pairs; `sΔ` from N envelopes has df = N−1 and one-sided 95 % upper factor `√(df/χ²_{0.05,df})`: **N=6 → df 5 → 2.09×** (3 disjoint pairs → df 3 → 2.92×), **N=12 → df 11 → 1.55×**. Since n scales as `sΔ²`, a 75-min N≈6 pilot can under-size block two by **4–8×**; N=12 caps it at ~2.4×. δ was never ruled: at `sΔ = 3 J`, n = 3 pairs at δ = 5 J (claim-side bar), 72 at δ = 1 J (attribution limit), **288 at δ = 0.5 J** — the margin adopted in Exhibit E record 14 cl. 6, ~96 h of quiet machine; a ~100× spread across the three bars. **Rule δ = 0.5 J/slot and pre-register the stop branch:** if the *upper bound* on `sΔ` implies more pairs than the ruled budget (propose 24 pairs ≈ 8 h), report a one-sided upper bound with its coverage and seal no cutoff. An idle slot is ~1440 J at ~3 W (illustrative), so `sΔ ≲ 0.6 J` demands 0.04 % stability; 1 J/480 s = 2.083 mW ≈ 0.0004 core at 5 W/core, below the sampler's own cost (`night_quiet_admission.md:45-56`). Expect the null branch and pre-register it as acceptable.

**Can an idle-only pilot be inadmissible under v2's own admission? Yes, both ways.** (i) it can refuse: `LOAD_MAX = 2.0` (`night_gate.py:60`, `:1218-1245`) refused a census-clean machine at load 3.66 on 09-17 (`night_quiet_admission.md:38-44`), as can thermal/HID/displaysleep/AC. (ii) it can **admit** a contaminated pilot: load 1.3–1.5 passed while `fseventsd` held 85–100 % of a core (same lines), and v2 admission is evaluated **once** — the only in-window re-check is the agent census loop (`run_night.py:880-905`). Nothing re-tests machine quiet across 2 h of envelopes, so the pilot's admission certifies nothing about its own idle reference.

**Strongest reason 44a is wrong:** it adopts "sampler as recorder, exclude post hoc" as the *cure*. The recorder is only the instrument; the cure is the **frozen exclusion rule**, whose number 44a leaves to the analyst. A busy-core exclusion threshold chosen after seeing the data is numerically a cutoff — the campaign that exists to justify a cutoff would have used an unjustified one to clean its own reference, the circularity ruling 70 refused. It belongs in the Q1 registration document, PROVISIONAL, named before the arm; Q1's digest binding is what makes "frozen before arming" enforceable instead of promised.

## Q3 — a second probe kind IS an amendment, but of a different clause than 44a names

**Ruled (a), with its own schema string.** The runbook verify-only row (`derivation_night_runbook.md:3048`) is behavioural — access checks, stops before append/settle/capture, non-authorizing, matching fingerprints, fresh timestamps — so an evidence probe that verifies manifest bindings, interpreter, chain digest and harness identity and starts nothing **conforms**; it needs a new chain-exit entry, not an amendment. Amend to payload-class bindings (calibration: ledger head + bytes; evidence: sealed manifest digest + harness identity).

Schema: **`joulewise.night_probe_receipt.evidence.v1`**, not v1 with optional keys — the v1 validator hard-codes calibration arithmetic with no honest evidence analogue: `elapsed * WRITER_CUSTODY_PASSES * CUSTODY_HEADROOM_FACTOR > budget` (`night_agent_install.py:834`), `observations == 0` versus finalized ledger rows (`:860-868`), and field equality against `probe_bindings()` (`:873-874`), whose output carries `ledger_head_sha256`, `ledger_sha256`, `ledger_pin_sha256` (`:751-791`). Reusing the v1 string while skipping those checks makes schema identity stop implying the checks. Fields: schema, outcome, plan_id, plan_sha256, launchd_label, started/finished_epoch_s, input_digests, measurement_head, chain_sha256, chain_source_sha256, evidence_manifest_sha256, harness_sha256, registration_sha256 (ties to Q1), both interpreter identities, code_digests, `capture_started: false`. Gates unchanged: outcome ok, fresh mtime and `finished_epoch_s` under `--probe-max-age-s 21600`, label match, input digests re-derived at install, equality against the evidence bindings function, non-authorizing.

**Two dispatch points, not one.** 44a cites `night_agent_install.py:750-771` and `run_night.py:3263`; the installer's own `validate_probe_receipt` independently re-derives the ledger and re-runs `probe_bindings` (`:859-860`). **Authoring tool: lead authority to write, ruled refusal set.** `gen_derivation_night.py:475-505` refuses non-derivation classes and any slot count but twelve — calibration pre-registration guards, untouched by a separate tool. The evidence tool must be their mirror: refuse any plan whose chain references `CALIBRATION_LEDGER`/`LEDGER_HEAD_PIN` or the derivation chain, and refuse the derivation class. Without that the repo gains a second authoring path able to emit calibration-shaped nights outside the twelve-slot guard — a pre-registration guard weakened by omission, which `NIGHT_HANDBACK.md:134` puts on this gate.

**Sampler as in-chain recorder: plain code.** `night_quiet_admission.md:20-60` governs the sealed v4 policy block and `cutoff_authority`; a v2 plan has no such block and never calls `is_quiet` (`quiet_admission.py:165-170`). Two conditions: the recorder's own CPU counts in reported observer cost (no exemptions, `:118-130`), and the exclusion rule it feeds is pre-registration content per Q2. If its output ever gates capture, or a number from it is sealed as `busy_core_max`, `:20-27` binds and it returns to this gate.

**Strongest reason 44a is wrong:** "seat work is lead authority once ruled" is too broad in two named places — the frozen exclusion threshold (pre-registration, numerically a cutoff) and the authoring tool's refusal set — and it names the runbook verify-only row as amended when that row is satisfied by a conforming probe while `NIGHT_HANDBACK.md:563-575` is not.
