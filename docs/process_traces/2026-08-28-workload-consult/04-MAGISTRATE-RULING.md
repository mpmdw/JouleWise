## What converged

accepts); replace the CONTENT. The registered quantity is energy per query
at a declared cap; J/token is a derived column; lengths and stop reasons are
published, never divided out. Thinking is pinned OFF in the rendered token
IDs, template hash-bound. Unbounded natural stop and mixed profiles are
`_v6`. GSM8K contamination on Qwen3 is unmitigable: accuracy is a property
of the pinned set, never a capability claim (C5-1.9 under AP-5 quarantine).

analysis-plan amendment, unpriced. The 168-h campaign clock is the scarce
resource; desk-days are not. So the campaign starts on the D-164 date and
the scored leg is BUILT DURING it.
