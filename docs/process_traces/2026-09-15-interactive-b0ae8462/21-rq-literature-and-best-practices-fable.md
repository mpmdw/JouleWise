# RQ literature seat — related work, best practices, question-path review

Fresh Fable seat, 2026-09-16. Every entry was verified by fetching the page or PDF; "not stated" means absent from the fetched text. Brief correction: the brief says `powermetrics` at ~1 s; `docs/paper/draft-v1.md` §A.2 (line 425) commands 100 ms (~10 Hz observed). I use the draft's number.

## 1. Related work — what they measured, how, and at what resolution

**Benchmarks / suites.** MLPerf Power (Tschand et al., HPCA 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12032): external SPEC-PTDaemon/Yokogawa analyzer, 1 s samples, ≥60 s collection, ranging run then fixed ranges, NTP sync; the compliance checker (https://github.com/mlcommons/power-dev/blob/master/compliance/check.py) tolerates 800 ms timestamp skew and rejects analyzer uncertainty >1.00%. No warm-up, ambient, idle-subtraction or repeat-count rule. ML.ENERGY Benchmark (NeurIPS 2025 D&B, https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06371; v3 https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22076): Zeus/NVML, steady-state saturated batching, energy/request = (steady energy/steady tokens)×mean output tokens; cadence not stated; **no repetitions**, no CI. Zeus (You et al., NSDI 2023, https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi23/presentation/you): NVML energy query, no energy CIs. Zeus's Apple backend (https://github.com/ml-energy/zeus-apple-silicon) reads **IOReport "Energy Model" cumulative counters** (CPU/GPU/ANE/DRAM, 1 mJ, arbitrary instants, no sudo), self-described as "model-based estimates"; its RFC (https://github.com/ml-energy/zeus/issues/159) rejected `powermetrics` for fixed-interval reporting. HF AI Energy Score (https://huggingface.github.io/AIEnergyScore/): H100 only, CodeCarbon, 10-run mean, no uncertainty; Optimum-Benchmark: CodeCarbon at 1 s with CUDA-synchronised prefill/decode context managers, no Apple backend. MLPerf Client (https://mlcommons.org/benchmarks/client): power not an official metric. LLMCarbon (ICLR 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14393): pure model, inference validated only against GPT-3's published number.

**Measurement tools and their limits.** CodeCarbon (https://docs.codecarbon.io/latest/explanation/methodology): RAPL/NVML/`powermetrics` (sudo), TDP fallback, default 15 s. Jay et al. (CCGrid 2023, https://doi.org/10.1109/CCGrid57682.2023.00020): slope 1.17–1.18 vs wall meter, not a constant offset. Cao et al. (SustaiNLP 2020, https://aclanthology.org/2020.sustainlp-1.19/): software estimates 20% off a Watts Up meter, 2× the spread. NAACL 2025 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05610): tracker overhead inflates inference time 15–50%. Counter mechanics: Hähnel et al. 2012 (http://www.sigmetrics.org/greenmetrics/2012/papers/Hahnel.pdf) align to RAPL updates; Yang et al. (SC24-W, https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02741): A100/H100 samples cover ~25% of runtime, naive error up to 70%; Dauner et al. (HotCarbon 2026, https://hotcarbon.org/assets/2026/paper-46.pdf): NVML counters update no faster than ~100 ms, 95% underestimate at 0.5 ms polling.

**Phase-level (prefill vs decode).** Splitwise (ISCA 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18677): phases run separately, TDP-normalised power, instrument/cadence not stated, no error bars. POLCA (ASPLOS 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12908): DCGM 100 ms, boundary read visually from the trace, 3 repeats. DistServe: no energy. Illusion of Power Capping (https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11999): NVML 50 ms trapezoid, counter cross-check within 2% for ops ≥200 ms, 3 warm-ups, 10–20 repeats, ±1σ, die temperature logged — the strongest phase methodology, but phases are **separate operations**. TokenPowerBench (AAAI 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03024): samples tagged by active stage, cadence/repeats/CI not stated. Ruf & Detyniecki (HotCarbon 2026, https://hotcarbon.org/assets/2026/paper-17.pdf): NVML 1 ms, prefill isolated by generation length = 1, single run. **Nobody quantifies energy moved by boundary-placement error; Illusion and Ruf avoid the boundary by construction.**

**Consumer / Apple silicon.** Silicon Showdown (https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00519): `powermetrics`, interval not stated, 2 warm-ups, mean of 3, no CI. AgentStop (ACM CAIS '26, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15206): M1 Max, `powermetrics` 100 ms, trapezoid, baseline CPU subtracted, 95% CI — the only Apple study with intervals. GreenBench (ICCUBEA 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28667): M4 Pro, `powermetrics` **every 2 s**, energy = mean power × Ollama latency, one warm-up, no synchronisation statement, no error bars, no meter validation; its 0.47 W package figure beside an 8–12 W system estimate is irreconcilable. Wilkins et al. (e-Energy 2024, https://doi.org/10.1145/3632775.3662830): `powermetrics` daemon at 200 ms, idle subtracted, repeat until runtime within 0.5 s at 95% CI (≤25 trials). Intelligence per Watt (https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07885): `powermetrics` GPU at 50 ms on M4 Max, mean±1σ; denominator is **power**, not energy. **No Apple-silicon study states time anchoring between `powermetrics` and the workload, thermal control, or a resolution bound.**

**MoE / speculative / quantization / KV.** Fernandez et al. (ACL 2025, https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1563/): CodeCarbon-NVML, 3-run means, no CI; OLMoE up to 54% **more** energy than dense OLMo-1B; speculative decoding −29% at batch ≤16, +26% at batch 128. ML.ENERGY v3: Qwen3-30B-A3B 3.56× lower J/token than Qwen3-32B. Arya & Simmhan (PAISE 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09554): INT8 can cost more than FP16. Watt Counts (https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09048): pyNVML 10 Hz validated <1% vs 200 Hz, cool-down to ±3 W/30 s and <65 °C, 5 iterations, 95% CI; KV-head count strongest J/token predictor. No dedicated measured KV-cache energy study found.

## 2. Best practices, item by item

| Practice (source) | JouleWise |
|---|---|
| Analyzer ≤1% uncertainty, calibrated yearly, fixed ranges (SPEC methodology https://www.spec.org/power/docs/SPEC-Power_and_Performance_Methodology.pdf; MLPerf checker) | **Not done**: software counter, no gain calibration; Apple calls the values "estimated and may be inaccurate". `draft-v1.md` §7 admits no wall check. **Weaker**; the field's first question. |
| Clock sync (MLPerf: NTP, 800 ms tolerance) | **Stronger**: in-window pulse fiducials, rate-aware clock model, refusal on active NTP correction (`draft-v1.md` §2, line 49). Sub-100 ms bound vs 800 ms. |
| Sampling cadence vs phase length (Dauner, Yang, Hähnel) | **Partly**: resolvability rule <3 records → "not resolvable" (`draft-v1.md` §1); edge smear characterised by pulses (§A.3). **Not done**: `powermetrics` internal update period / stale reads uncharacterised — Dauner's NVML finding is the analogue. |
| Repetitions ≥30 (Cruz https://luiscruz.github.io/2021/10/10/scientific-guide.html), 10–20 (Illusion), CI stopping rule (Wilkins) | **Differently**: n=10 ABBA blocks in the diagnostic contrast (`CLAIMS_STATUS.md` §2), pre-registered n with one-way prior ratchet; repetition attacks the smaller term by design. Defensible; state why n is fixed rather than adaptive. |
| Warm-up (Cruz 5 min; Illusion 3 iters) | **Done for calibration** (3 warm-up pulses, `draft-v1.md` §A.3.4). Inference warm-up member: I could not verify a stated rule in the draft — make it explicit. |
| Thermal control (SPEC ambient ≥20 °C, ±0.5 °C sensor, 4 samples/min; Watt Counts <65 °C cool-down; Illusion logs die temp) | **Differently**: cooldown gate ≤300 s to ≤1.10× reference power, "nominal thermal pressure", environment snapshots (`draft-v1.md` §3 line 97, §5 line 223). No ambient sensor, no die temperature. **Weaker on record, stronger on gating.** |
| Idle: measure as its own interval, never subtract (SPEC); report gross + marginal (Beginner's guide) | **Done**: gross headline, idle-subtracted labelled secondary, phase energy gross-only (`draft-v1.md` §4 line 113). |
| Counterbalancing / drift (Georges; Mytkowicz) | **Stronger**: ABBA with midpoint evidence and a separate drift allowance (`draft-v1.md` §5). Nobody in §1 does this. |
| Uncertainty reporting | **Stronger**: cell resolution bound + separate directional interval + printed refusal (`draft-v1.md` §4). Only AgentStop, Watt Counts, Bench360, Illusion report any CI. |
| Tracker overhead (Khan; Cao; NAACL 2025) | **Not done**: `powermetrics` sampler overhead at 100 ms not reported. Cheap to add. |
| Pre-registration (Zhuang et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28873) | **Stronger and unique in energy work**: frozen packs, hashes, freeze receipts (`docs/paper/protocol` §P.4). |
| Artifacts (ACM v1.1 via https://sigir.org/general-information/acm-sigir-artifact-badging/; ICPE 2026 AE https://icpe2026.spec.org/tracks-and-submissions/artifact-evaluation-track/: Zenodo DOI, README with time estimates) | **Partly**: code open, evidence archive pending, FLOOR-BIND-01 limits third-party re-derivation (`draft-v1.md` §9). Target Available + Functional; Results Validated cannot be claimed at submission. |

## 3. Improvements to the current path

**Reviewer questions we cannot yet answer.** (1) Gain: how far is `powermetrics` from wall power under this load? Cao/Jay show 17–20% load-dependent gaps for RAPL/NVML; we have no number and `Q6` is cut. (2) Counter mechanics: what is the `powermetrics` update granularity, and do 100 ms interval averages alias (Dauner/Yang analogue)? (3) Why not isolate prefill with generation length = 1 (Ruf, Illusion) and avoid the boundary? The in-request answer (same KV and thermal state, one request) is the paper's point, but the 1-token arm is a cheap falsifier we do not run. (4) Sampler overhead. (5) One unit, one stack, pulse-to-inference transfer untested (stated limitation; Future Work #1 is the right test).

**Duplicates — cite and reposition.** The 1.7B-vs-8B decode contrast: model-size scaling is published on GPUs (ML.ENERGY, Watt Counts) and Apple (Silicon Showdown, GreenBench); keep it as the decision-rule demonstration only, with those citations. C5-1.1: Fernandez (MoE costs more) vs ML.ENERGY v3 (MoE 3.56× cheaper) already conflict on kernels, so stack-conditioned pairwise is the honest framing, as C-014 caps it. C5-1.12: Arya & Simmhan and ML.ENERGY FP8 show non-monotone quantization results; cite, do not re-measure as a headline. C5-2.5: Fernandez already shows the batch-size sign flip.

**Novel.** RQ-ATTRIBUTION-DOMINANCE: no paper quantifies energy moved by phase-edge placement; the closest (Hähnel) is CPU-path scale. RQ-SHORT-PREFILL-RESOLVABILITY as a printed negative with a record-count rule is new for Apple silicon and dovetails with Dauner. The pre-registered floor with refusal semantics is unique here. C5-1.3 (per-phase power asymmetry) is known on GPUs (Splitwise, Illusion) but unmeasured with a calibrated boundary on a unified-memory SoC — future characterisation, not a claim.

**One change that most raises citation-worthiness.** Add an independent-read-path cross-check: read the IOReport "Energy Model" cumulative counters (Zeus's interface; 1 mJ, arbitrary instants, no sudo) at the runtime's own phase edges, in the same window, and compare each phase's counter delta with the `powermetrics` interval integral against the cell floor. This is the "independent counter check" the draft names as its one lack relative to the Illusion paper, it directly tests the ~1 J attribution scale, and it needs no new hardware. Caveat to print: both paths are Apple's model, so it tests read-path and time-base independence, not physics. Run it as a post-`_v5` diagnostic-window arm beside Future Work #1; do not touch the frozen pack. Runner-up: a generation-length-1 prefill-isolation arm (Ruf's design) as a boundary-free falsifier.
