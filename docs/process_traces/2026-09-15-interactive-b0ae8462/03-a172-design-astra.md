**Design decisions recommended for adoption:**

- One JSON policy file, read by tests and rendered into both live documents. No second hand-maintained policy enumeration and no changes to gate/driver refusal registries.
- Exactly four arm-event categories; watchdog uncertainty has two named states in one category. **None of the 22 registered gate/driver reason codes is retryable.**
- The “notice hash” is SHA-256 of the exact prepared `night_plan.json` bytes, accompanied by the reviewed full Git head and the existing input bindings. It is not the mail body digest, the wrapper digest, or the Git head alone.
- A fresh accepted notice precedes every actual retry, even with identical plan bytes. A failed delivery, an uncertain tick, a NO, or a failed final check never authorizes publication.
- Procedural enforcement belongs in the magistrate's runbook, with small executable document guards tested in memory. The watchdog still governs activation eligibility, clock recovery, stop detection and stand-down. The installer still governs job loading and cleanup. The driver never retries a measurement.
- Retry pacing: at most one additional arm attempt per 60 seconds; a finite time budget ending at the original attempt's next listed span close or the plan install cutoff, whichever comes first, additionally subject to plan-age validity. No independent count cap and no delay after a successful window's harvest. R1 below asks the magistrate to adopt these proposed constants, which Ed did not specify.
- A narrowly documented successor-activation handoff is needed. The present relaunch prompt permits alteration of only a plan the current session authored. R2 below must resolve that conflict before cross-activation recovery can ship.

The “arm runbook template” is the live §1.4 foreground block in `docs/phase_2/derivation_night_runbook.md`. Its own text cites historical record 12 as the executed predecessor.
