Terms used in this packet, in plain words. An *envelope* is one 600 s idle capture of the whole machine's power; its *interior* is the 480 s slice beginning 60 s after the envelope's scheduled start, and the *interior joules* are the energy the power sampler integrated over that slice. A *busy core* is one CPU core's worth of non-idle time: a process at 1.0 busy cores kept one core fully occupied for the sampled interval. The *observer* is the night's own machinery (chain, collector, recorder, sampler, census); every other process is *non-observer*. The *census* is a `pgrep` over the words `codex`, `claude`, `t3`: it sees agent sessions and nothing else. The *pilot* is the registered twelve-envelope campaign whose only job is to measure the spread of idle interior joules between adjacent envelopes so that a later experiment (*block two*) can be sized; the *stop branch* "no cutoff qualifies" is the registered conclusion when that spread is too large for block two to resolve a 1 J contrast within 24 pairs. *Rules-before-data* means every exclusion and every sizing rule is fixed in a byte-pinned registration before the night runs; nothing is excluded afterwards by looking at the numbers.
