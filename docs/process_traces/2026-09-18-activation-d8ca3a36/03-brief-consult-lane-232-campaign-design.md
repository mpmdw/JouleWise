## The problem in one paragraph

The merged night gate (PR #358, `docs/contracts/night_quiet_admission.md`) admits an unattended measurement only when the interval-CPU predicate `cpu_interval_v1` (busy CPU seconds per elapsed second, observer included) stays at or below a sealed `busy_core_max` for k consecutive samples inside a bind window. No value may be sealed until a cold gate affirms one; cold judge ruling 70 Q4 (`docs/process_traces/2026-09-17-interactive-5c919872/70-coldgate-packet-quiet-admission/10-coldgate-fable-ruling.md` §6) REFUSED because the packet offered wattage arithmetic, not evidence in the instrument's units, and named its minimum cure (items 1–4: joules per 480 s slot attributable to a stationary synthetic load at the candidate and at 10×, through the actual floor pipeline with ABBA contrast; the sampler's busy-core distribution during accepted-clean captures; the reading under a known contaminant; one named cutoff). The registered lane text (`TASK_QUEUE.md` row A232) instead specifies co-recording `powermetrics` CPU package power and per-cluster active residency beside the predicate across quiet and contaminated states, with the r6 acceptance bundles as the idle reference, the cutoff expressed as the busy-core level whose excess energy per slot stays under the ≈5 J claim bar (≈1 J attribution limit, D-078 clause 11), and no `[QUIET-MAC]` exclusive window consumed. The magistrate synthesis (record 13) corrected the slot budget to 480 s (`scripts/gen_derivation_night.py:88`) and requires the observer cost quoted as the whole-round figure (1.15 cpu-s per 30 s round on this desktop, record 30), not the sampler-body figure.
