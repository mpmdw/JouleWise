## Findings

**R1 — BLOCKER (c). T-3 G3 checks a block against one problem's worst case, so the gate cannot pass.** Quoted: "no block's elapsed seconds exceed its registered worst case (`cap_tokens × s_per_token_upper + prefill_s`)". That formula is the worst case for a single problem. 45/21 §7 (W) reserves a block at "Σ item derived worst cases". Counter-example (V2 §2.6): P3 holds 4 problems at 200 s each and is predicted at 250 s. A normal 250 s run is under its 800 s block worst case, yet fails G3, which also falsifies `s_per_token_upper`, so every repeat fails too. **Replace** with: "no block's elapsed seconds exceed the sum, over its problems, of `cap_tokens[arm] × s_per_token_upper + prefill_s`".

**R2 — MATERIAL (d). T-10's δ_upper is a two-point estimate with no noise allowance; a near-zero value turns off the drift check.** Quoted: "δ_upper is the larger model's |E_last − E_first| ÷ (slot separation)". Executed: with slot separation 20, P = 16 and budget 235.5 J (1 % of 23,552 J), a difference of 0.5 J gives δ = 0.025 and max_gap = 588.8 slots, so every roster passes. A difference of 0 J divides by zero. Both lie far inside the 7.38 J decode floor. **Replace** the δ_upper clause with: "δ_upper = (|E_last − E_first| + floor_gate_j) ÷ (slot separation), using the block-window `floor_gate_j` (T-6)". Executed: max_gap ≈ 37–40 slots, finite and binding.

**R3 — MATERIAL (b). T-10 leaves open which cell `budget_j` comes from and at what n.** Quoted: "0.01 × the smaller of the two models' projected cell energies (… pilot token totals …)". One budget or one per (arm, level)? Are the 16-problem pilot totals scaled to n? Counter-example: one budget of 235 J taken from Level 5, against a Level-1 1.7B cell of 2,000 J, admits a bias of 11.8 % of that cell, not "about one percent". **Replace**: "`budget_j` and `max_gap` are registered per (arm, level): 0.01 × the smaller of the two models' projected energies for that cell at the registered n (pilot tokens per problem × n × shakedown s/token × mean shakedown power)."

**R4 — MATERIAL (b)(d). With T-13's per-night index, K22 can leave a night's offset uncancelled.** Quoted (K22): "both models' missing or displaced parents are re-run"; (T-13) "Envelope indices start at 0 in every night … lever … within each night". Read per model, only the 1.7B re-runs its lost P3 on night 2, so night 2's lever averages over zero 8B parents and is undefined. T-12's single linear function, with indices restarting each night, assumes every night shares one baseline, which the shakedown never measures. If night 2 runs 30 J per block higher, only the 1.7B sum shifts: a 30 J bias δ_upper does not bound. **Replace** K22's run sentence with: "for every affected parent, both models' copies are re-run with the same problems on the recapture night, packed to lever 0 within that night; the recaptured copies supersede both models' earlier windows of that parent. 'Affected' = missing a counted window, or (for `drift_exceeded`) holding a retried or rescheduled window." Add to T-12: "each night has its own intercept; balance within every night is what cancels it."

**R5 — MATERIAL (a). T-8's Holm membership list is incomplete.** Quoted: "no constituent level was forced NR before testing by `spread_exceeded` or `drift_exceeded`". NE(`ceiling_violation`) is assigned at step 12 (T-11), after Holm at step 10. `unattributed_overrun` (45/21 (W)), `night_exhausted` (T-13) and `below_floor` (T-6) also fix a status before testing, and T-8 does not list them. The ruling's Q5 example applies: such a group at p = 0.002 beside 0.012 and 0.014 lets both reject; removed, neither does. **Replace** T-8's second sentence with: "A group is *tested* when it is estimable and no constituent carries `ceiling_violation`, `spread_exceeded`, `drift_exceeded`, `unattributed_overrun`, `night_exhausted` or `below_floor`; these flags are all determined before step 10." In T-11 put NE(`ceiling_violation`) first, then NR reasons in this order: `spread_exceeded`, `drift_exceeded`, `unattributed_overrun`, `night_exhausted`, `below_floor`.

**R6 — MATERIAL (a)(b). T-14 cannot hold for problems left with no counted attempt.** Quoted: "For every problem and model exactly one attempt is *counted*". K17 (ceiling violation), `unattributed_overrun`, `night_exhausted` and a `below_floor` refusal (T-6) each leave a problem with no counted window. Whether its correct flag stays in the denominator is unsaid. Counter-example: with 16 parents per cell, one incomplete parent leaves 15, so `spread_exceeded` never fires. The estimator must then drop the problem (the selection M12 forbids) or keep a correct flag with no energy (J/correct biased low). **Append to T-14**: "A problem with no counted attempt for either model (terminal refusal or `below_floor`) contributes to neither model's energy nor counts; its level carries that reason and is NR under T-11 (NE if `ceiling_violation`) until the K22 or S8 recapture."
