## 1. VERDICT on the question presented: ADOPT (a), with a widened amendment scope and attached conditions

**Serialized `draft_status` fields and README bytes must never transition after a D-134 freeze receipt is minted. The committed receipt plus plan-tree attachment IS the draft→frozen transition. The v1 semantics ratified by the prior cold gate and documented in the runbook apply unchanged, generationally.**

Reasoning, soundness first:

**The circularity is real, structural, and correctly fail-closed.** This is not an implementation accident to be repaired; it is the direct consequence of two soundness features colliding: (i) freeze = a cryptographic pin over the plan bytes, and (ii) status text lives inside the plan bytes. Given both, a post-mint serialized transition *necessarily* falsifies the attestation — and the instrument correctly refuses at every gate. The delta-5 B2 expectation, executed, produces a permanently unarmable pack (verified probe, item 6 above). A "fix" satisfying B2 as specified would require weakening one of the two features: either remove `plan_sha256` from the pin (a material soundness regression — the pin is what makes "frozen" mean something) or build receipt supersession into the freeze path (option (b), rejected in §4). Neither is justified by a byte that no machine gate reads.

**Receipt-over-bytes is the semantically correct freeze model, not a workaround.** "Frozen" is an extrinsic, authenticated property attested over exact committed bytes. Bytes that rewrite themselves *after* attestation are in definitional tension with freezing. The serialized `draft_status` is draft-time descriptive metadata; the authoritative record is the receipt, which every gate authenticates cryptographically. For the paper, this is the stronger story: one uniform semantic across v1/v2/vN (the v1 precedent, packet 06, already accepted and documented), rather than a two-regime system or an attestation-chain epicycle introduced to keep a comment field cosmetically current.

**The operator-ambiguity residual is bounded and mitigated.** The one real cost of (a) is that a human reading a frozen pack sees "unfrozen_draft." The prior cold gate already narrowed M-2 to exactly this human-only ambiguity (no machine gate reads the field — verified). Mitigations now in code: every emission site reads dynamic `target_status` from the authenticated freeze attachment; the fail-closed guard refuses plain regeneration of a frozen identity; the receipt sits in the pack's own namespace. Conditions below add the documentation layer.

**Scope correction to the magistrate's proposal (accepted in substance, widened in coverage).** Packet 01 proposes amending "the consult's D-6 sentence" and quotes "the intentional draft→frozen transition happens once during the governed freeze transaction." That sentence is not in D-6 — it is in the consult's **§B3 ruling paragraph** (packet 02, B3: "...Subsequent preserve checks must reproduce that final frozen snapshot."). D-6 is the companion sentence ("must be governed by the target's authenticated freeze state and finalized within the freeze transaction"). And **R-7** independently commands "a modeled valid fresh freeze attachment produces the governed frozen status at every enumerated status field." Amending D-6 alone leaves the B3 sentence and R-7 still ordering a serialized transition. The amendment must supersede **all three sites**: consult §B3's transition sentences, D-6, and R-7's assertion target. Exact language in §3.

**Delta-5 B2 disposition: SPLIT, not blanket "not-a-defect."** B2 has two components:
- Its **expectation** (serialized bytes must transition) is REFUTED BY EXECUTION — not a defect; ruling on this component reverses the corresponding DO_NOT_MERGE ingredient.
- Its **observation** (the authenticated-freeze regression at `tests/test_d117_decode_contrast_plan.py:855` asserts only dynamic status and never pins the serialized bytes' post-freeze state — verified against the test) remains VALID and must be resolved under (a) by *inverting* the assertion, not deleting it. The round-5 implementer correctly refused to write that test without a ruling; this ruling now orders it (condition C1).

The audit record must show B2 as "expectation refuted / observation resolved by inverted regression," not silently voided.

## 2. Disposition 1 (B1 symlink race → registered residual): RATIFY, with two conditions

The check-then-write race demonstrated by delta-5 V5 requires a concurrent process substituting a validated ancestor inside the window between `validate_generation_write_boundary()` returning and the bytes landing. Delta-5's own residual-risk note concedes its probe was a deterministic in-process instrumentation, not a measurement of a real unsynchronized race. The ratified D-139 A1 threat model ("no adversarial programs affecting the measurement can be assumed") plus single-operator discipline exclude the only actor that can win that race on purpose. The *accidental* class — pre-existing links anywhere in the pack path, spec, sidecar, or ancestors — IS closed, with 16-case refuse-before-any-write coverage verified by delta-5 V4 (zero escape files), and the residual is registered verbatim at all three boundaries (verified alpha :487, beta :2554, gamma :412). Genuine dirfd/`O_NOFOLLOW` hardening in Python generator code is a real complexity spend defending against an actor the instrument's ratified threat model excludes by decision, in a desk-side config generator, not a measurement-time daemon. Registered residual is the sound disposition.

Conditions:
- **C-B1a:** a decision-log row recording the residual, its D-139 A1 basis, the 16-case accepted boundary, and an **explicit reopening trigger**: any revision of the threat model admitting concurrent adversarial local processes, or any move to multi-operator/shared-machine generation. The in-code comments imply this; the log row must state it so a future threat-model amendment mechanically re-opens B1 instead of relying on memory.
- **C-B1b:** delta-4's F2 remedy demand ("reject symlinks... or use dirfd-relative no-follow writes") is formally superseded by this ratification in the audit record — again recorded, not silently dropped.

## 4. Options (b) and (c): REJECTED

**(b) Two-phase freeze with superseding receipts — rejected.** The packet's framing ("schema has `supersedes`; vocabulary has `readiness_receipt_superseded`") materially understates the blast radius: I verified that supersession chain semantics exist **only for arm receipts** (`if kind == "arm"` in `scan_receipt_namespace`); freeze receipts carry `supersedes` as an always-`None` schema field with no chain validation, and `generate_freeze_receipt`/`_load_freeze_reference` have no notion of a freeze chain. Option (b) is therefore *new* attestation-chain machinery in the measurement gate engine — receipt numbering, chain validation, mid-transaction plan-tree re-pinning, latest-selection at three gates — built solely so a descriptive comment field can read nicely. It also breaks the soundness-positive invariant that a freeze happens exactly once (`number=1` is fail-closed simplicity, not a limitation), and it still leaves the v1 packs on single-receipt semantics, producing the two-regime system that (a) avoids. Cost is all soundness-negative surface; benefit is cosmetic. Rejected.

**(c) Transition receipt-unpinned artifacts only (README) — rejected.** Mechanically viable — I verified only the plan is receipt-pinned (the whole-pack digest is computed and discarded at mint) — but it is the worst of the three: it rewrites committed frozen pack bytes, in direct violation of the upheld M-2 core and the runbook's explicit "Do not 'tidy' that serialization"; it creates split-brain descriptive state (README "frozen" beside serialized `unfrozen_draft`), which is *worse* for the human-operator ambiguity M-2 exists to resolve than uniform draft wording under a governing receipt; and it forces the emitted generator's post-freeze `--check` to reproduce state-dependent README bytes, reintroducing exactly the mode/state-keyed emission class this entire five-round campaign just eliminated. Rejected.
