## Q2 (my brief) — is `None` truthful for the r-series?

Yes, and D-125 does **not** bind r3–r6 to n19 — but the ruling's argument is under-evidenced. The load-bearing facts, none cited in the ruling: (i) D-145 (decision_log.md:187) ratifies r3 as issued with **screens TIGHTENED** relative to the n=19 era and states the quantile statistics are "generation-DERIVED … derived from the corpus, never copied between generations"; (ii) the n17 family re-derived under changed estimator bytes (`estimator_code_sha256` differs from n19's), which is the artifacts' own `protocol_or_estimator_byte_change` re-derivation trigger, not the "as more observations arrive" accretion D-125 cl.2 governs; (iii) r6's screen `0.009724` sits **below** D-125's genesis floor `S₀ = 0.010818`, which is only coherent if the r-lineage is not a D-125 envelope successor at all; (iv) the project's own forward position agrees — the prereg names r6's ceiling (`0.010164…`, below n19's) as the inherited ceiling for the next generation. Neither D-138 nor D-145 states an *exemption clause*; the exemption is structural (re-derivation ≠ successor) and should be recorded as such. Registering n19's ceiling as the r-series predecessor would refuse all four r-rows, which is why `None` is the only truthful value.

## Q4 — the isolation rule

**Authority:** within scope. Rule 11 makes "any proposed process rule" a mandatory cold-gate trigger and forbids the lieutenant/magistrate from adopting one alone; the ruling adopts it *as amended, subject to Ed's veto*, which is the correct shape (recommendation, not ratification). **Replicability:** clause (1) is mechanical (delete one term, the named test must fail); clause (2) is mechanical given a fence list; clause (4) is a routing rule and is the genuine lesson of this packet. **Gap in clause (3):** its bound is "the seat's whole sweep" — a quantity the *seat* chooses, so a narrow sweep discharges the rule vacuously; and it carries no cost bound, while "one term per cut" over a large module is unbounded work. Both are fixable by deriving the scope from the diff (see amendment A5). A second, smaller gap: clause (2) says "every existing clause downstream of that fence" without giving a method to enumerate "downstream" in a function full of early returns — clause (3), once diff-bounded, subsumes it, so no separate method is needed if A5 lands.
