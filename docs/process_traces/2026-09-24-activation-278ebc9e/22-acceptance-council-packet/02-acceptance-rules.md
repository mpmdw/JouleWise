# What live-epoch acceptance decides

**Acceptance** here is the issued D-079 artifact that authenticates an identity epoch and sets thresholds for *later* calibration captures. **B** or `b_fiducial_s` is the fiducial timing bound of one 59-pulse capture. **S** is the bracket drift screen; **C** is the maximum budgetable bracket drift; **Q99** is the 99.5% two-draw prediction from retained B values. [configs/calibration/preregistration_d079_epoch_25g83_rev1.md:15-30,83-90]

## Inputs, computations, refusals

- The successor route consumes 25G83 derivation-only ledger observations, their hash-authenticated `manifest.json` and `instrument_evidence.json`, and the stored anchor-v3 result. Valid, resolved registered captures are members regardless of B; valid same-epoch captures outside that registration refuse issuance. It does not consume G2-a, a floor, a claim, or the earlier equivalence-night values. [configs/calibration/preregistration_d079_epoch_25g83_rev1.md:156-171,250-259]
- It computes corpus min/max/range/mean/sample SD and t(0.975, n−1), t(0.995, n−1) two-draw predictions. The preflight level screen is the new maximum rounded to 1e−15 s. The successor **S = max(range rounded to 1e−6 s, 0.010818 s)** and **C = max(predecessor C, new Q99)**; issuance refuses if S ≥ C. The new C is not known until a prospective corpus exists. [configs/calibration/preregistration_d079_epoch_25g83_rev1.md:190-208,232-251]
- The historical r6 comparison used operative level **0.032898493715362 s**, S **0.009724 s**, C **0.010164834757777545 s**, and max excess **0.000440834757777545 s**. These are the 25F84 corpus maximum/range and prediction ceiling after registered rounding, not immutable physical limits. Its successor screen floor **0.010818 s** belongs to the new rule, not historical equivalence. [configs/calibration/preregistration_d079_epoch_25g83_rev1.md:372-419]
- The epoch-equivalence shortcut required m ≥ 6, every B no higher than the historical level, and the night's range no higher than S. n1 had m=4, so INCONCLUSIVE; n2 m=7 failed both comparisons (max B 0.133330958 s, range 0.105080561 s). It issued nothing. The review's simulated false-alarm concern attaches to this shortcut, not the successor's corpus issuance. [configs/calibration/preregistration_d079_epoch_25g83_rev1.md:421-460; docs/process_traces/2026-09-19-activation-d0b83820/01-n2-20260919-harvest-record.md:59-73; docs/process_traces/2026-09-24-activation-278ebc9e/20b-acceptance-critical-path-report.md:80]
- For ordinary captures, the validator authenticates protocol and estimator-code digests, then refuses `acceptance_artifact_epoch_mismatch` when the live six-field epoch is absent from the artifact's judged epochs. Derivation-only sessions bypass the old level screen because no acceptance yet judges 25G83. [scripts/validate_powermetrics_fiducial.py:395-434,2108-2129]
- A later pre/post bracket consumes both endpoint B values. Preflight B above the level refuses `instrument_calibration_mismatch`; protocol/estimator changes or expanded range can make the acceptance stale. Observed drift above C also refuses. Otherwise the operative timing bound is **max(Bpre,Bpost) + max(observed drift,S)**. Missing/mismatched authenticated pre/post attachments refuse claim-bearing whole-window reduction. [joulewise/calibration_bracketing.py:2490-2532,2534-2579; docs/contracts/run_bundle_layout.md:605-639]

## Physical meaning of a deliberate 0.245 s characterization

The published ~1 J attribution example is **33 W × 31 ms ≈ 1.02 J**, with 24.9 ms fiducial plus bundle-local 3.3–6.1 ms and edge span. The floor includes anchor-related false-effect width, and each claim's decision interval separately includes its local anchor shift; the practical ~5 J phase-contrast bar is their combined historical consequence. [docs/decision_log.md:4758-4769,4789-4814]

Changing delivered cadence from about 0.120 to 0.245 s is a factor of about **2.04** in time between samples, but the repo does **not** define B as one sample interval: B is the worst accepted pulse-residual endpoint plus effective anchor bound, and the sample cadence is not an additive term. Therefore neither 1 J nor 5 J scales mechanically by 2.04. [docs/process_traces/2026-09-19-activation-b165c535/03-diagnostic-invalid-captures-astra.md:2 `decomposition`, `reference`]

For scale only, **33 W × 0.041 s ≈ 1.35 J**, **33 W × 0.133 s ≈ 4.39 J**, and **33 W × 0.173 s ≈ 5.71 J** for B magnitudes observed in September. These products are hypothetical one-edge allocations using the historical 33 W phase step, not calculated floors or claim decisions: real reductions integrate actual edge power, local bounds, brackets, and corpus scatter. The 0.133 s and 0.173 s values are visible in retained n2 and n1 evidence. [docs/decision_log.md:4762-4768; docs/process_traces/2026-09-19-activation-d0b83820/01-n2-20260919-harvest-record.md:61-69; docs/process_traces/2026-09-19-activation-b165c535/03-diagnostic-invalid-captures-astra.md:2 `valid_fields_s`; joulewise/detection_floor.py:1041-1095,1132-1152]

Concretely, re-characterization at the observed cadence would freeze the pulse protocol/estimator and enrollment rules before capture; verify 59-pulse detectability, plateau interiors, edge coverage and anchor feasibility at that cadence; collect a valid, resolved prospective 25G83 corpus; derive its new maximum, range, SD and Q99; apply S/C issuance tests; then propagate the resulting B and bracket allowances through energy floors and claim intervals. A pulse-length or margin change changes the protocol/hash and needs its own prospective evidence. [configs/calibration/preregistration_d079_epoch_25g83_rev1.md:143-177,188-259; docs/process_traces/2026-09-19-activation-a743be05/06-consult-instrument-cadence-astra.md:54-62]
