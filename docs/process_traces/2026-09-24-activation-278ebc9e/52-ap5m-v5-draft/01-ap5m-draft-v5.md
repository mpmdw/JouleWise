## 1. Question, scope, and frozen population

At each published MATH author level 1–5 and each registered thinking budget, estimate gross joules per correct answer for pinned Qwen3 1.7B and 8B 4-bit MLX models on the same M3 Max instrument epoch. The independent variable is thinking budget, crossed with model and level. The question is how budget changes the **between-model relative energy efficiency** on this frozen set. Level is the author's pre-existing label, never computed from model success. Accuracy describes performance on this particular set and is not a general capability estimate. No causal difficulty, intelligence-per-joule, routing, model-ranking beyond this pair, other-device, other-weight, or per-problem-energy claim is licensed.

The importer population and eligibility rule from v4 K1 are retained: `openai/prm800k` commit `7ecc7947`, its `test.jsonl` plus `test/`-tagged `train.jsonl`, duplicate-ID pairs excluded, non-single-rational references excluded, and plain-comma references excluded. The v4 eligibility count is 4,040 across levels 1–5. Selection is deterministic, subject-balanced round robin after domain-separated SHA-256 ordering; the pilot and test draws are disjoint. Every retained problem ID appears for both models and every selected budget. The importer's frozen version, problem IDs, individual content SHA-256 hashes, level, subject, scorer ID and selection-domain identifiers are registration inputs. Public files contain IDs and hashes only. The actual question, reference, and worked solution remain in a local custody path bound by those hashes. No examples here reproduce a problem statement.

The exact rational-answer extractor and scorer are pinned before the pilot; malformed or unparseable answers are incorrect. A scorer addition requires a new scorer ID and a new registration before any test outcome is examined. The last parseable final answer inside the answer allowance is the scored answer. The parser must identify the forced-close boundary and allowance span from token and byte positions, not from a post-hoc search of all output.

## 2. Registration, pilot, and budget ladder

The immutable registration packet names the model-weight hashes, MLX/runtime and operating-system identity, instrument and floor manifest, importer and scorer hashes, prompt template, single sampling seed, temperature/top-p/top-k, tokenization and byte encoding, selected problem IDs and hashes, budgets, answer allowance A, pilot feasibility criteria, selected confirmatory budget pair, accuracy n, energy-subsample precision target and draw, byte-match ceiling, trace mismatch action, envelope and retry settings, bootstrap specification, Holm family, floor conversion, and claim renderer. Hash the packet before any test-problem outcome exists. An outcome-dependent top-up or rung change demotes the affected analysis to exploratory under D-062.

The declared ladder is exactly `{0 = thinking off, 1k, 2k, 4k, 8k, 16k}` thinking tokens. A is registered, default 256 answer tokens. Budget b is a force-close threshold on the thinking block; after it, the generator has A answer tokens. A natural stop earlier than b is recorded. At b = 0 the model starts in thinking-off mode. The registered rule selects at most five ladder rungs after a **disjoint 16-problem pilot**. Its allocation across levels is registered before the pilot; v4's 16-per-level expansion is not carried because R-Q3(1) says 16-problem pilot. Selection uses feasibility only: runtime, memory, complete capture, trace reproducibility and precision projections. It never selects on correctness, J/correct, model advantage or a favorable frontier. The feasibility ordering and deterministic tie rule must be frozen before the pilot. All five levels remain in the report. If feasibility cannot support an interpretable selected ladder and a prespecified pair, no confirmatory campaign starts.

The pilot fixes n at **at least 128 distinct test problems per level** outside quiet windows; it may raise n to 200 by a prespecified precision/feasibility rule, never after test outcomes. The old `64 if 128 does not fit` branch is removed. The pilot sizes a seeded random energy subsample per (model, level, budget) to its registered precision target. It may set resource and timeout numbers named in the registration, using pilot data only. Pilot attempts are never test attempts or claim-bearing energy observations. A shakedown of the selected quiet capture shape must pass strict bundle and marker checks; its exact shape awaits the A291 v5 contract and instrument-epoch acceptance.

The confirmatory pair `(b_low, b_high)` is selected from the feasible pilot ladder using a registered, outcome-blind ordering. The pair and direction of subtraction are frozen before test problems run. The pair is not selected by which comparison looks strongest. The exact log-contrast interpretation in §6 is a proposed operationalization of R-Q3(7) for E2 council review; the council must settle it before adoption.

## 3. Decoding, nesting, and scoring

All MATH cells use seeded sampling with **one pinned seed** and pinned sampling parameters. Repeated attempts must reproduce token IDs and decoded bytes on the same frozen stack. The planned nested design generates one longest-budget thinking trace per (problem, model, seed); lower-budget traces use its prefix through the budget threshold, a forced-close marker, and a separately generated answer continuation inside A. The pilot checks that each budget's prefix is **byte-identical** to the corresponding longest-run prefix after a fresh generation. Token-ID equality is audited too. The pilot reports forced-close accuracy against natural-stop accuracy at matched length as a distortion diagnostic, without selecting budgets on that accuracy.

If any required prefix is not byte-identical, the nested execution path is dropped and **each selected budget is generated separately** under the same pinned sampling configuration. That fallback is registered before test runs; it does not permit a new seed, a changed ladder, or a selected result. A pilot failure to reproduce even separate traces leaves trace matching OPEN and blocks headline energy for affected cells.

The cap has two distinct events. A **thinking-cap hit** means the thinking block reached b and was force-closed; this is an intended intervention, not automatically an incorrect answer. An **answer-allowance hit** means A ended without a parseable final answer, which is incorrect. An attempt is correct exactly when the pinned scorer accepts a parseable final answer that appears inside A, whether thinking ended naturally or was force-closed. A parseable but wrong answer is incorrect. Missing, malformed, and out-of-allowance answers are incorrect. Per cell report thinking-cap hits, answer-allowance hits, natural stops, malformed answers, correctness, and stop reasons. The v4 rule `capped = incorrect even when an answer parses` is superseded for AP-5M v5. Runtime or wall-clock ceiling violations are distinct capture refusals and retain typed evidence.

Accuracy runs occur outside quiet windows, on the frozen n problems per level and both models at every selected budget. Record complete generated token IDs and bytes, thinking/answer boundary, token and per-token stamps, stop reason, scorer output and hashes. A trace used for the headline has one counted accuracy outcome per (problem, model, budget). Superseded or retried traces remain recorded with their status. Any problem lost from a paired comparison is reported with its cause; it is never silently removed because it was long or incorrect.

## 4. Quiet energy subsample and measurement

For each (model, level, budget), draw a random subset of the frozen accuracy problems with the pinned selection seed and an independent domain string; the draw size follows the pilot precision target and is frozen before test outcomes. For a paired between-model comparison, the proposal uses the same selected IDs across the two models for a given level and budget. Quiet runs regenerate those items inside census-clean measurement envelopes, with per-token stamps and the same prompt, seed, runtime and settings. The complete scored trace, including the forced-close marker and answer continuation, must **byte-match** its accuracy-run trace. Match token IDs as a diagnostic. Record every mismatch and compare the count or fraction with the **registered ceiling**; any exceeded ceiling withholds that cell's headline rather than substituting a different trace. The ceiling value and mismatch disposition are OPEN design inputs for E2/A291 v5, not invented here.

Energy uses strict-valid raw bundles, macOS `powermetrics` summed CPU, GPU and ANE rails at the registered cadence, and gross joules between the measured block's outer item edges. No idle subtraction or per-token-energy prediction enters the headline. If several items share a measured block, gross block joules are summed over homogeneous (model, level, budget) blocks and divided by the number of counted subsample attempts; no per-problem joules are inferred from a multi-item block. The sampling estimator and bootstrap account for envelope and parent-block clustering. Idle slots are recorded and reported as context, never a numerator. Each cell must have at least five independent parent blocks in at least five envelopes, as v4's rung-L2 spread minimum, subject to redesign if the A291 v5 contract makes the new cells incompatible.

Each counted block window must clear the accepted block-window `floor_gate_j = max(floor_abs_j, floor_cmp_j)` for its backend, condition family and OS/sampler epoch. It is a window-admission floor, distinct from the **estimate-level** O-21 floor F. A below-floor window is refused with a typed cause, and the corresponding item is handled symmetrically in the paired comparison. The clock-anchor bound is recorded per window and widens the claim interval; the floor is never inserted a second time into that interval. **25G83 acceptance is OPEN:** this instrument epoch needs an accepted calibration/floor manifest for the AP-5M block-window class before any claim-bearing capture. No historical phase-floor figure is used as its substitute.

The v4 scheduler's safeguards remain design requirements for the adapted contract: fixed envelopes and capacity, one loaded model per envelope, parent identity across split retries, typed `completed`/`cut_off`/`not_started` observations, monotone within-night retries, bounded single-problem retries, terminal `ceiling_violation`, `unattributed_overrun` and `night_exhausted`, no silently dropped tail, five-parent/five-envelope spread, balance of model positions, and one bounded registered recapture for named defects. The former per-arm and per-level formulas, cap-derived worst case, and status effects cannot simply be reused for (model, level, **budget**) cells. **A291 contract v5 is OPEN** and must specify their exact mapping, seal invariants, reducer fields and recapture edges before a campaign is scheduled. No v4 roster or `claims.py` verdict is grandfathered into v5.

## 5. Estimands and descriptive display

For model `m`, level `L`, budget `b`, let `p_mLb` be correctness among all n frozen accuracy problems; let `e_mLb` be **mean directly measured gross J/attempt** over the registered, byte-matched quiet subsample. The cell estimand is `C_mLb = e_mLb / p_mLb` J/correct. It is undefined when `p_mLb = 0`; no finite ratio or directional claim is printed. `R_L(b) = C_8B,L,b / C_1.7B,L,b` compares models on the same level and budget. Both `C` and `R` are labelled for this frozen set, stack and budget. A finite R below 1 favors 8B in J/correct; above 1 favors 1.7B. Publish the numerator `e`, denominator `p`, n, energy-subsample size, gross block energy, cap-hit counts, mismatch counts and paired-bootstrap bands beside each C/R.

Report mean emitted tokens/attempt and descriptive J/token only for context; the algebraic v4 identity using a full-cell energy sum is **not** applied to a separately sampled energy numerator. A registered token-based energy law may be fitted as a secondary analysis, with a held-out tolerance fixed before outcomes, but it never replaces direct energy in `e`, C or R. The budget frontier displays C, R, accuracy, energy and uncertainty at every selected b, with the feasible-rung selection disclosed. Any minimum over b is descriptive with bootstrap bands and cannot be called an optimal budget.

Uncertainty uses a registered paired bootstrap (v4 used 20,000 replicates). Resample problem IDs jointly across models and selected budgets for accuracy, and resample the matched energy subset in parent/envelope clusters while preserving its selection design and the pairing. Recompute `e/p`, R and the confirmatory contrast in each replicate. Widen the energy side of the interval by the recorded clock-anchor bounds and report point bounds separately. Undefined denominator replicates are counted and handled by a preregistered rule, not discarded for convenience. The exact nesting of problem and energy draws, percentile convention, anchor propagation for this split sample, and power validation are **OPEN claim-gate redesign details**; a tested v5 estimator is required before admission.

## 6. Confirmatory decision and claim ceiling

There is **one hypothesis per MATH level**, five total; Holm family size is fixed at `m = 5`, two-sided α = 0.05. The proposed budget-pair contrast is `Δ_L = log R_L(b_high) − log R_L(b_low)` with the pair and sign convention frozen in §2. The null is `Δ_L = 0`; a supported sign says how the relative between-model J/correct ratio changed between those **two registered budgets**. No test searches budgets for the largest effect. The E2 council must rule whether this exact Δ is the intended operational reading of R-Q3(7); until it does, no confirmatory sign is admitted. Descriptive per-budget R signs do not become additional hypotheses.

The candidate claim passes only if the redesigned, validated claim gate yields all of: (1) Holm rejection for its level, with the five-level family preserved even when a level is non-estimable; (2) the **anchor-widened** confidence interval on the same signed J/correct contrast excludes zero in the claimed direction; and (3) its absolute J/correct difference exceeds the corresponding accepted instrument floor `F` (`|estimate| > F`, strict inequality). The ratio/log contrast and the J/correct-difference floor live on different scales, so the registration must specify the exact budget-pair difference to which F applies and a traceable scale conversion or matched direct difference. That mapping is an **OPEN claim-gate redesign question** for the E2 council. There is no claim if F is missing, stale for 25G83, or not applicable to the estimate class. Holm, interval and floor are three separate checks; neither floor nor anchor is silently deleted as a supposed duplicate.

Report `direction supported`, `not resolved`, `not estimable`, and named refusal/quality flags as appropriate. A direction cannot arise from a refused trace, below-floor window, byte-match ceiling breach, missing denominator, inadequate energy spread, or unbalanced capture. Sparse levels are reported as such. V4's fixed sparse-level merge and crossover decision table cannot be used automatically: merging changes the five predeclared level hypotheses. Any pooling sensitivity is descriptive until the E2 council approves a five-hypothesis rule that preserves family membership. Claim language names the frozen subset, pair of budgets, both models, direct-measurement boundary, uncertainty and cap semantics. No `optimal budget`, general capability, causal difficulty, or policy recommendation is authorized.

## 7. Votes and later experiments

A1 votes are **deferred** to a separate later registration. Before that, a batch memory/power pilot at `k ∈ {1, 4}` assesses feasibility and resources. Vote count, vote aggregation, routing and their energy are absent from every AP-5M v5 estimand or confirmatory family. Neither pilot votes nor the later A1 registration alter this frozen budget study retroactively.

## 8. Proposed D-166 dated addendum, v5

MATH author levels may stratify direct-measured J/correct on the frozen subset. The variable is thinking budget b, crossed with model and level; correctness is a quarantined annotation for the denominator and never a capability claim. Gross rail energy is measured inside quiet envelopes on the byte-matched subsample; accuracy uses the larger outside-quiet cohort. J/token and tokens/attempt are descriptive. The GSM8K manifest and AP-5 synthetic ladder retain their own contracts. An AP-5M headline requires its own adopted registration, accepted 25G83 calibration/floor row, amended A291 contract, validated claim gate and the §6 tests. The v4 addendum's thinking-on primary family, 64-problem branch, capped-is-incorrect rule, crossover L* and pooled-region language are superseded. This addendum is only proposed for E2 council adoption.
