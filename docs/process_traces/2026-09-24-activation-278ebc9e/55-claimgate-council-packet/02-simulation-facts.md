# Desk simulation facts

Here **σ** is one block's local-error standard deviation, **δ** is the true contrast, **w** is an allowed per-block timing half-width, and a **component** is one of twelve contrasts sharing a simulated night shock. Four models were run: Gaussian local error, variance-one Student t with three degrees of freedom, centered linear drift, and shared-plus-local stochastic/timing effects (`docs/process_traces/2026-09-24-activation-278ebc9e/19-desk-simulations/README.md:3-5,13-17`; `docs/process_traces/2026-09-24-activation-278ebc9e/19-desk-simulations/sim.py:43-65`).

The floor/claim grid has 144 cells: four models × calibration n 5/8 × analysis n 10/30 × δ/σ 0/2/5 × w/σ 0.5/1/2, with 30 nights and twelve components per cell. The equivalence grid has 768 cells, 600 nights each (`docs/process_traces/2026-09-24-activation-278ebc9e/19-desk-simulations/README.md:17,21-23`; `docs/process_traces/2026-09-24-activation-278ebc9e/19-desk-simulations/results/metadata.json:13-20`). The replay calls production floor, paired estimator, Holm adjustment, `evaluate_claim`, and the old equivalence numerical verdict; synthetic retained rows bypass ledger authentication (`docs/process_traces/2026-09-24-activation-278ebc9e/19-desk-simulations/sim.py:27-35,76-99,171-184`).
