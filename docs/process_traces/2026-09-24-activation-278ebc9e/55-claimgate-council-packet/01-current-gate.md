# Current production decisions

## Terms and inputs

An analysis **block** is one paired A/B contrast; the paired estimator reports mean B−A across blocks, standard error, a 95% metrology interval, and that interval widened by deterministic bounds (`joulewise/analysis_engine/estimators.py:450-509`). **F** below is the resolved `floor_gate_j`, the larger applicable absolute or comparative component, not the smaller point-floor diagnostic (`docs/phase_2/detection_floor.md:64-84`). A **frozen family** is the prespecified set of contrast IDs whose multiplicity denominator cannot shrink for missing hypotheses; Holm is the stepwise adjustment used for small confirmatory families (`docs/contracts/analysis_plans.md:48-58`; `joulewise/analysis_engine/multiplicity.py:49-77`). TOST means two one-sided tests for a prespecified equivalence margin (`joulewise/analysis_engine/estimators.py:251-279`).

1. The caller supplies estimate, metrology-aware 95% interval, expanded decision interval, F, adjusted rejection, optional equivalence specification, reason codes, role/status, and sensitivity flags. The evaluator validates finite numbers and ordered intervals; missing estimate or interval becomes `not_estimable`, missing floor becomes `not_resolvable`, and negative floor is invalid (`joulewise/analysis_engine/claims.py:237-286,325-341`).
2. If supplied, floor metadata must match the attribution-limited source and single-count discipline or it adds `floor_artifact_invalid`. Preexisting reason codes take ordered precedence: `not_estimable`, then `not_resolvable`, then `unresolved`, then sensitivity codes (`joulewise/analysis_engine/claims.py:143-217,288-323,333-341`).
3. For a directional contrast, `abs(estimate) <= F` adds `effect_not_above_floor`, which is a `not_resolvable` reason. This is a strict **point-estimate** test; no confidence endpoint is compared with F (`joulewise/analysis_engine/claims.py:183-196,343-355`).
4. Without an overriding reason, equivalence accepts only positive finite margin, method `tost_v1`, and margin strictly above F. It then requires both supplied intervals strictly inside (−margin,+margin) and `adjusted_rejected`; failure is `unresolved`, success `equivalent` (`joulewise/analysis_engine/claims.py:254-255,346-368`). Upstream TOST returns the larger of two one-sided p-values; the caller supplies the adjusted decision (`joulewise/analysis_engine/estimators.py:251-279`; `joulewise/analysis_engine/claims.py:274-279`).
5. For direction, both intervals must exclude zero. If only the expanded interval includes zero, the outcome is `not_resolvable`; if the metrology interval includes zero, it is `unresolved`. Otherwise failure of adjusted rejection is `unresolved`; passing yields `direction_supported` and the estimate's sign (`joulewise/analysis_engine/claims.py:369-386`).
6. A supported outcome is claim-ready for L2/L3 only for registered primary/secondary roles, confirmatory and nonlegacy evidence, no blocking sensitivity, no specified adverse sensitivity reason, and agreement with a registered sign when one exists (`joulewise/analysis_engine/claims.py:388-420`). L2/L3 are comparative/model-fit claim levels in the claims ladder (`docs/contracts/claims_ladder.md:63-64`).

## Constants and authority

- The floor formula uses `max(maximum absolute observed deviation, |mean| + t_0.975,n−1 × s × sqrt(1+1/n))` for comparative blocks; the `sqrt(1+1/n)` term predicts a future block. It comes from D-054's P2-015 floor design (`docs/decision_log.md:3018-3037`; `joulewise/detection_floor.py:871-901,1132-1152`; `docs/phase_2/detection_floor.md:96-113`). The small-sample guard is `sqrt(9/(n−1))` for 5≤n<10, 1 from n=10; n<5 is smoke only (`joulewise/detection_floor.py:121-122,850-854`; `docs/phase_2/detection_floor.md:111-113`). The nominal 95% t bound is in the floor and paired estimator (`joulewise/detection_floor.py:878-879`; `joulewise/analysis_engine/estimators.py:466-486`).
- F is `max(floor_abs_j,floor_cmp_j)` for the same metric/window class, per P2-015; D-078 clause 11 keeps the widened floor and separately widened claim interval; the D-083 addendum rejects an extra additive F+B acceptance test (`docs/contracts/analysis_plans.md:24-30`; `docs/decision_log.md:4803-4814,11275-11303`).
- `tost_v1` is the accepted method string; a positive margin above F is required, but no universal numerical margin is hard-coded (`joulewise/analysis_engine/claims.py:346-352`; `joulewise/analysis_engine/artifact.py:1802-1813`). The AP-4 analysis-plan example fixes a 2%-of-request-energy margin for its own category-residual family, under Holm, not for every equivalence claim (`docs/contracts/analysis_plans.md:236-251`). Holm's denominator `m` comes from the frozen family under D-038; the code retains missing hypotheses as `None` (`docs/decision_log.md:2201-2220`; `docs/contracts/analysis_plans.md:48-58`; `joulewise/analysis_engine/multiplicity.py:49-77`). The simulated `4σ` margin and unadjusted 0.05 are diagnostic choices, not production constants (`docs/process_traces/2026-09-24-activation-278ebc9e/19-desk-simulations/sim.py:84-99`; `docs/process_traces/2026-09-24-activation-278ebc9e/19-desk-simulations/README.md:9`).
- Zero is the directional null and interval crossing value; its use is visible in the evaluator. D-057 makes reason codes stable vocabulary, and D-062 freezes confirmatory n and demotes outcome-dependent top-ups (`joulewise/analysis_engine/claims.py:369-382`; `docs/decision_log.md:3111-3135,3367-3391`).
- The separate epoch-equivalence tool uses a minimum six retained valid/resolved captures, then passes only if the new maximum is no higher than the old operative level screen and the new range no wider than the old operative bracket screen. Its comparison shape and six-capture minimum come from Ed's pre-capture directive issue 316; the operative screens come from the D-079/D-102 acceptance artifact, not raw corpus statistics (`scripts/epoch_equivalence_check.py:1-27,33-57,125-127,460-536`; `docs/decision_log.md:6453-6485`).
