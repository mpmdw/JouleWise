Deciding evidence. The seats agree budget is the independent variable and votes are later. They differ on (i) whether energy is measured on every counted trace (Fable) or on a quiet subsample with accuracy at large n outside quiet windows (Opus); (ii) the confirmatory test (Fable: pre-registered budget pair; Opus: min-over-b with bootstrap re-minimisation); (iii) cap semantics (Sol and Astra: forced-close answers need an explicit scoring rule; Astra cites the AP-5M rule that capped attempts score incorrect, which I could not read). Final text R-Q3. (1) Independent variable: thinking budget b, defined as the number of thinking tokens after which the thinking block is force-closed, followed by an answer allowance of A tokens (A registered, default 256). Declared ladder {0 = thinking off, 1k, 2k, 4k, 8k, 16k}; the disjoint 16-problem pilot selects at most five budgets, on feasibility only, never on outcomes. Crossed with model {1.7B, 8B} and MATH level {1..5}, same problems in every cell. (2) Decoding: seeded sampling with one pinned seed for every MATH arm (M4). Budgets are obtained by nesting: one generation per (problem, model, seed) to the largest budget, lower budgets by truncation plus their own forced-close continuation. The pilot must show byte-identical prefixes across budgets and must report forced-close accuracy against natural-stop accuracy at matched length; if prefixes are not byte-identical, nesting is dropped and each budget is generated separately. (3) Scoring: an attempt scores correct only if a parseable final answer appears inside the allowance A; the AP-5M cap-hit rule is rewritten to say exactly this, and cap-hit counts are reported per cell. (4) Accuracy runs: outside quiet windows, n ≥ 128 problems per level (pilot may raise to 200), recording tokens and per-token stamps. (5) Energy: a seeded random subsample per (model, level, budget), size set by the pilot's precision target, generated again inside quiet envelopes with per-token stamps; each subsample trace must byte-match the accuracy-run trace, mismatches counted against a registered ceiling. Headline J/attempt uses only these direct measurements; a token-based energy law is a registered secondary with a stated tolerance and is never substituted into the headline. (6) Estimand per cell: J/correct = mean J/attempt ÷ accuracy, reported with accuracy, J/attempt, cap hits and paired-bootstrap bands. (7) Confirmatory: one hypothesis per level, Holm m = 5, on the sign of log R_L at a budget pair pre-registered from the pilot ladder; the frontier over b and any min-over-b comparison are descriptive with bootstrap bands, and no "optimal budget" claim is made. (8) A1 votes: a separate later registration, preceded by a batch memory/power pilot at k ∈ {1, 4}. (9) Sequence: AP-5M v4 → v5 and A291 contract v4 → v5 are amended to this text; the E2 adoption council runs on v5; E3 is fixed by (2); E4's n rule by (4)–(5).
