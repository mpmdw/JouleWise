## Registered reports and tests

1. For each model and L, headline table: six envelope-level `P̄_decode(L)` values **per instrument**, arithmetic mean and two-sided 95% Student-t interval with df=5. Do the same for prefill and sustained-prefill phases, clearly labelled. A missing/excluded segment yields its actual n; do not present n<6 as a six-replicate interval. The 512-token decode segment's **joules per segment** is the claim unit, with both instruments and uncertainty. Tokens within a segment are not independent replicates. `J/token(L)=P̄_decode(L)×s/token(L)` is a segment mean and **descriptive**, never a per-token joule claim (`docs/research_question_registry.md:55`).
2. Bytes-law diagnostic: the **ruled equation** is `J/token_m(L)/J/token_m(512)=1+κ·KV_m(L)/W_m`, with one shared κ across both models and reported model/rung residuals. Council G1 gives `KV_m(L)=L×{112,144} KiB` for 1.7B/8B against approximate `W_m={0.94,4.3} GB`. At **L=512**, its left side is identically 1 but the right side is `1+κ·KV_m(512)/W_m`; the equation therefore forces κ=0 if the baseline point is treated as an exact fitted observation. **NEEDS_RULING before seal:** either exclude L=512 from fitting while retaining the ruled formula for L>512, or amend the formula to use `KV(L)−KV(512)` (or another council-approved normalization). The drafting seat does not select either. After that ruling, fit one shared κ, state byte units and fit weights, report residuals and held-out-by-envelope error; do not interpret the fit as causal proof. If a rung was dropped, name the estimable domain.
3. **PROPOSED stopwatch-equivalent band:** for each model and instrument, all rung means of `P̄_decode(L)−P̄_decode(512)` must have their paired 95% t intervals (df=5) entirely within **±0.10 W**. This is an equivalence rule, not a failure-to-reject test. At up to 50 s per 512-token segment it bounds a position-linked energy difference by ≤5 J, the approximately 5 J effective contrast scale recorded in `docs/process_traces/2026-09-24-activation-278ebc9e/08-council-407-seat-astra.md:69`; the smoke must show a ≤50 s worst-case decode for that physical justification. If it does not, the cold gate must choose a new numeric band **before seal**. Report the full power differences and intervals whether or not equivalence passes; make no global equivalence claim from one model or one instrument.
4. Secondary held-out predictor: fit energy from `{model, input tokens, output tokens, phase}` only, with six-fold leave-one-envelope-per-model-out folds (one 1.7B and one 8B envelope withheld per fold); train preprocessing only on training folds. Compare predicted vs directly measured 512-segment joules, report MAE, median absolute error, signed bias and per-model errors. This never replaces direct measurement in the headline.
