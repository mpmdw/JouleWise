## Replication verdicts

**1. Dominance-ratio rule (lines 77–94) — FAILS the replication bar.**

A reader can restate the *comparison* — timing-aware floor ÷ naive floor ≥ 2, required for every component in every registered cell, with a separate shared-shift check that must also reach 2 — but cannot compute either input, and therefore cannot apply the rule to numbers.

Three gaps, in order of cost:

- The numerator is undefined as a procedure. Line 83–84: *"the timing-aware floor, which includes the worst admitted energy change caused by uncertainty in where the workload begins and ends."* "Includes" does not say whether the term is added to, maximised against, or substituted for the naive floor; "worst admitted" does not say the worst is enumerated over boundary-shift combinations rather than observed.
- The denominator is undefined. Line 81–82: *"the naive floor, which uses the observed run-to-run residuals without moving the measurement boundaries through their timing uncertainty."* "Residuals" — from what? A per-cell mean, a fit, a paired difference? No formula, no scale factor, no sample-size dependence.
- No worked example with real numbers anywhere, and no count of the objects the "every component in every registered cell" quantifier ranges over (the repository fixes this at exactly eight ordinary ratios and four common-mode ratios, `docs/decision_log.md:195`). A reader cannot tell whether "every" means 4 tests or 40.

Additionally, line 90–91 — *"A shared-shift replay also asks what happens when the same timing displacement is applied to all paired blocks; if its comparative ratio is below two, the sentence is withdrawn"* — carries three unbuilt terms at once (replay, blocks, "its comparative ratio", where *comparative* has already been given a different meaning at line 22–23). The reader cannot tell whether "replay" re-runs the experiment or recomputes from stored inputs. It is the latter.

**2. Prefill-length selection rule (lines 96–103) — PASSES as a decision procedure, FAILS as physics.**

Given a table of per-run overlap counts, a reader *can* execute this rule correctly: walk the four lengths in ascending order, keep the first at which every small-model run has ≥5 overlapping power records, else collect at 4,096 with a refusal. That matches `scripts/select_g2a_prefill_length.py:98–120` exactly. This rule is well written.

Two defects remain:

- **Under-specified branch.** Line 99–101: *"A checked program then selects the shortest length for which every small-model run contains at least five power records overlapping prefill."* Silent on a rung with fewer than five small-model runs. The code treats such a rung as unevaluable and unselectable (`MIN_SMALL_MEMBERS = 5`, line 18, enforced at line 105), which is a different outcome from "failed the count test". The document states the ≥5-runs requirement at line 98 as a *collection* instruction and never re-states it as a *selection precondition*.
- **The number 5 is unexplained.** The reader is never told what a "power record" physically is (one sampling interval of `powermetrics`), how long one lasts, or why five is the bar. The repository's actual justification is that the reducer's physical floor is 3 samples and the +2 is a declared pre-registration safety factor (`docs/decision_log.md:193`; `PREREGISTERED_MIN_PHASE_SAMPLES = 3` at `select_g2a_prefill_length.py:23`). Without that, a metrology reader cannot judge whether the rule is conservative or arbitrary — which is precisely the judgement this advisor will make.

# VERDICT: **SHOULD-FIX**

Every load-bearing fact I checked is corroborated against the repository — the model pair, the ratio gate, the neither-branch rule, the four-rung selector down to the constants in `select_g2a_prefill_length.py`, and the full chain order against the reconciled kernel — and the document is admirably free of orchestration shorthand; but it fails Ed's binding first-use test in four places that carry the science (A1 registered semantics, A2 "worst admitted energy change", A3 *rung*, A4 *block*), the headline dominance-ratio rule is not restatable from the text alone, and the ≈1 J attribution paragraph both narrows the repeatability range to its optimistic edge and drops the ≈5 J effective-bar disclosure that D-078 clause 11 makes mandatory wherever an attribution-limited floor is published.
