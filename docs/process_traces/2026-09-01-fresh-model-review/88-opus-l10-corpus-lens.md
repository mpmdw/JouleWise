# Reversal or parameter fix?

**Parameter fix.** D-160 R-1 forbids one thing — a *synthetic* clean leg standing in for the real edge — and the recommendation introduces no synthetic leg; the G2-b corpus is real telemetry from the real `_v5` pack, which is precisely the class R-1 demanded and F-1/F-2/F-3 proved synthetic evidence can never join. D-160 R-2 is not reversed here either, because it was already superseded eight days ago by D-162 R-1, which ruled the diagnostic family "NOT built pre-campaign" and re-homed it to PIPELINE-SMOKE-TIER2-01; G2 on the real pack is R-2's ruled successor, and adopting the successor's corpus is following D-160's own lineage forward, not overturning it. The two clauses that genuinely move — "any window" → "any claim-bearing window", and D-167's "never consumed by a floor, mint, or claim" → "never consumed by a claim-bearing floor, mint, or claim" — are both already implied by ruled text (D-167(1) records Ed's 08-28 "diagnostic windows at lead discretion; the transaction on Ed's go"; V5-TRANSACTION-01's own fence spells the purpose as "no diagnostic byte is **promoted into the transaction**", which a quarantined qualification read does not do). Magistrate authority suffices; no cold gate.

**Strongest counter-argument against my own verdict:** D-167's fence is a *soundness* fence, and D-167 closes by declaring "Soundness fences unchanged: pre-registration, evidence immutability, D-078 no-retry, D-149 T-0 conditions" — evidence immutability named explicitly. Rule 11 makes "any reinterpretation of a stop signal or verdict" a mandatory cold-gate trigger, and reading "never consumed by a floor, mint, or claim" as "never consumed by a *claim-bearing* floor, mint, or claim" is exactly a reinterpretation of a fence whose whole function is to stop a diagnostic byte from being laundered. The memory note `ruled-not-installed-pattern` records the failure mode where rulings with implementation clauses never reached code; the mirror-image failure — a fence narrowed in a lane doc so the lane can proceed — is the one this consult is at risk of committing. I hold the verdict anyway, on one discriminator: the amendment **adds** a refusal surface rather than removing one (outputs must carry the label, must live below `$L10_CUSTODY_ROOT`, must never enter `$ANALYSIS_ROOT`/`$CLAIMS_ROOT`, finalization must use a scratch copy), and the reads themselves are byte-non-mutating on merged code (`cli.py:2003-2019`), so the immutability the fence protects is preserved in fact and not merely in intent. But the amendment must be **written into the kernel fence text**, not applied as a purposive reading — a silent purposive reading of that fence *would* be the reversal.
