1. **STAGE MAP.**  
   **Generator:** `generate_configs.py:main/generate/_generate` constructs the generation, configs, ordering, and prospective analysis manifest; `check` proves reproducible bytes, but neither calls the v3 prospective validator nor proves claim consumability.  
   **Mint:** the same generator serializes the versioned pack and inventories its hashes; it proves identity and byte closure, not semantic admissibility.  
   **Freeze:** `scripts/generate_arm_readiness.py freeze` → `arm_readiness.generate_freeze_receipt` authenticates committed pack bytes, lineage, registry, evidence, and repository state. Today it does not call `validate_prospective_analysis_manifest_v3`: the D-157 hole.  
   **Arm:** `generate_arm_readiness.py dry-run/arm` verifies freeze, reservations, evidence, T-0 roots, and machine/repository gates, but assumes the frozen analysis contract is consumable.  
   **T-0/launch:** `capture_t0_step.py`, `author_arm_evidence_t0.py`, then `launch_window.py:_assemble_launch_inputs/_consume_launch_capability` validate governed receipts, launch manifest, capability, argv, and custody. They do not validate statistical semantics; the runbook currently omits required confirmation-table/digest arguments.  
   **Collection:** `run_campaign.py:run_campaign` validates order/configs, child execution, bundles, calibration custody, and whole-window verdict. Its `load_analysis_manifest` uses a generic validator, not prospective-v3 admission.  
   **Finalization:** `finalize_analysis_manifest.py` → `finalize_prospective_analysis_manifest_v3` authenticates runs, verdict, bracket binding, ledger, floor artifact, and immutable family copying. It has no lifecycle caller outside tests/script: an orphan-stage hole.  
   **Claim edge/verdict:** `python -m joulewise.cli analyze-claims` → `analysis_engine.analyze_claims`, `artifact.py`, and `multiplicity.adjust_p_values` consume finalized inputs, enforce `len(p_values)==m`, adjust families, and emit validated `claim_verdicts`. This is currently the first guaranteed end-to-end semantic encounter.

Any exception or any other reason—especially `missing`, `invalid`, `mismatch`, incomplete blocks/fixed-N, absent floor/verdict/bundle, or unfrozen manifest—is a pipeline-smoke failure.
