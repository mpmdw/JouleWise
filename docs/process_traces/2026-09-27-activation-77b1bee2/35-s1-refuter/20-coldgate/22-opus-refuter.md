### SHOULD-FIX

**SF-1. Amendment 77 (a) form 1 is not "can only stop execution" as claimed.** Form 1 includes "the statements that build that call's argument from paths and labels". An argument-building statement decides *which* members the gate sees; a changed filter there can drop a member from the gate and so open an ungated route, while being classified safe. The candidate holds a live example: `run_campaign.py:9020-9031` builds `battery_members` with the filter `if (evaluation.bundle_path.is_dir() or evaluation.status is not None or evaluation.summary is not None or evaluation.metadata is not None)`; an edit narrowing that condition (for example to `evaluation.status == "succeeded"`) would be form 1 under 77 (a) yet exempt members from the window gate. The ruling's safety sentence ("Neither binds, passes, returns or stores the value") is true of the gate call and of the handler, not of member selection. Fix: form 1 = the gate call itself and the storing of its verdict statuses; argument-building lines count as on the route unless the return shows (by execution) that the argument covers every member the function later consumes. Does not affect F1: the anchor is written during collection, before the `:9031` gate runs, so these lines are not on its route either way.

**SF-2.
