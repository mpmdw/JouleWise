## The problem (lane text: TASK_QUEUE.md row A223; design: docs/process_traces/2026-09-16-activation-9853dd2b/44-structural-bound-consult-report.md section 3; counter-review record 36 F2)
Per capture slot the writer (`scripts/validate_powermetrics_fiducial.py`) performs FOUR whole-corpus custody verification passes on ONE shared 120 s `CustodyDeadline`: (1) preflight snapshot before the lease, (2) under-lease snapshot, (3) enforcing readiness under the lease, (4) slot validation. The reservation and the arm-time launchd probe perform ONE pass, so the install-time headroom gate (`joulewise/night_agent_install.py`: `WRITER_CUSTODY_PASSES = 4`, `CUSTODY_HEADROOM_FACTOR = 1.5`) demands a measured pass T ≤ 20 s. Consult §3(a): custody verification is a pure function of (locator, expected artifact hash) pairs re-derived from the ledger on every pass; only the disk bytes are what a pass re-reads. Reuse is SOUND under the lease and NOT sound across the lease boundary (pass 1 must never feed pass 2: recovery may mutate first). `repair_calibration_ledger` runs between passes 2 and 3, so the memo must be keyed on the physical ledger head digest: repair-as-no-op → passes 2/3/4 collapse (P = 2); repair mutated → the memo invalidates and one honest re-read follows (P = 3).
