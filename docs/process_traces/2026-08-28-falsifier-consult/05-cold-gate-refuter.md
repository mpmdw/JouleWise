## Q2 — R ≥ 2

Denominator: `_point_floor_diagnostic` recomputes `max(max_abs_deviation_j, prediction_component_j)` (`detection_floor.py:788-793`) — identical to `_floor_estimate`'s `unguarded` (`:715`). Numerator: `_corner_maximized_unguarded_floor` (`:856-913`) enumerates all 2ⁿ vertices and takes `max` of the **complete** floor recomputed at each corner (`:908-912`), i.e. `max(max|dev|, t·s+prediction_extra)` with **s and the t-term recomputed at the corner** (`:689-719`), and for `comparative`, `prediction_extra = |mean|` at that corner (`:907`).

So **R − 1 is not "timing widening"**: it is the fractional lift of a max-of-two-branches quantity, and the branch can switch (point floor on the residual branch, corner floor on the Student-t branch). It bundles residual displacement, corner-inflated scatter, and — comparative only — mean-shift.

Non-vacuity, however, is real. Each branch is convex in the displacement vector, so the max over the box is attained at a vertex and is ≥ the centre value: R ≥ 1 always, and strictly > 1 whenever any width > 0 (pick the sign-matching corner: `max|r_i + u_i| > max|r|`). Widths are never zero (`calibration_bracketing.py:199-214`). Hence a ">1" threshold would be exactly F-1's tautology; **R ≥ 2 is a bar that can fail.** Semantics defence stands as a stipulation on the *published* number.

TERM B / TERM A (`:822-835` over `:788-803`) is cleaner — pure linear widening, no branch switching — but TERM B is only a lower bound and is never published; the corner-maximised floor *is* the published number (`:528-529`). The ruling's choice is right; its *justification* is phrased in TERM-A/B language it does not use. Cheap bonus: R is a ratio of two already-emitted fields, `corner_widened_unguarded_floor_j / unguarded_floor_j` (`floor_extraction.py:1328-1336`), so ≈1 Sol-day is plausible.

Underspecified: the **quantifier**. Diagnostic R came as three per-cell numbers; the ruling never says all-cells vs claim-bearing-cell vs min, nor `absolute` vs `comparative` kind (the contrasts consume `comparative_false_effect_floor`, `floor_extraction.py:629-632`; a9/a10 were absolute). That needs one clause, not a ruling.

**Verdict: HOLDS WITH CAVEAT.**

## Q3 — R_cm as mandatory disclosure

`_common_mode_block_half_width` (`floor_extraction.py:446-490`) computes `shared_width` (`:481-484`) and `local_width` (`:485`) as **locals** and returns only their outward-rounded sum (`:487-489`). The caller appends that scalar to `block_widths` (`:616-628`) and hands it to `comparative_false_effect_floor` (`:629-632`), which varies the ten composed widths **independently**. The emitted floor row carries only `admissible_half_widths_j` (`:1331-1332`) — the composed values. **The shared/local split is destroyed before emission**, and R-2 itself concedes no cross-block shared-fiducial estimator exists. So R_cm is **not derivable at the desk** from what `_v5` will mint; disclosing it requires either a new estimator (the cost R-2 declines) or an artifact-schema change that must land *before* the mint, since D-140 forbids post-mint repair. As written, the mandatory disclosure is unenforceable.

Worse, the null consequence is unreachable. If R_cm is built the same way, the convexity argument of Q2 gives R_cm > 1 strictly for any positive width — and `local_width = fsum(residuals)/2` plus the never-zero allowance guarantee positivity. "R_cm ≤ 1 ⇒ withdraw" therefore re-imports precisely the tautology F-1 just deleted from `draft-v1.md:103`. The 1 < R_cm < 2 band the question raises is not a corner case; it is the entire reachable domain below 2.

The F-2 cancellation argument does hold structurally: composition is a plain magnitude sum plus a ~64-ULP pad (`:466-489`), so the shared component enters each block width at full magnitude and is then treated as independent across blocks. But note its scope — the deviations-from-mean cancellation is an **absolute**-kind argument (`detection_floor.py:846-860`); the claim-bearing floors here are comparative, where a common shift moves `|mean Δ|` and does *not* cancel. R-5's bench check should be scoped accordingly.

**Verdict: FAILS** — R_cm is neither computable from emitted artifacts nor capable of triggering its own null consequence.
