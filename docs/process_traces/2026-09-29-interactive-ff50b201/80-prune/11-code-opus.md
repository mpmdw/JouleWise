# Code-side prune inventory (Opus 5.5, independent; read-only at main 32ff9013)

Method. I ran a census of refusal reason codes, exception classes and `raise` counts per module, sized each module with `wc -l` and counted commits per file. For catches I used `git log -S`, grep across `docs/process_traces/`, `docs/run_reports/` and the decision log, and the per-module CI seconds in `scripts/test_timings.json`. Five read-only sub-audits (arm-readiness, night driver, calibration, machine state, analysis/paper) each read their modules and records. I spot-checked their load-bearing claims myself; the checks are listed at the end, and one sub-audit claim that I could not confirm is marked there. I read no file in `80-prune/` other than `00-rubric.md`. **Every "removable" figure is an estimate.** Line counts are `wc -l` on main.

**Scale.** Production code is 216,863 lines (`joulewise/` 139,859; `scripts/` about 75,000). Tests are 264,365 lines. The suite has 7,549 tests. CI time is 7,107 s summed over modules.

**The three facts that decide most verdicts:**

1. **The paper prints no number that this enforcement machinery produces or checks.** The D-174 disposition (`docs/paper/results-fill-registry.md` §"Submission disposition") places only historical diagnostic values: the clock/pulse reconstruction, record-support results, Appendix A calibration arithmetic, the 17-capture corpus and figures. It places no new model means, floors or verdicts. Record 00 item 156 (d528efb2) counts **241 printed results numbers that no checker compares by value**. The printed numbers that are guarded are guarded by small recompute paths: the replay fence (678 lines), R7F's registry-to-artefact check, the SYN-01 recompute test and the Table A3 test.
2. **The one live instrument number is the 25G83 calibration: S = 0.013701 s, C = 0.019021 s.** It came from W1/W2, whose arm scripts (`docs/process_traces/2026-09-26-activation-22784e38/10-w1-arm/scripts/*.zsh`, verified by grep) call `night_gate`, `evidence_night`, `battery_float`, `arm_census`, `arm_retry`, `calibration_ledger`, `gen_derivation_night` and `run_night`. They never call the 12,898-line `arm_readiness.py` receipt/launch stack. Their identity checks are shell pins: plan sha256, clone head equal to the reviewed H, a clean tree and a pip lock.
3. **Every production night ever armed used plan v2 `DIAGNOSTIC_NO_PACK`.** No v3 (pack transaction) plan, no v4 (quiet-admission) plan and no `_v5` pack root has ever run. Every mechanism built only for those paths has never fired on real data.

The pattern holds in every sub-audit. **Real data defects were caught by physics predicates inside the estimator or at t0, or by people** (the D-078 soundness audit, Ed's cadence challenge, the hand diagnosis of the July screensaver). **The custody, lifecycle, receipt and recovery layers have no recorded data catch.** Their recorded history is fix rounds on themselves, plus two lost nights and one lost night report that they caused.
