- **§9 thresholds (O-1 to O-4):** replaced, not sized.

In my simulation with no dependence at all, about 1 campaign in 35 had its ceiling C inflated by more than half, and some about twentyfold. It also corrected for the wrong comparison, because a bracket's two captures always sit in the same window.
  - **Replacement:** one always-computed value from the spread inside windows, Q99_within = t(0.995, n − K) × s_within × √2, taken into the maximum that sets C. It has no threshold and can exceed the plain Q99 by at most 15.7 % (2.7 % at n = 24 in two windows).
