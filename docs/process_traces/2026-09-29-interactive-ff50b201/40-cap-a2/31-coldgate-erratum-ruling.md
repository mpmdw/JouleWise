## 1. Words used

Only the terms this erratum needs; A2 §1 defines the rest and stands.

- **Calibration**: an issued JSON file of limits on **B**, the one timing-uncertainty number a capture yields. A **member** is a capture whose B enters the limits. **R7** is `d079_calibration_acceptance_v2_n17_r7`, the 17-member calibration for build 25F84 that is the default today.
- **P8** (new name, §3.5): the file A2 called "R8": R7 re-issued with only its recorded code digests, its identity and its notes changed. Its identifier stays `d079_calibration_acceptance_v2_n17_r8`, subject to the owner's approval of the name.
- **Rule R8**: the cap rule's tripwire (A1 §4, rule R8): at harvest, the harness reports every capture's cell count and stops; (a) a capture over half the cap halts claim windows for a sizing review; (b) a stop on the cap or the deadline holds that window's claim status.
- **Pins**: the sha256 digests of the four estimator files a calibration records. A **pin-delta** is a copy of a calibration whose only differences are its pins, its digest and, once renamed, its identity.
- **The validator**: `_valid_acceptance_bound` in `joulewise/calibration_bracketing.py`. It recomputes a calibration's statistics and operatives from its member table and refuses unless they equal the **generation row** registered for that identifier in `_D102_GENERATION_DERIVATIONS`, and unless the identifier is in `ISSUED_ACCEPTANCE_REGISTRY`.
- **The re-issue tool**: `scripts/reissue_calibration_acceptance.py`. It authenticates each member's bundle, then builds a pin-delta and a **delta report** that stops on any difference other than pins.
- **The verify script**: `tests/verify_calibration_acceptance_corpus.py`. It recomputes the statistics from the member table and checks the bundle hashes; it reads no raw bytes. Its table `EXPECTED_BY_ACCEPTANCE_ID` banks, per generation, the expected statistics and a flag `stored_lexeme_is_member_value`: `True` for the n=19 generations, whose member values are copies of each bundle's `instrument_evidence.json`; `False` for r3–r7, whose values were re-derived from raw bytes and therefore differ from the evidence file **by design** (its comment: equality "would be a defect").
- **The issuer**: `scripts/issue_calibration_acceptance_generation.py`, which derives a successor calibration from a sealed registration and a ledger of observations.
- **Content identifier**: the sha256 the ledger gives a capture from its manifest and evidence hashes. **Disposition registry**: `configs/calibration/observation_dispositions.json`, rows of content identifier, decision identifier and mechanism text, pinned by digest, that tell the issuer and the loader which valid rows of an epoch are set aside and why. On the option (a) branch its decisions live in `joulewise/calibration_dispositions.py` as `DISPOSITION_DECISIONS`; on main the issuer carries one decision inline.
- **Foreign row**: a valid observation of the target epoch that belongs to no session of the registration and carries no disposition; the issuer refuses while any exists (its addendum A-7 rule).
- **W1/W2**: the two windows of 2026-09 whose 24 captures under the 165,000-cell cap gave 12 valid rows and 8 cap stops. **r1**: the unregistered 25G83 candidate built from those 12. **The successor**: the calibration to be derived from the fresh cap-acceptance windows.
- **N-1 … N-6**: A2 §3.1's conditions that must be executed before P8 is admissible. **Steps**: numbered items of the sequence in §4; "A2 step n" means the number in A2 §4.1, "step n" alone means the number in §4 below.
