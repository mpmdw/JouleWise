`--window-id C1 --extra-sentinels 2` and verifies its output with
`--check` using the same options. W1's recorded U4 inconclusive triggers the supplemental pair.
The result has **24 members and four sentinels**. Anchors are 1–4, ordinary
sentinels 5 and 22, and sixteen fresh members 6–21 (seven benign and one
seeded instructed per pair). The midpoint follows 11. Supplemental
sentinels 23 and 24 follow 22 before the closing reference and scaffold,
replay the admitted W1:5 trajectory, use no held-out seed and count toward
nothing. Verify the unchanged base roster and manifest, the seed and
replay bindings, and the sizing and disk reservation, with a **62,400 s
(17 h 20 min) window ceiling**.
