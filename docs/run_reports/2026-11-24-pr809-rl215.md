## NEEDS_SPEC

1. **One outer file without the other in an archive.** RL-215 rules the archive with both outer files (compare as native) and with neither (archived custody). With one and not the other I refuse `evidence_unverifiable`. (Natively, a missing row is `evidence_unverifiable` and a missing reference `source_integrity`.)
2. **The epoch-to-monotonic offset.** "Over the hull of its pairs" is read as the hull of the pairs' offset intervals `[epoch − after, epoch − before]`, with no widening by the declared resolutions. A widening of even 1 ns would admit the one-nanosecond tick-window counterexamples the table refuses.
3. **Where the archived row names its segment.** An archive is moved by nature, so the row's `output_dir` is compared only against the reference's `source_output` there; its resolution to the segment stays a native ledger check.
4. **A frozen-contract field that is absent.** The frozen invoker's own word is `receipt_contract`. Where the table gives a class's reason for `missing` (`period-config`, `token-count`, `step-g0`, `admission-count`, `phase-total`), the owning class's reason is used instead. `drain` and `desk-owned-required` keep `receipt_contract`, as ruled.
