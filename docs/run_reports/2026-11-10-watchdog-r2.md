**W-A route choice:** `refs/heads/watchdog-status/<host>` on origin, using the
same Git-push mechanism as W1 A-02. A per-host ref separates independent
writers and avoids main/code-tree mutation. Each snapshot has only
`status.json` and `transitions.jsonl`, with the previous status commit as its
parent. No force push. Exported fields are host, state, named hold category,
transition time/sequence, dwell, last progress/heartbeat age, heartbeat
staleness, last activation ID, and observation epoch. Raw hold details,
paths, transcripts and outcomes are excluded.

Every transition enters a durable outbox. Stale-progress changes also enter
the outbox when the state stays ACTIVE. Only an acknowledged push clears
entries and writes a delivery receipt. Failure leaves them available after
restart. One attempt per 60 seconds, including backward clock steps; keepalive
due at 3,250 seconds reserves a 300-second tick and transport margin before
an hour. Scheduling/sleep/network failure can exceed that target, so Air
staleness is still essential. Transport shares a 25-second deadline plus up
to two seconds for timeout cleanup; Git/SSH prompts are disabled and only the
transport's own new process group is killed on timeout. Credentials must work
noninteractively in launchd.

The short tick publishes after its safety action. Resident publication uses a
background thread and a separate publisher flock; the REQUEST/TERM/KILL path
does not wait for transport. Missing/stale delivery, failed export or an
expired publisher prevents a new activation with `HOLD_STATUS_EXPORT`.
A fresh prior receipt permits an ordinary refresh already in flight; an old
failure stays latched until push succeeds. Plan/desk safety decisions retain
priority, and all sources are checked again immediately before spawn.

Air `poll` fetches only status-ref history, follows an Air-owned cursor, and
retains alarm transitions until explicit `--ack`. A transient hold recovered
between polls or within one rate-limited batch remains visible. It exits 3
for holds/STRANDED, uncertain clock/network, stale magistrate progress,
missing/invalid/unavailable status, or a watchdog observation at least
75 minutes old. The process document supplies the command and notification
handoff. No Air scheduler/notifier is installed here. **W-01 and W-02 are not
closed:** prove launchd publication plus actual Air alarm delivery first.
