Serialization/transport is not material anywhere measured (step-2 driver pickling
1.2 s; member-pool export/merge well under 1 s self). Decimal arithmetic is material
only in acceptance validation (below). Rehash of registered outputs is 3-4 s per step.

Two structural facts matter more than any hot loop:

- **L4 never stores.** Every `calibration_bracket_for_bundles` fill is unstorable
  (`L4_calibration_bracket` after step 4: miss 10, unstorable 5, fill 0; after
  step 5: miss 18, unstorable 9, fill 0; identical in r2 and this branch):
  `calibration_ledger.probe_custody` starts a daemon thread, and the store marks
  any fill that starts a thread unstorable. So steps 4 and 5 recompute brackets
  (2 + 4 evaluations, each with its candidate refits) already evaluated in 3b:
  about 45% of step 4's child work and 26% of step 5's wall in the synthetic runs.
- **Candidate refits repeat across packs.** The three packs' ledger prefixes share
  candidates, but each bracket evaluation re-verifies every candidate from bytes.

Both are frozen code or cache eligibility, so neither is changed here (section 6).

**Step 5 driver attribution** (`part5-head-stacks/stacks-step5.json`: driver main
thread sampled every 20 ms, 55,495 samples = 1,110 s; inclusive seconds):
mint 575, pinset emit 402 (its producer preparation waits 282 on the pack pool);
`whole_window_refusal_reasons` 420, of which current-core rederivation 238 (fiducial
refits 208); `launch_lineage_refusal_reasons` -> `ordinary_present_bundle_paths`
130 (an authenticated read of every bundle manifest per call); the unstorable L4
bracket recomputation 157; strict JSONL parsing 126 (first parses in this process:
the L7 grammar-verdict memo is per process and is not among the memos shipped
from workers); `Path.resolve` inside every authenticated read 111. Everything
except the refits is non-frozen code.

## 5. Projected schedule (real data)

Base: dry-j r2 per-step walls (7 workers, nice 5, loaded machine). Each step's
projection multiplies the r2 wall by the synthetic ratio measured for that step's
change (1.0 where nothing changed). Step 10 is projected with `reported_pack`

Ten workers instead of seven (4 P + 6 E cores; E about half a P core; fanless):
about +15-25% throughput on pool-bound work (steps 1 and 4, the 3b validations,
member pools inside 5-11), little on the serial refits. Estimate -250 to -450 s:
**about 129-132 min**. A quiet machine (dry-j shared it) could be materially
faster on pool-bound steps; that is not a code effect and must be measured.

What would be needed for 90 min (5,400 s; about 2,700 s more), by size:

1. **Make L4 storable** (whitelist the custody-probe thread, whose only actions are
   `exists`/`is_dir`, in the fill recorder): steps 4 and 5 (and any later bracket
   consumer) stop recomputing brackets already evaluated in 3b. Synthetic shares
   (45% of step 4, 26% of step 5) applied to r2: about -380 s and -370 s, **about
   -12 min**. Cache-eligibility change -> needs a ruling. Largest single lever found.
2. **Run-scoped memo + parallel prefetch of candidate refits** in
   `calibration_bracketing._load_calibration_candidate_unbounded` (pin4 file; the
   estimator `powermetrics_fiducial.py` itself unchanged): 3b's per-pack wall drops
   from 4 sequential refits to about 1-2, and step 4 benefits likewise if (1) is not
   taken. Estimate -300 to -450 s. Frozen-file change -> needs a ruling.
3. Step 11: run the primary-bound and wider-bound passes concurrently, consuming
   in order (both are independent pure recomputations from the same primary
   outputs). Unmeasured; plausibly -300 to -600 s.
4. Pack pools ship the **entire** VDL store to each pack worker
   (`pack_parallel.pack_map` -> `export_entries()`); late in the run that is large.
   Ship only the pack's roots plus path-free entries (as `_store_for(step 4)` does).
   Suspected cause of step 10's warm regression; would make 5/10/11 staging pay.
5. Step 5's driver-serial authenticated I/O (reads, strict parse, `Path.resolve`
   per read: about 560 of 1,205 synthetic seconds) is non-frozen and repeated per
   program: the emit and the mint each authenticate the same producer inputs
   serially. Running the mint's per-pack authentication ahead in pack workers, as
   the emit's producer preparation now does, is the analogue of `f4d000128`;
   unmeasured, plausibly -200 to -400 s.
6. Ship the L7 grammar-verdict memo (`authentication_io.grammar_ok`) back from
   workers with the two memos already shipped. Exact by the memo's own
   stack-headroom argument (a worker's pushed-down success bounds the frames the
   bytes need), but it extends `SHIPPED_MEMOS`, a D-188 design choice -> ruling.
   Step 5 synthetic: up to about -120 s; likely similar in 8-11.
7. Steps 8, 6, 9, 10, 11 have **no profile** (no synthetic run reaches them in a
   cloud hour). They are 4,220 s of r2's 9,145 s.

Even with 1-6 at their high ends and ten workers, the projection is about 100-105
min. Ninety minutes needs the L4 and refit rulings, a profile of steps 8-11 and
work there.

## 6. What the post-release timing run must confirm

Run `scripts/run_b5_analysis.py` (release-authorized operator) with `--phase-log`,
on the quiet laptop, twice: `--workers 10` and `--workers 7`, VDL default, then the
D-188 comparator against the r2 reference.

1. Comparator EQUIVALENT (inputs, decisions, registered, intermediate) for the
   integrated branch over all steps; in particular steps 5-11, which no synthetic
   run of the integrated code has covered.
2. **Refit deadline margin.** 3b now runs three packs' candidate refits
   concurrently with member validations. Record each refit's wall against the
   120 s projection budget (restricted `runtime-phases`, `k.detect`/`k.refit`);
   a firing is a tooling refusal. If margins are thin, use
   `JOULEWISE_B5_PACK_PARALLEL=off` for 3b or fewer workers.
3. Step 10 with staging on vs off (warm regression), and peak RSS of pack pools
   (each pack worker receives the full store).
4. Per-step walls vs the table above; steps 5-11 phase breakdown
   (`b5_phase_report.py --detail` on the restricted log, operator side) to decide
   items 3-5 above.
5. Ten-worker sustained thermals (fanless) and swap.
