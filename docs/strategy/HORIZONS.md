# JouleWise horizons — the goal map (2026-08-07)

Ed asked for a goal to work toward, and a structure he can point at:
"finish the paper, the long horizon research question goals, etc."
This is that map. Each horizon states what DONE means, what would KILL
it, and what it unblocks. Ed points at a horizon; the magistrate picks
the next unblocked move inside it.

**The north star (why any of this matters).** Energy should be a
first-class axis in how LLM inference techniques are evaluated —
alongside quality and latency — and the thing that makes that possible
is an instrument that knows what it cannot resolve. Everything below is
a step toward measurements a stranger can trust about mechanisms that
actually matter.

## H1 — THE ICPE-CLASS VERSION  ← next semester

**Done means:** the metrology core plus ONE designed extension, at a
venue where the community that cares about measurement rigor reads it.

**The adopted bet:** quantization BF16/Q4/Q8 (4 nights; consumes the
D-117 Q4 floors directly as a rung; the BF16-Q4 arm is guaranteed
resolvable and Q4-Q8 adjudicates a real open question). The strongest
alternative — and the one that serves the original goals better — is the
single-window KV-context contrast (128 vs 4096 context at fixed decode),
which is also the cheapest designed extension on the board.

**Kills it:** the conversion smoke failing; or the second-paper work
being allowed to touch the mint/pinset file set before U10 closes
(portfolio rule R1 exists to prevent exactly that).

**Unblocks:** a venue record that makes the mechanism work publishable
rather than hobbyist.

## H2 — MECHANISM-LEVEL ENERGY  ← the original research goals

**Done means:** energy measured as a third axis for inference techniques,
each result carrying its floor and its refusals.

Status of each axis, honestly:
- **MoE routing** — the live stretch bet. Re-anchored to Qwen3-30B-A3B vs
  its matched-active dense partner, with a genuinely novel thesis: at
  batch 1 on unified memory, expert-budget savings should be SUBLINEAR in
  removed parameters. Four nights behind seven desk gates.
- **Speculative decoding** — a 2-hour daytime pilot decides it, and the
  repo's own smoke suggests spec-on may never repay its energy at batch
  1. If so, that negative result is publishable and costs one afternoon.
- **KV / attention** — reachable now via the context-slope contrast; the
  architecture-variant version is not (no admitted checkpoint has the
  toggle).
- **MTP** — closed by a dated runtime verdict; reopens only if a pinned
  runtime retains the heads and supplies real counters.
- **Split inference** — the most ambitious original goal and currently
  the least reachable: no cross-device fiducial exists, and transfer
  energy is unmeasurable at both endpoint boundaries. One evening of GPU
  cadence characterization tells us whether it is sizeable at all.

**Kills any of them:** an effect that cannot clear a projected floor at
3x. That refusal is itself a result and should be published as one.

## H3 — WHAT THE INSTRUMENT COULD BECOME  ← optional, only if H0-H2 land

The honest candidates, none funded: a second physical unit (better
generalization value than a wall meter); external-meter validation of
absolute scale (validates totals, never the phase split); the
detection-floor methodology generalized to other software counters; and
the reporting-standard work, whose one genuinely uncovered claim is that
no existing ML-energy regime states a detection floor at all.

## Standing rules that bind every horizon

- **P1 the MVP paper, P2 the ICPE version, P3 modularity for future
  axes** — P3 is sacrificed whenever it costs P1 or P2 (Ed, 2026-08-06).
- **Nothing merges without the full council gate** (D-118); a burn
  license increases what enters the gate, never reduces it.
- **Kill thresholds are multiples of a projected floor**, never joule
  literals, never at 1x.
- **Claim wording is conservative by default** (D-119).
- **Quiet nights are the scarcest resource.** Desk work that removes a
  night's risk outranks desk work that adds a night's ambition.
