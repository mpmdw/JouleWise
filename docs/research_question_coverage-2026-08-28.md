# Research-question coverage against the frozen `_v5` campaign, the transfer fiducial (known-gap diagnostic), and the three-model ladder

**Date:** 2026-08-28

This is the coverage map Ed requested and decision D-163 (the ruling on what follows `_v5`: the inserted-gap fiducial and the three-model decode ladder) ordered (`docs/decision_log.md:10336-10348`). It puts every live row in `docs/research_question_registry.md` beside the additional identifiers retained only in `docs/research_question_bank.md`, then asks what the frozen `_v5` campaign on `mlx-community/Qwen3-1.7B-4bit` and `mlx-community/Qwen3-8B-4bit`, the first post-campaign transfer-fiducial diagnostic, the three-model ladder whose D-163 model set is unresolved under `_v5`, or evidence already retained at the desk can honestly answer. The live registry supplies current type, status, and claim ceiling; the bank supplies the historical question wording, rejected upgrades, and the ten identifiers that were folded into live rows rather than given their own registry rows.

Here, a large language model (LLM) has two measured stages: **prefill**, which processes the prompt, and **decode**, which emits output tokens. MLX is Apple’s open-source machine-learning framework and is the inference runtime used here. Apple’s `powermetrics` program samples modeled central-processing-unit (CPU), graphics-processing-unit (GPU), and Apple Neural Engine (ANE) on-chip power channels, called **rails**, for the system on a chip (SoC); these are not physical wall measurements. Its **sampler cadence** is how often it writes a power record, about every tenth of a second here. A **cell resolution bound**, called a detection floor in the artifacts, is the largest false energy difference the named measurement system can manufacture for one fixed condition. An **A/B/B/A block** is a counterbalanced four-run order whose first and last runs use condition A and whose middle two use condition B.

A **bundle** is the per-run evidence folder—power trace, events, outputs, and metadata—that the reducer, the analysis program, consumes. A **holdout** or **held-out request** is deliberately excluded from fitting and used only to test prediction. A **smoke run** is a small feasibility test, and a **shim** is a small adapter between the external runner and JouleWise. Greedy, sampled, and beam decoding are output-selection strategies: greedy always chooses the most probable next token, sampling draws randomly, and beam decoding keeps several candidate continuations. HumanEval is a public code-generation benchmark of programming problems; FLORES is a public multilingual translation benchmark that presents the same sentences in many languages; and **tokenizer fertility** means the number of tokens produced per word or character for a language. AXI names the project’s architecture-extension stream. A state-space/attention hybrid combines recurrent state updates with attention layers, rather than using attention alone.

## How to read the tables

The registry/bank status is copied, not reconsidered: **promoted** means the question owns a named project slot; **banked** means viable but not promoted; **candidate** means proposed and bounded; **killed** means deliberately rejected; **answered-L1** means answered only at the registry’s limited descriptive claim level; and **candidate (C-023)** is the whole-project review’s candidate class.

Every coverage cell uses exactly one of these labels. The letter is only a reading aid; the words are controlling.

- **A. `answered by _v5`** — the frozen three-pack design on `mlx-community/Qwen3-1.7B-4bit` and `mlx-community/Qwen3-8B-4bit` will answer the row if collection and governed close-out succeed.
- **B. `answerable by fiducial`** — the diagnostic `TRANSFER-FIDUCIAL-01` post-campaign window will answer the row. It is not eligible to support a claim and cannot create or widen one.
- **C. `answerable by ladder`** — the D-163 decode-only 0.5B/1.5B/7B ladder as ruled predates D-164, and its model set is unresolved under `_v5` (`docs/decision_log.md:191`).
- **D. `answerable at the desk now`** — retained evidence already supports an answer at the row’s stated ceiling. “Partial” means the artifact answers only the named subpart, not the whole row.
- **E. `not answerable with this instrument`** — the present M3 Max, `powermetrics` rails, and software boundary cannot physically reach the answer. The short reason names the structural limit: another device or meter, an unmeasured endpoint, a runtime that cannot run here, an event faster than the sampler cadence, or a killed question.
- **F. `not a question`** — an application idea or methodology artifact is built, used, proposed, or killed rather than answered as a research question. The state is stated in the same cell.
- **G. `answerable with this instrument, no route scheduled`** — this Mac, sampler, bundle format, and floor machinery could answer the row, but `_v5`, the fiducial, the ladder, and retained desk evidence do not. The short reason states the additional cells and measurement night or nights required.
- **H. `answerable by the _v6 scored leg`** — Ed’s scored benchmark leg uses GSM8K, eight items per member, and a pinned checker; accuracy is a set property under the AP-5 / C5-1.9 quarantine because training-data contamination cannot be ruled out. It is built at the desk during `_v5` and run post-campaign after the fiducial night (`docs/decision_log.md:193`).

The `_v5` rows below cite the checked-in `*_v3` predecessor pack shapes. The contrast pack has a checked-in **analysis manifest**—the predeclared instructions for reducing and testing its data—at `configs/campaigns/d117_contrast_qwen25_1p5b_vs_7b_v3/analysis_manifest_v3.json`; the two floor directories have plan trees but no `analysis_manifest_v3.json`. No separately named `_v5` pack configuration or fifth-generation freeze record was found in this worktree. The predecessor `_v4` project task record and final ruling say that the real transaction would have created freeze records from these three predecessor campaign families (`docs/process/state_kernel.json:4500-4548`; `docs/process_traces/2026-08-20-go-session/rulings-r5-consolidation.md:72-104`), but D-164 says `_v4` is never collected. The `_v5` pack re-pins the models to `mlx-community/Qwen3-1.7B-4bit` and `mlx-community/Qwen3-8B-4bit` per D-164 and is not yet in this tree (`docs/decision_log.md:191`). ## Gap statement for the advisor

Ed’s original list was deliberately larger than one capstone paper. The bank opens as “Candidate research questions the instrument can answer, beyond the named set in `PROJECT_STATUS.md`” and says promotion requires a named slot, a non-displacing data plan, and scope fit (`docs/research_question_bank.md:3-8`). Its early promoted questions asked for a predictive energy model, workload-dependent ranking stability, and rail-versus-wall boundary sensitivity (`docs/research_question_bank.md:14-31`). Its banked questions then ranged from rail utilization and phase structure to KV-cache growth, cooldown tails, failures, model residency, correctness-conditioned energy, speculative decoding, and power modes (`docs/research_question_bank.md:42-84`). The later agenda made the ambition explicit: the “infrastructure outlives any single result” and every target should pass through the same config-to-bundle-to-reduction contract (`docs/research_question_bank.md:577-580`). That is an instrument research program, not a promise that one paper answers all 89 indexed rows.

The draft’s intended research-question answer set is narrow: `RQ-ATTRIBUTION-DOMINANCE`, still pending `_v5`, the `_v5` demonstration answer to `C5-1.1` at its fixed 8B-versus-1.7B pairwise ceiling, and the already retained negative `RQ-SHORT-PREFILL-RESOLVABILITY`. `RQ-METHOD-FLOOR` is the method they consume, not another research-question answer. The paper explicitly refuses to turn the fixed pair into a scaling law; the D-163 ladder as ruled predates D-164, and its 0.5B/1.5B/7B model set remains unresolved under `_v5` (`docs/decision_log.md:191`). This narrowing follows the T26 paper-goal ruling (`docs/process_traces/2026-08-27-t26/paper-goal-consult/03-MAGISTRATE-RULING.md:79-87`) and the D-163 ladder ruling (`docs/process_traces/2026-08-28-ladder-consult/04-MAGISTRATE-RULING.md:46-50`). The draft mentions other rows only as limitations, exclusions, methods, or future work. In particular it names `C5-1.3` as future characterization rather than a capstone research question (`docs/paper/draft-v1.md:312-318`).

The two gap numbers now say different things. **Seventeen rows cannot be reached with this instrument**: one M3 Max cannot answer machine-to-machine variance, cross-vendor ranking, phone feasibility, or cross-laboratory replication; its processor-package rails cannot supply wall, battery-path, or conversion-loss energy; and split, transfer, and cloud questions lack measured remote endpoints. The killed general intelligence-per-joule row also remains in this class. A wall meter could check total gain, but it still could not decide how the software trace divides a correct total between prefill and decode. Split inference and KV transfer need two live endpoints, measured links, serialization/deserialization windows, and clocks whose disagreement is bounded at both ends.

**Forty-one rows could be reached with this instrument but have no scheduled route.** They need their own cell floors and measurement night or nights on this Mac. These include shape, content, power-mode, runtime, quantization, batching, speculative decoding, cache-policy, workload-category, imported-benchmark, prompt-template, and version-drift questions. A fixed condition cannot reveal an axis it holds constant, but that is a campaign-planning gap rather than a physical limit of the Mac, sampler, bundle format, or floor machinery.

Time resolution creates a second hard boundary. Output tokens arrive around milliseconds while `powermetrics` records arrive around a tenth of a second. The instrument can integrate longer decode chunks, but it cannot attach honest joules to individual tokens or very short prefill phases. The retained short-prefill result makes this concrete: 37 of 50 phases overlapped only two power records, below the rule’s minimum of three. Repetition does not fix this alignment problem. Likewise, the roughly one-joule attribution width comes from uncertainty in where phase edges fall during steep power changes; collecting more repetitions reduces ordinary scatter but not a boundary shift shared by the runs. Questions whose expected effects live below that cell-specific bound need a longer aggregation window, a larger predeclared contrast, a faster independent instrument, or an honest `not resolvable` result.

The additions are separate. `_v5` supplies independently constructed Qwen3 1.7B and 8B prefill/decode cell bounds, tests whether timing attribution dominates repeatability, and applies those bounds to a fixed 8B-minus-1.7B decision; it does not test whether pulse calibration transfers to inference. `TRANSFER-FIDUCIAL-01` inserts an approximately 500-millisecond commanded gap into about ten real inference runs, fits both edges with the existing estimator, and compares the residual with the pulse-derived bound. It is diagnostic, is ineligible to support claims, and answers the transfer limitation only. The D-163 ladder as ruled predates D-164: its 0.5B/1.5B/7B model set is unresolved under `_v5`, so its adjacent pairwise extension for `C5-1.1` is not presently assigned. The `_v6` scored GSM8K leg is built at the desk during `_v5` and runs after the fiducial night; it supplies the scheduled correctness-guarded and imported-benchmark evidence represented by the H rows. These additions leave workload-shape energy, prefill scaling, quantization, runtime, cross-device, and wall-power questions untouched.
