## Open questions for Ed

1. **Title.** The draft's title is protocol-first, and the "attribution-limited" subtitle prints only under
   dominance branch A. Is the title acceptable, and is a shorter one wanted for the venue?
2. **Length and venue.** The main text is about 17,600 words outside the placeholders (§3 about 4,000, §6 about
   3,600). For a conference-length paper, the natural cuts are §3.3, §3.6 and §6.3 into an appendix or
   supplement. Which venue and limit should the draft aim at?
3. **AI agents as authors.** §7 and §8 say that agents ran the study and wrote much of the text. How should
   authorship and acknowledgement be worded for the target venue, and may model families and versions be named
   (`P1-AUTO-MODELS`)?
4. **Tier naming.** The contingency table calls Tier D "floor" and Tier E "last resort". The brief asked for "BYTES
   as registered design" and "the floor version" as separate variants, so this draft maps them to D and E. Is that
   the intended mapping?
5. **Tier B with a partial second pass.** If pass 2 exists for some models but not all, should those models
   average both passes (the registration's per-model rule) or print pass 2 as description only (this draft's
   `tierB` text)? This needs a ruling before any BYTES number is seen.

## Points the registrations do not settle

1. **BYTES collection end.** `bytes_pack_v1.md` (work branch) registers "no t0 at or after
   2026-11-02T00:00:00-08:00"; the extension note of 2026-10-27 and the contingency plan both use 5 November; the
   pipeline audit proposed a blind three-day extension. The draft leaves the date as `P1-BY-END`. Which text is the
   registered end, and where is the amendment recorded?
2. **Where Part 1 may print at all.** Every block-5 print site is `PROPOSED_STOP_FILL` and every reused registry
   row is `RETIRED_FALLBACK` under D-174. Block-5 registration §14 Q4 requires a placement ruling before anything
   prints. That ruling is still needed. It should also cover the new `P1-*` ids.
3. **When the block-5 independent recompute runs.** The contingency plan puts the recompute's agreement before the
   block-5 release (8 November). The pipeline-audit critique cites RUN_STATE item 37 for "after the release". The
   draft says only that the recompute ran "before a result was printed" (§4.7). Which order applies?
4. **The D-177 sentence.** Block-5 registration §1 says that *every* phase-energy sentence carries the limitation
   that phase attribution was not checked by a measured instrument. The draft states it once at the head of §5, in
   §8, in Appendix C and in the abstract. Is that enough, or must each results sentence repeat it?
5. **Verbatim sentences against plain language.** Several registered fixed sentences use internal names ("NEG-8
   screen", "window"). The brief forbids internal shorthand. The draft writes "drift screen" and notes the
   substitution in Appendix C. A ruling is needed on whether the registered sentences may be reworded this way.
6. **Claims-ladder level for BYTES.** The block-5 ladder defines L3 as "a fitted model checked on held-out
   cells", which describes H1. Neither BYTES text assigns a ladder level to its words. The draft assigns none.
7. **Open slots in the replication registration.** The registration's §14 slots S-5, S-9, S-11 (stop lines from
   each pack's smoke), S-14 (the analysis commit) and S-15 (the per-phase whole-machine definition, `P1-B6-WM-DEF`)
   were open when the draft was written. Their final state should be checked before the replication fill.
8. **Possible blinding exposure in BYTES.** The pipeline-audit critique (item 3) says that a process audit ran
   harvest readers on a held-out model's session (s5-p1) and read request-relative timing before stage 1. Decode
   time is one of the rival hypotheses. The draft keeps `P1-AUTO-BLINDCHECK`, so the outcome of that check is
   disclosed whichever way it falls.
9. **Block-5 attempt history.** The replication registration's §0 names which attempt of each pack was analysed.
   The draft leaves this to `P1-B5-ANALYSED` and `P1-B5-ATTEMPTS`, so that it comes from the harvest records
   rather than from prose.
