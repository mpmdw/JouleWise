Independent project critique

JouleWise is a strong instrument, not yet a settled study.

A critic-forward review of the project goals, documents, architecture, tests, measurement methodology, stack choices, and research usefulness. Prepared from a local codebase review, focused parallel critiques, and a second-pass reassessment after the project's critique-response work.

Executive Verdict

JouleWise is useful and worth continuing. Its strongest current contribution is not a headline energy result; it is the auditable measurement instrument: typed configs, adapter boundaries, raw evidence bundles, reducer re-derivation, and explicit measurement boundaries.

The project should be judged harshly on claims. Today it can support a narrow statement: auditable local LLM inference energy measurement on one Apple-Silicon stack is working and has produced real bundles. It cannot yet support broad cross-device rankings, split-inference crossover claims, or general model-scaling laws.

Main critical finding after reassessment: the project has converted several critique findings into real mechanics, but the center of gravity is still ahead: detection-floor calibration, the quiet-machine 2M baseline corpus, and live non-Mac validation.
564 tests pass Local suite ran green with 10 skips and zero expected failures.
6 real bundles Strict-valid Mac MLX + powermetrics evidence exists, but it is narrow.
1 measured hardware boundary Apple SoC rails via powermetrics; cross-target science is still gated.
Pre-results study The harness is real. The research matrix is still mostly ahead.

Second-Pass Reassessment

I reassessed the project after the critique-response session that explicitly asked other reviewers to judge what this report was right about. The new verdict is more nuanced: the first critique was directionally right about the research risks, but several of its highest-leverage recommendations are no longer open critiques. They have become code, contracts, tests, and queue gates.

Updated thesis: JouleWise is now a substantially stronger evidence-preserving measurement harness than the first critique gave it credit for. It is still not yet a complete empirical study. That distinction matters: the engineering instrument is credible; the broad scientific claims still need the calibration and campaign corpus.

Layer-By-Layer Updated Verdict

Research Claims

Grade: promising but still gated. The best current claim is that the project can produce auditable local LLM energy evidence on one Apple-Silicon stack. The next acceptable research step is not a grand conclusion; it is a calibrated 2M corpus with confidence intervals and claim levels.

Measurement Core

Grade: strong for this stage. Typed configs, immutable bundles, raw artifacts, config hashes, strict re-reduction, and source-preserving telemetry make the instrument more trustworthy than a normal benchmark script.

Campaign Science

Grade: recently upgraded, not yet proven in the field. The runner now has the right fail-closed shape. The remaining proof is operational: run P2-015 and 2M under quiet-machine constraints and see whether the mechanics hold under real pressure.

Architecture And Stack

Grade: pragmatic and test-backed. The stdlib-only core is a good measurement choice. The price is custom validation and protocol code, so the project must keep pinning edge cases with tests rather than relying on conventions.

Tests And CI

Grade: unusually good for a prototype. The suite tests the evidence path, not just happy-path functions. The gap is live optional paths: MLX privileges, real SSH, vLLM, nvidia-smi behavior, and cross-boundary calibration remain outside ordinary CI.

Documentation

Grade: rich but high-maintenance. The docs have become a strength because they preserve decisions, reversals, and caveats. They also need editorial pressure so old status or old overclaim wording does not leak into reader-facing summaries.

Research Value

The project is trying to prove two related claims: first, that local LLM inference energy can be measured in an auditable way; second, that prefill/decode split inference can be studied as an energy tradeoff. The first claim is already partially substantiated. The second is still an aspiration with a sensible feasibility ladder.

Strongest Research Thread

Q4, the fixed-vs-marginal energy model, is the best centerpiece. A model such as E = fixed + prefill(prompt_tokens) + decode(output_tokens) is valuable even if live split inference never works. It gives the project a practical contribution for app builders and creates a bridge from benchmarking to energy budgeting.

Most Fragile Research Thread

Q1-Q3, split crossover and Pareto claims, are the most distinctive but least evidenced. They require second-target telemetry, network topology, transfer measurements, compatible KV replay, and real split or composition validation.

Useful Today

  • Auditable Mac-local energy measurement for MLX workloads.
  • Bundle contract and validation machinery that can outlive this specific study.
  • Preliminary Apple-stack observations that generate hypotheses about fixed and marginal energy.
  • A credible fallback capstone: harness + Mac characterization + synthetic transfer + analytical split model.

Not Yet Supported

  • Broad active-parameter scaling claims from only two confounded model points.
  • Cross-device energy rankings before NVIDIA/Orin live validation and boundary calibration.
  • Fine-grained per-phase claims where phase windows are shorter than the sampler can resolve.
  • Split-inference energy crossover claims before Phase 3 produces measured or carefully composed evidence.

Architecture Critique

The project has a strong spine: CLI to config, config to controller, controller to adapters, adapters to immutable bundle, bundle to reducer/report/validation. That shape matches the research goal. The most important architectural risk is policy accumulation in large modules.

Strong

Raw evidence first. summary_metrics.json is written last as a completion marker, and summaries are derived rather than trusted as source evidence.

Strong

BundleReader centralizes interpretation policy for rail manifests, measured windows, trace validation, and completion.

Strong

Injected clocks and mock adapters make measurement logic testable without hardware or sleeps.

Architectural Pressure Points

  • controller.py owns lifecycle, failure recovery, buffered logging, metadata assembly, cooldown, and experiment manifests. It is becoming the project center of gravity.
  • Remote execution has two transport concepts: public transport adapters and node-worker transport/session behavior. A first-class RemoteNodeSession would reduce drift.
  • Strict raw-to-derived validation is strong for powermetrics but not yet generalized to every telemetry backend.
  • The remote node worker is pragmatic, but large. Protocol validation needs to become more mechanical so correctness does not depend on careful memory.
  • Run IDs are immutable and deterministic, which is good for tests, but normal reruns can collide too easily.

Remaining False Confidence Risks

  • The 2M config generator and campaign runner are tested, but the real 2M corpus has not yet run.
  • Detection-floor calibration is queued, not completed.
  • The 2M config generator is deterministic and schema-valid, but it does not prove that four hardcoded profiles are representative.
  • The NVIDIA/vLLM path is fixture-first and honest about being provisional, but tests are still fake/stub dominated until live hardware contact.
  • CI intentionally avoids optional/hardware paths, so live-gated verification scripts remain important.

What The Current Evidence Actually Proves

The current real evidence proves the Mac evidence path: MLX runtime plus powermetrics telemetry can produce strict-valid bundles and plausible energy summaries. It does not yet prove stable model rankings, cross-device comparisons, split economics, or active-parameter scaling.

The methodology is better than the average energy benchmark because it names measurement boundaries, keeps raw evidence, records idle baselines, and treats uncertainty as part of the artifact. The next step is to make several caveats mechanically binding.

How I'd Improve The Rigor

The project does not need a different soul. It needs a stricter conversion layer between promising engineering artifacts and publishable claims. I would improve rigor by making every result pass through explicit gates: can the instrument resolve the effect, can the campaign design separate the effect from drift, can the raw evidence be re-derived, and does the claim wording stay inside the measurement boundary?

Rigor principle: no result should become a headline because it is interesting. It becomes a headline only after it survives detection-floor, ordering, validation, boundary, and provenance checks.

1. Create A Claims Ladder Resolved since first pass

Right now the project distinguishes engineering progress from research evidence in prose, but the distinction should become a formal ladder that every report uses. This would prevent the strongest language from arriving too early.

Superseded - see Reassessment A binding claims ladder now exists for reader-facing claims from Slice 2M onward.

LevelAllowed ClaimRequired EvidenceForbidden Language
L0 Capability The harness can execute this path and preserve evidence. One complete bundle, strict validation, raw artifacts present. Faster, cheaper, more efficient, scales, crossover.
L1 Instrument Result On this exact stack and workload, this measured quantity was observed. n >= 3, strict-valid bundles, no suspect quality flags unwaived. General device ranking or model-family law.
L2 Comparative Result Condition A differs from condition B within the same measurement boundary. n >= 5, interleaved order, intervals reported, effect exceeds detection floor. Cross-boundary comparisons unless calibrated.
L3 Model Fit A fitted fixed/marginal model predicts held-out cells within stated error. Designed matrix, holdout cells, residual analysis, sensitivity audit. Law, universal, architecture-wide conclusion.
L4 Generalized Finding The finding holds across stacks, units, or boundaries under stated limits. Second target or second unit, independent boundary check, replicated runbook. Unqualified claims outside tested hardware and workloads.

2. Measure The Instrument Before Measuring Models

The most important missing experiment is a measurement-floor study. Before the 2M corpus is promoted into a baseline dataset, run a small calibration campaign whose only purpose is to characterize what the instrument can and cannot resolve.

Detection-Floor Campaign

  • Run idle-only windows at the same sampling rate and duration as real runs.
  • Run no-op or tiny synthetic workloads that should be near the floor.
  • Run a duration ladder: 50 ms, 100 ms, 250 ms, 500 ms, 1 s, 5 s, 30 s measured windows.
  • Report the smallest energy delta resolvable with acceptable false-positive risk.
  • Store this as a versioned calibration bundle set, not as a paragraph in a report.

Mechanical Outputs

  • detection_floor_j per target and telemetry backend.
  • minimum_identifiable_window_s per sampling regime.
  • phase_identifiable flags for prefill/decode/transfer windows.
  • quality_gate_status: pass, waived, or fail.
  • A calibration manifest hash referenced by every later report.

3. Make Campaigns Fail Closed Resolved since first pass

The campaign runner should stop acting like an executor and become a research gatekeeper. A campaign should not call a run usable merely because a command exited successfully or a summary file exists.

Superseded - see Reassessment The runner path now has strict per-member validation, typed campaign waivers, quality flags, and publishable/partial/blocked/invalid verdicts.

Campaign MomentCurrent RiskRigor Upgrade
Before running Machine quietness is mostly a warning. Record a preflight snapshot and refuse if disallowed conditions are present, or require a waiver field.
After each run Exit code can be treated as enough. Immediately run strict validation and record the validation result in the campaign log.
On resume Existing failed or malformed members can be skipped as complete enough. Skip only if every expected member is strict-valid, succeeded, and quality-pass or waiver-approved.
At campaign end The operator must infer whether evidence is publishable. Write a campaign verdict: publishable, partial, blocked, or invalid, with reasons and bundle IDs.

4. Deconfound Ordering Resolved since first pass

The baseline matrix should not be sorted and run as a neat block if the goal is comparative evidence. Sorted order is convenient for humans and dangerous for thermals. I would change the generator and runner to make execution order an explicit experimental design choice.

Superseded - see Reassessment The 2M generator now emits an order manifest with workload rotation and model-block counterbalancing.

  • Interleave by repetition: run profile A rep 1, profile B rep 1, profile C rep 1, then loop, instead of A1-A5 then B1-B5.
  • Use ABBA for two-condition comparisons: A, B, B, A reduces monotonic drift bias.
  • Seed randomization: allow a deterministic random order recorded in the manifest, so reviewers can reproduce the order exactly.
  • Separate model-load cost from inference cost: where reordering forces reloads, record that as either out-of-window overhead or a separate cold-start experiment.
  • Require order plots: every aggregate should include metric vs run index so thermal or background drift is visible before conclusions are written.

5. Tighten Claim Boundaries

Measurement boundaries should become part of every result label. The project already documents boundaries well; the rigor upgrade is to make boundary mistakes hard to write.

Label Every Chart

Use labels such as M3 Max / MLX / powermetrics SoC rails or RTX / vLLM / nvidia-smi board power. The boundary belongs in titles, captions, CSV outputs, and claim tables.

Separate Three Claim Types

Within-boundary comparisons can be primary. Cross-boundary comparisons are descriptive unless calibrated. Wall-power or USB-C PD calibrated comparisons can become stronger, but only with the calibration bundle named.

6. Upgrade Provenance From Present To Sufficient

The project already records impressive provenance. The next question is whether the provenance is sufficient for the exact metric being claimed. Energy per token requires stronger token provenance than energy per request. Split economics requires stronger stage-window provenance than monolithic request energy.

MetricMinimum ProvenancePublishable Gate
Energy per request Strict-valid power trace, measured window, idle baseline, boundary label. Passes strict validation and quality gates.
Energy per output token Runtime-observed emitted token count, stop reason, fixed output policy or natural-EOS policy label. No approximate token fallback unless the claim is downgraded.
Prefill/decode energy Phase windows with enough samples, or explicit unresolved flag. Phase window exceeds detection floor and minimum sample count.
Split transfer energy Both-end sampling, payload hash/size, link speed, clock offset bound. Cross-node intervals longer than offset bound and independently re-derived.

7. Build A Claims-To-Evidence Index Early

Phase 4 plans a claims index, but I would start it now. Every report line that sounds like a result should get an ID, claim level, evidence bundle list, scripts used, quality flags, and allowed wording. This turns the final report from an interpretive essay into a defensible chain of custody.

Example downgrade: "Energy per token scales almost exactly with active parameters" should become "In two strict-valid Mac/MLX/powermetrics points, energy per output token was consistent with active-parameter-driven scaling; this is a hypothesis pending bridge models and a fitted Q4 matrix."

8. Sequence The Rigor Work Updated in second pass

  1. Before 2M: use the already-patched campaign fail-closed behavior and run the detection-floor calibration.
  2. During 2M: use the counterbalanced order manifest, strict-validate each member, record machine state, and produce a campaign verdict.
  3. After 2M: fit Q4 only inside one boundary, with raw points and interval separation rules.
  4. Before cross-target claims: de-provisionalize NVIDIA/Orin and calibrate at least one external power boundary.
  5. Before final report: freeze the dataset, generate a claim index, and rewrite every claim to the highest level its evidence actually supports.

Stack Choices

The stack is conservative in a way that mostly serves the project: Python, stdlib core, JSON/JSONL/CSV bundles, optional matplotlib, MLX on Mac, powermetrics for Apple rails, SSH node worker for remote NVIDIA, and vLLM/nvidia-smi as fixture-first backends.

Good Choices

  • Stdlib core keeps mock runs and CI simple.
  • JSON/JSONL/CSV are inspectable and easy to archive.
  • Optional extras keep analysis and Mac runtime dependencies out of the core path.
  • Adapter protocols match the natural measurement layers: runtime, telemetry, transport.
  • Static report generation is enough for a research bundle browser.

Costs Of Those Choices

  • Manual dataclass validation increases contract drift risk.
  • No console script entry point makes installed CLI ergonomics weaker.
  • The self-contained remote worker duplicates constants and policy.
  • Large adapter modules make measurement bugs harder to isolate.
  • CSV/JSON are readable, but schema enforcement must remain relentless.

Recommended Next Actions Updated in second pass

  1. Run the detection-floor study first. Use idle-only repeats, no-op/small workloads, and phase-window duration ladders. Treat the output as a calibration corpus, not a prose caveat.
  2. Run 2M under the new campaign gates. Use strict validation after each run and before every skip; require usable members or explicit typed waivers.
  3. Use the counterbalanced baseline order. Preserve the order manifest and audit metric-vs-run-index drift before writing comparisons.
  4. Fit Q4 narrowly first. One stack, one model family where possible, prompt/output ladders, n >= 5, clear uncertainty intervals.
  5. Demote active-parameter scaling language. Treat the 1.5B vs 122B comparison as hypothesis-generating until bridge models exist.
  6. De-provisionalize NVIDIA only with live artifacts. Require SSH, vLLM, nvidia-smi, raw CSV, strict validation, and quality flags before cross-target charts.
  7. Refactor for contract enforcement. Extract controller responsibilities, introduce a remote node session, and add mechanical worker protocol validators.
  8. Calibrate a second boundary. Wall meter or USB-C PD measurements would greatly improve credibility of Apple rail results and cross-target comparisons.
