The questions · steelmanned, devil's-advocated, hardware-tiered

Six named questions,
and a bank of thirty-one.

The research agenda was built by review council: each question steelmanned, attacked, and tiered by what hardware it needs — 16 of the 31 banked questions are answerable on the current machine alone (docs/research_question_bank.md).

Named questions

The split-inference core (Q1–Q3)…

Q1 · the crossover

Under what conditions — model size, prompt length, link speed, device pair — does splitting inference reduce total energy versus running whole on either device?

Q2 · the interconnect

How sensitive is the split's energy cost to bandwidth (1 GbE → 2.5 GbE, with 10 GbE optional)? Where exactly is the crossover?

Q3 · the trade

When splitting saves energy, what does it cost in latency — and vice versa? The energy–latency Pareto frontier.

…and the characterization spine (Q4–Q6).

Q4 · the energy model

What fixed-plus-marginal model E = fixed + prefill(p) + decode(d) does each target/model/quantization follow — and can split energy be predicted compositionally from monolithic coefficients plus transfer measurements?

Q5 · ranking stability

Do efficiency rankings across workloads, models, and quantizations stay stable on one machine — or where do they flip? (Cross-device ranking is a hardware-gated extension.)

Q6 · the boundary

How sensitive are conclusions to the measurement boundary — package power vs wall power? (Gates on the wall-meter decision.)

Method discipline

Claims are gated, not asserted.

The detection floor

Before any effect is claimed, the instrument's own noise floor is measured per target and metric. Effects below the floor are reported as "not resolvable" — a distinct verdict from "no difference." Null results above the floor are real findings; silence below it is honesty.

Correctness quarantine

Scored workloads record per-item correctness as annotation only — the project measures energy per token under controlled shape, and refuses the tempting "intelligence-per-joule" claim its own data cannot defend.

Capability first, claims only when the instrument can defend them — every measured number on this site re-derives from committed raw evidence, and every process count traces to a committed record.

2M — the two-model baseline campaign

4 workload shapes × 2 models × 5 repetitions, run unattended on a quiet machine with the contamination gate armed. The corpus is born under the fixed validator with full prompt provenance.
