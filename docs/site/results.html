Interpretation

What can—and cannot—be said today.

Supported

The harness has made real, descriptive, gross-basis and labeled idle-secondary observations on the named Mac and telemetry boundary.

Calibration only

The verified floor values describe the observed campaign conditions; they do not by themselves establish a model or configuration difference.

Not supported

No cross-device ranking, broad content-neutrality result, or split-inference energy win is claimed from these measurements.
