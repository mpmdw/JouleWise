The design discussion (held over the bridge being designed)

Three rounds, Fable lead ↔ Sol (gpt-5.6-sol, high effort), project MCP server, thread 019f5a67-00f5-7cb1-9c9a-d0cd4916ee82:

  • Round 1 — Sol answered six pain points with a field-complete prompt contract, a sentinel envelope it demonstrated live in its own reply, thread continue-vs-fresh rules, and four gaps the lead had missed entirely (cancellation semantics, concurrent workspace ownership, protocol version negotiation, thread closure/GC).
  • Round 2 — lead adjudications; Sol out-designed the lead in three places, all accepted: hard-block atomic leases (lead had proposed warn-only; Sol: "warn-only would reproduce yesterday's failure while creating the appearance of protection"), path-level baseline manifests (lead had proposed a status-digest; Sol showed four ways it is not attribution-sound), and separate lease/thread event logs joined by invocation_id. Sol also named the design's highest-risk failure mode: false confidence in scope attribution over a moving shared tree — and the audit-batch mitigation (pinned commit, isolated worktrees, snapshot_read leases, no writes until adjudication).
  • Round 3 — Sol drafted the full contract in-band with five marked draft-choices; the lead adjudicated all five (JSON header sentinel, {path, match} scope entries, flock locking, warn-don't-auto-abandon expiry, bridge-internal path exemption).

Lead dissents recorded: caller origin-markers KEPT on the reverse path (contract-compliance lint, not security); AGENTS.md deliberately restates Sol-side obligations (always-read enforcement surface) against the lens's one-fact-one-home nit.
